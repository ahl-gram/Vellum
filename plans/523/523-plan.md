## What ships

Three things on a table that already works by click and tap, plus the checks that prove the flourish never steals the map's gestures:

1. **The desktop drag.** A mouse press-and-move on the dog-ear picks the committed survey up: a ghost (the survey's own thumbnail as a blob `<img>`) rides the pointer, hanging from the corner the reader took it by; released over the drawer's band it files, exactly as the click does; released anywhere else it snaps back to the dog-ear and nothing has happened. A press-and-release without movement is the click, untouched. Touch and pen never drag (Issue #401 ruling 6: filing is click or tap everywhere; the drag is a desktop flourish and never the only door).
2. **The settle.** A sheet laid on the table lands with a short settle on its cutting: a slight rotate and a shadow that flattens, `motion.css`'s idiom (a class the host applies, a keyframe on `var(--paper-settle)` and `var(--ease-paper)`, the class retiring on `animationend`), collapsed to an instant place under `prefers-reduced-motion` by the shared blanket. Which doors play it (a lay from any door, a restore too, or the drag alone) is decision D5, Alex's; the plan's recommendation is a lay from any door and never a restore, because a reload or a shared link is a photograph and not a ceremony.
3. **The refusal at the cap.** The period line the table already speaks, plus a small jolt of the sheets on the table, reduced-motion safe by the same blanket. The "already on the table" refusal keeps its line and does not jolt (the issue names the cap; recorded as a call below).

Nothing moves a chart, a golden, a seed, the address grammar or the store. `specs/rulebook.md` regen posture: none, and the evidence is `npm test` green with `test/site/hero-charts.test.ts` in it.

## Design

### The drag: `src/site/explorer/table-drag.ts` (new, ~120 lines)

Pure half, DOM-free, unit-tested:

- `grabbable(p: { pointerType, button, isPrimary })`: mouse, primary button, primary pointer. Touch and pen return false, so a phone never sees a ghost.
- `beganDrag(from, to)`: the pointer has travelled at least `DRAG_SLOP_PX` (6px) from the press. Under it the gesture is the click.
- `dropOutcome(at, band)`: `"file"` when the release point is inside the drawer's band (at or below its top edge, and the band exists), else `"snap"`. The band is the drawer's LAYOUT seat and never its painted rect (skeptic 5): `innerHeight - var(--chart-drawer-h)` resolved once through the root's `offsetHeight` while open, or the same height read off the shut drawer's computed style, because during the 0.32s slide `getBoundingClientRect` follows `translateY` and a release inside the slide would read the band lower than it will be, and a shut drawer's rect is all zeros, which would file on EVERY release. A band whose top is at or below `innerHeight` is `null`, not a band at 0 (TD7). Where no drawer can show (the 900px stand-down, read through the same `(max-width: 900px)` query `bindTableLeaf` holds, never from a zero rect) `canDrag()` is false and a press never begins a drag at all: the click path is all there is at narrow, with or without a mouse.
- `ghostSeat(at, width)`: where the ghost's box sits for a pointer at `at`: its top-right corner under the pointer, inset `GRIP_INSET_PX`, because the reader took the sheet by its turned corner.

DOM half, `bindTableDrag(deps)`, bound on every dog-ear `makeDogEar` builds (the ear is rebuilt with every inset commit, so the binding is per ear and holds no state past it):

- `pointerdown` on the handle: if `grabbable`, record the start and add `pointermove`, `pointerup`, `pointercancel` on the DOCUMENT and `blur` on the window (the issue's rule: a drag that leaves the window must not strand the ghost). No `setPointerCapture`: capture retargets the click (`specs/explorer-doctrine.md`, the home stage's scar) and the document listeners need no capture.
- first `pointermove` past the slop: the drag begins. Mint ONE blob url from the committed svg (`deps.ghostUrl()`), build the ghost (`<img class="sheet-ghost">`, `position: fixed`, `pointer-events: none`, seated with the individual `translate` and `rotate` properties per `specs/ui-design.md`), append it to `document.body` and never inside `#map` (a fixed element inside the transformed mount re-anchors to it, `specs/cascade-traps.md`), and put `sheet-drag` on `body` (`user-select: none; cursor: grabbing`). Whether and when the drawer opens during the carry is decision D4; the module calls `deps.reveal()` at the moment D4 rules (the grab, or the first move into the band, or never) and keeps the function it returns to put the drawer back.
- every `pointermove` while dragging: re-seat the ghost, and mark the drawer `receiving` while the pointer is over its band.
- `pointerup`: remove the document listeners. If no drag began, return and let the click fire. Else set the `dragged` flag that a capture-phase `click` listener on the handle consumes (`stopImmediatePropagation`), so a release on the handle after a jiggle does not file twice; the flag is scoped to ONE gesture, cleared at every `pointerdown`, because a release off the handle fires its click on the common ancestor and never reaches the handle's listener, and a flag left standing would eat the reader's next honest click (skeptic 4; CD45 drives that click). Then `dropOutcome`: `"file"` hands the url to `deps.file(url)`, which is `chartTable.lay(item, svg, title, { url })` adopting the ghost's url as the cutting's thumbnail (no second url per drag, the issue's rule), and removes the ghost; a refused lay (the cap, or already laid) snaps the ghost back and leaves the drawer where `lay` put it (open, the ruled click behaviour). `"snap"` snaps the ghost back and restores the drawer through the function `reveal` returned. Url ownership is one line (skeptic 11): the drag module revokes its url on every path except a `lay` that returned true, which adopted it.
- `pointercancel`, `blur`, and Escape while dragging: snap back, restore, revoke.
- The snap-back is a WAAPI animation on the ghost (`translate` to the ear's rect, `scale` down, `opacity` to 0) over `--paper-settle`, removed on `finished` (and on rejection). It is WAAPI and not a CSS class because the shared reduced-motion blanket cannot reach a Web Animation, so the module reads `prefersReduce()` live (`specs/ui-design.md`: read the preference live) and plays a zero-duration snap under it.

Where it is wired: `app.ts`'s `decorateInset` already builds the ear with `makeDogEar(label, k, onLay)`; it gains one call, `bindTableDrag({ handle: ear, ghostUrl: () => URL.createObjectURL(blob of committed.svg), table: () => drawer band or null, reveal: chartTable.reveal, file: (url) => chartTable.lay(item, committed.svg, committed.title, { url }), receiving: chartTable.receiving, prefersReduce })`. `app.ts` stays under its 400-line pin (339 today; this adds about 12).

### The settle and the jolt: `src/site/explorer/chart-drawer.ts` and `public/explorer/chart-drawer.css`

- `bindChartDrawer` gains `let landing: string | null`. `lay()` sets it to the laid item's key before `commit()`; `render()` puts `landing` on that one `<li>`, wires `animationend` (target the li itself, so the img's shadow keyframe ending does not retire it early) to remove the class, and clears the mark, so it is consumed exactly once: a later render rebuilds every li without it (the deterministic half of `specs/ui-design.md`'s one-shot rule, since `render` IS the host's draw and `replaceChildren` is what it does).
- **A ceremony plays only where the sheets are on screen when it is due; off screen it is dropped, never queued** (skeptic 9). After `setOpen(true)` in `lay()`, if the cuttings list has no client rects (the phone, where the drawer is stood down and the list is docked in a leaf that is not up), the `landing` class comes off the marked li and no settle plays later when the leaf is next shown; the pill and the "The Table · N" tally are the phone's whole answer, which CD33 already pins. The desktop's click from a shut drawer is the case that makes the order matter: the li is marked while the drawer is `display: none`, `setOpen(true)` displays it in the same tick, and the display change is what starts the animation, so the mark is applied before the open and checked after it.
- `lay()` at a `"full"` refusal, after `setOpen(true)`: `jolt()` puts `jolt` on the cuttings list only if the list has client rects (the same rule), removes it on `animationend`, and `render()` removes it at its top as the deterministic half.
- **A thumbnail arriving in `fill()` patches its cutting in place instead of rebuilding the list** (skeptic 8): the li carries its key in `data-key`, and `patchArt(item)` swaps the reserved frame for the img and relabels, falling back to `render()` only when no li carries that key. So a settle in flight survives a plate landing beside it, and the place card's `lay(item, null)` settles as a reserved frame and then receives its plate without a rebuild. Recovered-sheet fills (CD4) read the same patched result: one img, no frame, the drawn title.
- `lay(item, svg, title, art?: { url })`: adopts `art.url` when given instead of minting one from `svg`. Existing callers pass nothing and are unchanged.
- `reveal(): () => void`: opens the drawer if shut (through `setOpen`, which folds the Broadside per Issue #543) and returns a function that shuts it again only if it was shut; `receiving(over: boolean)` toggles the class on the root.
- CSS in `public/explorer/chart-drawer.css` (the table's own sheet; no new sheet, so no roster joins):
  - `@keyframes cutting-land` on the li, written on `transform` and NOT on the individual properties, ending at `transform: none`, with `both` fill: the seat is `rotate: var(--tilt)` and the ruled hover lift (Issue #520, 2026-09-08 ruling 2) is `translate`/`rotate`, and the resolved transform is `translate`, then `rotate`, then `scale`, then `transform`, so a keyframe on `transform` composes over the seat and the lift at every frame, and a `both` fill pinning `transform: none` pins the identity, which kills nothing (the animation-cascade trap in `specs/ui-design.md` bites a keyframe on the individual properties, which would pin the seat at `to` and kill the lift; recon blocking finding 5). `@keyframes cutting-shadow` on the img, its `box-shadow` from lifted to resting. `.cuttings li.landing { animation: cutting-land var(--paper-settle) var(--ease-paper) both }` and `.cuttings li.landing img { animation: cutting-shadow var(--paper-settle) var(--ease-paper) both }`. The `rotate(` lives INSIDE the keyframe blocks and the class rule carries none, so `hoverTipsIn` in `test/site/tip-affordance.test.ts` (which keys on `:hover` in the selector AND `rotate(` in the block) sees no tip; the keyframe selectors `from`/`to` carry no `:hover`. Verified against the sweep's own regex before writing: a node one-liner over a fixture with the keyframe passed, and the same fixture plus a `:hover` rule carrying `rotate(` redded.
  - `@keyframes cutting-jolt` on `.cuttings.jolt`, NEVER on `.chart-drawer` itself: that element carries `chart-drawer-up` in its `animation` shorthand, and a second animation shorthand on it would replace and then restart the slide when the class left (the animation-cascade trap in `specs/ui-design.md`).
  - The cutting's resting shadow becomes a token, `--cutting-shadow`, declared in this sheet's `:root` and used by `.cuttings img` and by `cutting-shadow`'s `to` step alike, so the `both` fill's pinned rest and the static rest cannot drift apart (skeptic 16).
  - `.sheet-ghost` (fixed, top/left 0, seated by `translate`/`rotate`, `pointer-events: none`, `z-index: 30`, above the Broadside slip's 20 as well as the drawer's 18 and the tab's 19 (skeptic 14), the cutting's width, the cutting's img dress with a raised shadow), `body.sheet-drag { user-select: none }`, `body.sheet-drag :is(#map-viewport, *) { cursor: grabbing }` (the `:is()` takes the id's specificity, so it beats `#map-viewport { cursor: grab }` without `!important`), `.chart-drawer.receiving` (a brightened top hairline, the drop cue).
  - No `:hover` rule is added anywhere (Issue #520's ruling of 2026-09-07 already put the cuttings' lift on Issue #514's docket as the kit's third; this sub adds no fourth).
  - Under `@media (max-width: 900px)` nothing changes: the drag never begins where the band is null, and the settle on the leaf's cuttings is the same class.
