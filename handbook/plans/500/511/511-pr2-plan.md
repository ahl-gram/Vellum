## The design

**One rule seats every caption, in both dresses.** Each event's caption is seated by one pure function, `seatCaption` in a new `src/itinerary/dress/seats.ts`, called once per event in the order today's code captions them (`input.events` order, strip by strip, events with `strip.d0 <= dist < strip.d1`). It tries candidate seats in a fixed order and takes the first whose boxes pass two tests: every box lies inside its strip's **room**, and no box meets anything the strip **keeps** clear. A caption's boxes are the house estimate, one per line, exactly as `lineBoxes` below computes them (`textBox` with `WIDTH_FACTOR` from `src/render/geometry.ts`; a caps line with its 0.8 letter-spacing). The layout, the events, the fork keys and the caption wording do not change, so the slip's rows, RB7's same-address press and `test/itinerary/fork-keys.test.ts` stand. Nothing in the rule draws from the rng.

**The room**, per strip and dress:

- Sideways, at every height a box spans, the box's left edge at or right of `edgeLeft(y) + CAPTION_EDGE` and its right edge at or left of `edgeRight(y) - CAPTION_EDGE`, with `CAPTION_EDGE = 9`. On antique the edges are the cloth's (`stripBody(strip).left(y)` and `.right(y)` in `src/itinerary/dress/cloth.ts`), so the room is 3 inside the band's inner hairline; on ink they are the outer rule (`strip.x0` and `strip.x0 + strip.w`), 6 inside the inner rule. The heights checked are the box's top, every whole unit after it, and its bottom.
- Up and down, the box's top at or below `strip.y0 + 22` (today's clamp, under the continuation line) and its bottom at or above the foot: `strip.y0 + strip.h` on antique (the cloth's straight end), `strip.y0 + strip.h - 3.5` on ink (above the inner rule and its stroke).
- Every comparison allows `ROOM_EPSILON = 1e-6`, since a box slid to the edge lands on it to the last bit.

The 9 is derived, not chosen: real letters in the three faces of the style's stack, measured in a browser over the 469 distinct caption lines of the eight-seed sweep, run at most 8.47 past the house estimate (Palatino caps, NUMOWOPUA at 10.5; Iowan Old Style 7.65; Georgia italic 2.25), so a box 9 inside the edge keeps the drawn letters on the strip in every face, with 0.53 to spare (`out/511-pr2-dry-run/511-pr2-widths-all.log`, `511-pr2-widths-overrun.log`, `511-pr2-widths.ts`).

**The keep**, per strip, built once before the strip's captions are seated (`captionKeep`), in three kinds:

- *boxes* (a caption's box may not overlap one: strict inequality on both axes):
  - each waypoint's houses, around `(p.sx, p.sy - 1)`, by tier, x from-to and y from-to: capital -11.5 to 11.5 and -11.6 to 3.2; town -9 to 9.7 and -9.6 to 1.5; village -6.7 to 6.6 and -5.4 to 0.5; hamlet, farmstead and mill -2.6 to 2.6 and -5.1 to 0 (the extents of `settlementCluster` and `paintedSettlementCluster`), for every waypoint with `strip.d0 <= dist < strip.d1`;
  - each summit's mountain: x `p.sx + freeSide * 27 - 9.5`, width 19; y `p.sy + 4 - 12.6`, height 12.6;
  - each crossing's bridge or ford mark: x `p.sx - 7.5`, width 15; y `p.sy - 5.5`, height 11;
  - on ink, each fifth league's numeral: `textBox(p.sx + 5.5, p.sy + 2.4, String(l), 7, "start", WIDTH_FACTOR.mixed)`;
  - the continuation line, on every strip but the last: `textBox(strip.x0 + strip.w / 2, strip.y0 + 12, "· N leagues ·", 7.5, "middle", WIDTH_FACTOR.mixed)`, `N` as `continuation` computes it;
  - every line box of every caption already seated in the strip.
- *discs* (a box may not come closer to the centre than the radius: the box's nearest point strictly inside the circle):
  - the compass rose at `(compassX, compassY)` (`stripNodes`' seat): radius 15.5 on antique (the disc is r 15 with a 0.9 stroke), 14 on ink (r 13.4, stroke 0.9);
  - on antique the fleur, radius 5, at `(compassX + 20.5 sin t, compassY - 20.5 cos t)`; on ink the N, radius 4.5, at `(compassX + 17 sin t, compassY - 17 cos t)`; `t` the strip's `needleDeg` in radians;
  - every league's mark at `stripPos(strip, l * CELLS_PER_LEAGUE)` for every league the strip numbers (`leagueMarks`' own range): on antique radius 4.1 (the halo's 3.6 plus 0.5), 5.6 from league 100 on (the ellipse's rx 5.1 plus 0.5); on ink radius 1.9 (the dot's 1.4 plus 0.5).
- *lines* (a box beside the road keeps at least `clear` from every segment; a backed box, below, is exempt from all lines; the distance from a segment to a box is the least distance from the box to points taken along the segment at `n + 1` evenly spaced places, `n = max(1, ceil(length / 0.5))`, both ends included; a segment whose y-range lies wholly more than `clear` above or below the box is skipped):
  - the road's centre line, `strip.pts`, with `clear` the casing's half-width plus 0.55 (the casing dot's half stroke): 4.75 on antique, 2.85 on ink;
  - each waypoint's two solid town spans, exactly as `roadNodes` draws them (the same `solidSpan`, the same `d0 - 0.75` to `d1 + 0.75` range and `len >= 2` test, offset by the casing's half-width either way), each a two-point line with `clear` 0.6 (the span's half stroke);
  - each side road's stub, `(p.sx, p.sy)`, `(p.sx + side * 12, p.sy - 2)`, `(p.sx + side * 20, p.sy - 9)`, `clear` 1.5.

A river band is not kept clear (a crossing's caption reads over its own river, as today), nor a sea wash (ink on either measured 6.5:1 and up), nor a realm name (it lies outside the room).

**Today's seat, the want.** `captionSeat` (unchanged) gives a caption its side, its point and its lines: a waypoint and a crossing the free side, a side road its own side, a summit the side away from its mountain. Its anchor x is today's, `seat.sx - 10` on the left or `seat.sx + 10` on the right (so 30 from the road for a side road), its anchor `end` on the left and `start` on the right, its lines wrapped at 17 characters as today, and its top today's: `max(min(seat.sy + 2.6, track - need), strip.y0 + 22)`, `need` the lines' summed `size + 1.2` and `track` that side's running track. After a caption is seated beside the road, the running track of the side it was seated on (the left for `end`, the right for `start`) becomes its top minus 4; a caption set across the road, or forced, leaves both tracks as they were.

**The candidates.** Each beside candidate is a (side, slide) pair at a size and a wrapping: its top is today's top plus the slide; its anchor x is today's on today's side, and on the other side the road's extreme x toward that side over the box's height (`roadX` at the top and every 2 units down to the top plus the lines' summed `size + 1.2` at full size) plus `side * 10`; it is tried as it stands and then **slid inward** (the anchor moved toward the road just far enough that every box meets the room's sideways test: by the right edge for `start`, the left edge for `end`, either for `middle`). `roadX(y)` is the `sx` of the `strip.pts` point whose `sy` is nearest `y`, the first on a tie. Slides, in order, `SLIDES = [0, -4, 4, -8, 8, -12, 12, -16, 16, -20, 20, -24, 24, -28, 28, -32, 32]` (negative is up). The **near passes**: for a side road, today's side through every slide and then the other side through every slide; for every other kind, today's side through the slides of 12 or less and then the other side through the same. The **far passes**: none for a side road; for every other kind, the slides past 12 in order, each on today's side and then the other. An **across** candidate is centred (`text-anchor="middle"`) on `roadX(top + 5)`, its top `p.sy + 2.6` for a waypoint and `p.sy + 6` for anything else, plus a slide from `ACROSS_SLIDES = [0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, -4, -8, -12, -16, -20, -24]`, tried as it stands and then slid inward; it is backed.

The stages, in order; the first candidate that passes wins:

1. Beside, full size, wrapped at 17, near passes.
2. For a crossing or a summit only (a description, never a name): beside, full size, rewrapped at 12 and then at 9 (`WRAPS = [12, 9]`), each wrapping tried only where it gives more lines than the one before it; near passes.
3. Across, full size, wrapped at 17, then each rewrapping stage 2 used.
4. Beside, full size, far passes, wrapped at 17 and then each rewrapping stage 2 used.
5. For each size of `SHRINKS = [0.9, 0.8, 0.7]` in turn: beside at that size, wrapped at 17, near passes; then across at that size, wrapped at 17.
6. For each size of `SHRINKS` in turn: beside at that size, far passes.
7. **Forced.** Of the beside candidates at full size wrapped at 17, on today's side through every slide and then the other side through every slide, each slid inward, those whose boxes pass the room's test, the one whose boxes overlap the keep's boxes and discs (a disc taken as its bounding square) by the least summed area, the first on a tie; where none passes the room's test, today's seat at the size that fits the room's narrowest span between `want.top` and `want.top + 12` (no larger than full), slid inward. A forced caption is backed.

**The backing.** A caption set across the road, or forced, draws each line with `stroke` the dress's ground (`c.paint.landWash` on antique, `c.style.paper` on ink), `stroke-width` 2.4, `stroke-linejoin` round and `paint-order` stroke, so the road's dots and lines do not strike its letters. A caption seated beside the road has none.

**Decoration gives way.** A tree, hill, mountain, palm, dune, marsh tuft or ink wave that `decorNodes` draws is left out when its box, x from `x - 9s` to `x + 9s` and y from `y - 12s` to `y + 1` (`x` taken 6 to the left for an ink wave, `s` its scale `p.s`), overlaps any seated caption's line box widened by 1 on every side. Its nodes are still made, so its random draws are made in the same order and every other glyph lands where it does today. This is chart-dress's rule for a close-up, "a terrain mark gives way to a place or realm name", brought to the Ribbon.

**The layer and the handle.** Every caption stays in the one layer above every strip (pull request 1's Call 2), each a `<g class="caption">` holding its line `<text>`s: the handle the tests read, as `class="road"` is the frame guard's (ruling 3, comment 5533954362).

**The ink dress takes the same rule** (Q2, ruled, comment 6602622082), with its own room and keep above: "to Brin" struck through by the plate frame's rules and a summit's words running off the plate are the ink plate's defects too, and a style is a palette over identical geometry. Its frame, dots, fives, compass and title are untouched (Q1 of 2026-11-02 stands for everything but the caption seats). The ruled consequence: on ink the room's foot is the inner rule, so a departure's caps name, which today rests on that rule, is set at 0.8 (8.4) on 48 of the 75 journeys of the sweep (every journey from seed 99's and seed 42's capitals; `511-pr2-how-75.log`).
