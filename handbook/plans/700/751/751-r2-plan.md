## The design

**The ruled defect has two halves, and both are fixed.** A town's or a hamlet's ring and click area must sit on its dot at every depth, which means the hit must be placed where its rect says, and the dot must be painted where its rect says.

1. **Every place hit is placed by its own transform** (the engine's sheet, every host). `.place-overlay` becomes a size container; `.place-hit` stands at `left: 0; top: 0` and its transform becomes `translate(calc(var(--nx, 0) * 100cqw), calc(var(--ny, 0) * 100cqh)) translate(-50%, -50%) scale(calc(1 / var(--zoom-k, 1)))`; `placeHit` writes the place's fraction as `--nx` and `--ny` instead of `left` and `top`. The hit's offset from the overlay is then 0, which the browser has nothing to round, and the fraction rides inside the transform exactly.
2. **A region inset's place overlay fills the mount, and the box is folded into each fraction** (`buildPlaceOverlay`). Today a region overlay is placed at the inset's percentage box, whose fractional offset the hits inside it would inherit and the browser would round again. Instead every overlay fills the mount, each place's anchor is `box.x + place.nx * box.w` (and the same for y), and the card is anchored at that same anchor (`--pc-nx`, `--pc-ny`). The card's flip side is still chosen from the place's own fraction (`cardSide(place.nx, place.ny)`), so no card flips differently from today.
3. **A region inset's svg and dog-ear are placed by their own transforms, in the mount's units, and the inset box is left as it is** (the Explorer's sheets). `#map` becomes a size container. The inset keeps its inline percentage `left`, `top`, `width` and `height` (so every reader of them is unchanged) and also publishes `--ix`, `--iy` and `--iw` (`placeInset` in `src/site/explorer/lod-controller.ts`). Its svg is laid out back at the mount's origin (`left: calc(var(--ix) * -100cqw)`, `top: calc(var(--iy) * -100cqh)`, both cancelling the inset's own offset) and carried to the window by `transform: translate(calc(var(--ix) * 100cqw), calc(var(--iy) * 100cqh))`. The dog-ear goes the same way to the inset's top right, but through the individual `translate` and `scale` properties and never `transform`, which the house lift owns (`handbook/specs/ui-design.md`, "A piece seated by a transform is placed with the individual properties"); its counter-scale moves from its width and height into `scale`. The browser applies `translate`, then `scale`, then `transform`, so the house lift (-2 px) lands inside the counter-scale and lifts the ear the house's 2 px on screen at every depth, as it lifts every other press, where today it lifts 2 px times the depth. Whether the dog-ear should lift, or change its dress, under the hand at all is Issue #859's, open and Alex's from stills; this pull request keeps today's house hover and removes only its magnification, which made the ear unpressable at 24 times. The ear's dead `transition: width, height` goes. The svg markup is untouched (the rule is in the sheet, not an inline style on the svg).
4. **Paper.** In print `#map` stops being a size container, and the dog-ear is hidden like the drawer and its tab already are.
5. **(R3 (a), ruled) The card's unfold keeps every frame and only its resting state goes flat**: in `paperUnfurl`, today's last frame (`perspective(900px) rotateX(0)`) moves to 99.99% and a final frame `transform: none` follows it, so the motion is today's to within 0.004 px through its last sampled frame and the card at rest carries no 3D transform and is painted with the sheet. The last 0.01% lasts 0.04 ms of the 400 ms unfold (0.065 ms of the 650 ms pinned one), shorter than any frame. The reading frame's instrument and its journal (`.rf-instrument`, `.rf-log` in `src/styles/shared/reading-frame.css`) and the hunt's reveal (`src/styles/seed-of-the-day/index.css`) wear the same unfold and rest flat too, their motion likewise unchanged.

**Byte identity and the golden: nothing moves.** No renderer, engine or world file changes; the region svg string is inserted exactly as the worker drew it and positioned by a stylesheet rule; the world sheet, Download, the verso ghost and every saved sheet are the same bytes. No regen, no golden, no re-roll.

**What moves on screen, named as corrections.** The region sheet now lands exactly on its window: on main it stands off by up to half a pixel times the depth, about 4 px at 8 times, and the reader will see the region coast register more closely with the world coast under it. The dog-ear keeps 54.4 px at every depth, lifts 2 px on hover instead of 16 px at 8 times (and 48 at 24), and can be pressed at 24 times held (the lift itself stays Issue #859's). A region card's anchor is the same sum of the same fractions as before, now composed in one length rather than two; its rounded paint can differ from today's only where the two ways of summing straddle a half pixel (a 1/64 px difference), so it does not move in practice, and R2's issue baselines the card on this build. The rings and click areas sit on their dots. Under R3 (a) the card's text is as sharp as the page's.

**Rejected, with the reason:**

- *The region overlay placed at the box by a translate* (instead of design item 2). A transform makes the overlay a stacking context, which would sink the card (z-index 5 inside it) under the dog-ear (z-index 3 in the mount), where today the card stands over it.
- *The svg and the dog-ear placed in the inset's own units* (percentages of the inset, or the inset as a size container) instead of the mount's. Measured: the cancelling offset is then a ratio of two rounded lengths, and the error grows with how far right or down the window sits; the dog-ear painted 2 px off at 24 times in a mid-sheet window (arm `VE`), and, derived from one 1/64 px rounding of each length times the ratio of the window's offset to its size (up to 7 for a band-3 window at the sheet's far edge), the error reaches about 3.9 px there, past the guard's tolerance at the sheet's far edge (mid-sheet it stays under it, so ZH11 would not pin this rejection at a typical pick). Mount units leave one such rounding unmultiplied by any ratio, under 0.4 px at 24 times (derived the same way; measured within 1.22 px, arm `M`).
- *Rounding the sheet to whole pixels.* The world sheet's dots already paint within 1.4 px of their rects (the svg is at the mount's origin), and a whole-pixel sheet cannot keep the chart's aspect, so it would shift the dots it was meant to fix.
- *Snapping each hit to the pixel the browser would round the svg to.* It would encode one browser's rounding rule in the engine and be wrong in any other.
- *A guard reading rects.* Measured: every rect agrees with every other rect while the paint is 10 to 15 px apart (finding 2).
- *The dog-ear seated by `transform`* (this plan's first version). The house lift writes `transform` on a hovered or pressed button at specificity (0,3,1) and replaces the seat; the plan skeptic measured the hovered ear leaving the screen and a press laying nothing.
- *The dog-ear joined to the house's lift exclusions* (`.place-hit` and `.lf-station` in `src/styles/shared/motion.css`). It would also work, but takes the house lift away from a press, an appearance change, where the individual properties keep the lift at the house's own 2 px; ui-design already rules the individual properties for exactly this case.

Design item 3's other option, the inset moved as one piece, is R1's (b) and (c).
