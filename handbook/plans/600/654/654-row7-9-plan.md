**K3, a five-deep block (test 10, scripts 2, engine 10).** The innermost level becomes a named predicate or helper, or a guard clause (`continue`) replaces an `if` around the loop body, whichever keeps the statements and their order; a helper returns what its caller used to accumulate in place (the counters in `test/terrain/detail-guarantees-world.test.ts:116-135` that feed its vacuity floors, for one; skeptic 6). `src/core/mask-components.ts`'s four and `src/core/segment-chains.ts`'s one are engine code under row 9's proof.

**K6, a site room builder (row 8: 28).** The builder's inner functions fall into cohesive parts (named in each slice plan after reading the builder).
