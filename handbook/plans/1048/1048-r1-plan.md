## The design

The rule keeps its one normative home, the two bullets PR #1058 adds to `handbook/specs/check-placement.md`'s section "The browser lanes and a plate read", between the bullet "What the browser computes is read in a browser" and the bullet "How a browser check waits". The heading is not renamed, since archived plans cite it by name.

- **The first bullet is the rule**: a number the room's layout decides (the press that fills, a fill depth, a clearance, a contrast over a ground, a travel), asserted or steered by, is read off the run's own layout, and the check asserts the relation the rule names. Its models are SW2's `thirdPress`, for pressing up to the press that fills whichever it is, and SW4's chart folio clause in `sw1Rooms`, for asking some room rather than one named in advance. Then the instances, as Alex ruled D1 and D2 (comment 6394974240): the window a check opens is its input, and whether the layout there reaches the case is a reading; a witness names an instance when it faults because that one room or window misses its case, and where the case is such a reading held against a line (a fill against a press's depth, a contrast against the floor) it carries one dated line beside the list of what it names, each instance's reading on both layouts and its margin to that line; `ARMS` owes that line, with no "at least one" shortcut (D1); the one exception is ruling 3's, a panel-away contrast proof over more than one room, which may ask at least one of them and carry no line, while one that reads a single room names it (D2). Last, the pointer the issue's step 2 asks for: the two readings a plan prints are its fixture's and its mutation's, off its own build on one machine, and neither is the second layout's reading.
- **The second bullet is the escape ruling**: what counts as ruled (may stay a literal, as a named constant with one dated line naming the ruling or token), what counts as measured (read from the layout, or where no read gives it, bounded from both layouts on the dated line `CLAUDE.md` gives a bound, the runner's reading taken on GitHub's machines), and what is neither (a check's own inputs, and a product constant the check writes again, except one a ruling sets as a size in pixels, which is ruled; one a ruling sets as a duration stays neither). A tolerance around a relation, a window held round a ruled clearance among them, is a bound.
- **No process step is added anywhere** (menu ruling 1). `.claude/agents/vellum-planner.md`, `handbook/specs/development-workflow.md` and `handbook/specs/cloud-routine.md` are not edited, and no issue for a lint is filed.

The checks on main that the rule sorts as measured, and the sites that owe a dated line, are not changed here (menu ruling 2): they are Issue #785's step 3, handed over in the new comment under "Records the builder posts", which corrects and replaces the first hand-over.
