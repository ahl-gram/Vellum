## What the issue asked, and what the measurement found

The issue asks which is true: a flaky check, or a real defect in which the Prospect's fitted sheet moves when the chart of the atelier's panel opens at 1024. **It is the check.** The sheet does not move when the panel opens. It moves once, when the plate lands, and on the CI runner AT1's rest wait can hand the page over before the plate has landed, so the rest read is of the room's boot fit and the open read is of the fit the plate's refit leaves.

**The cause.** `arrive` in `e2e/suites/cluster/chart.ts` waits on `RESTING`, whose `still` asks that `#status` and `.rf-status` be empty. The Prospect's status pill is `#pp-status` (`src/pages/prospect/index.astro`, `pillId="pp-status"`), which that list does not name, and nothing in the wait reads the plate. The Prospect draws its plate after `await initWorker()` (`src/site/prospect/app.ts` line 207), and when the plate lands the draw's `.then` shows it (`showPlate`, `src/site/prospect/seats.ts` line 74) and, in the same task, writes the folio's sub and pressed lines and shows the ribbon road (`writeRoads`, `writeFolio`), then refits the room (`sheet.room.layout()`, `app.ts` line 171). At 1024 that refit moves the sheet: the folio grows by two lines and the legend row, now holding the ribbon road too, stacks above it, so the floor `fitStage` fits under rises from 669.391 to 585.484 and the sheet jumps up and shrinks. At 1280 the legend row does not stack, its top (701.391) is the floor before and after, and the plate's refit leaves the sheet where it was.

## The design

AT1 keeps its claim, its sentence, its reads and its assertion. Only the rest wait every AT check arrives through changes: `RESTING` reads one field more, `drawn`, true unless the page holds a Prospect plate that is still hidden (`!document.querySelector("#pp-plate[hidden]")`), and `arrive`'s judge asks for it beside `still` and the two near sheet reads. On every page without the Prospect's plate `drawn` is always true and the wait is today's. On the Prospect the wait hands over only once the plate is shown, and since `showPlate` unhides it in the task that refits the room, every read that sees the plate sees the plate's fit; the two near reads after it are the plate's fit holding still.

Then the skip goes (`E2E_SKIPS` loses `{ suite: "cluster", step: "AT1", issue: 1100 }`), the three AT1 rows of the flake record read settled, and three unit tests hold the wait on a fake page, since the browser check cannot see the wait regress on a machine whose plate lands before the rest read (the Mac's, above).

**Alternatives rejected:**

- **A site fix that stops the panel moving the sheet.** The panel does not move it: with the plate in, the open read equals the rest read at every cell (the readings below). There is nothing to fix there.
- **Giving the Prospect its final seat before the plate lands**, so the room never refits at boot. That changes the product for a check's sake, and it is a separate question about how the room arrives (Open decisions, D1).
- **Adding `#pp-status` to the pill list.** The pill is empty until `draw` writes "The engraver is at the plate…", and `draw` runs only after `await initWorker()` (`app.ts` lines 207 to 209): the trace reads it empty at +19 ms and +54 ms and written from +66 ms on, so an empty pill is no witness of the draw, and on a slow runner the wait could hand over in that gap. The plate's showing is a positive witness.
- **Reading `window.__vellumProspectState()`.** It turns non-null in the same task as the plate's showing, so it would serve, but it is a test hook where the plate's `hidden` is the page's own visible state (call C1).
- **A tolerance on AT1's sheet comparison.** The move is 36.156 px (199.359375 to 163.203125), the size of the defect the claim exists to catch: clause 1, the defect is the statistic, not the threshold.
- **A wait in `at1Press` alone.** `arrive` is the "at rest" wait for every AT check, and a Prospect whose plate has not landed is not at rest; the wait costs AT2 and AT5 at most the rest of an engraving on their Prospect rows (call C4).
- **Also waiting for the stage's landing ceremony** (`sheet-land`, `src/styles/shared/atelier.css`). No CI row shows a rest read taken mid-landing: every AT1 cell but this one passed on every CI run that ran it, and all three failing rest reads are the landed fit (call C5).
- **CPU throttling as the staged reproduction.** It does not reach the render worker (above); the held `postMessage` does.
