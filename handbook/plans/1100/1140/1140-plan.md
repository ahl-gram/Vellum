## The design

1. **The dropdowns (Issue #1140).** The four dropdowns on the Broadside (`#type`, `#band`, `#style`, `#theme`) take a 16 px font in a rule of the Broadside's own, `.broadside .l-ctl > select`, with their block padding trimmed from 0.22rem to 0.19rem so each box keeps today's height (31.1 px against 31 px; the shape box stays 30 px). The rule that set the padding for both, `.broadside .l-ctl > :is(select, input.place-box)`, is split: the dropdowns get the new rule and the shape box keeps 0.22rem inside its own rule `.broadside .l-ctl > input.place-box`. The split is required, not tidiness: `:is()` takes the specificity of its most specific argument, `input.place-box` (0,1,1), so that rule ranks (0,3,1) and beats a bare `.broadside .l-ctl > select` (0,2,1) on `padding-block`; a trim written beside it is present and loses (measured: the probe's first pass read every trim at 32 px, `out/1140-dry-run/1140-probe3-lost-trim.log`). Measured result: every seat's slip height at 1024 by 768 is today's to the pixel (plain 416, counted 472, place on the archipelago 489).
2. **The tallest seat (Issue #1151).** The Broadside's slip body takes a bottom padding of 0.4rem instead of the kit's 1.3rem, in `#broadside .slip-body`, beside the Broadside's existing `#broadside .slip-head` rule. The looseness readout keeps Alex's words and keeps wrapping to two lines past 80; the slip gains 14.4 px. Measured at 1024 by 768: the tallest seat (a counted archipelago, Texas picked, looseness at the end) reads 489 px of content in a body whose cap is 495, 6 px to spare, the same 6 px the place-on-the-archipelago seat has on main today (PR #1145's own figure, re-measured here at 489 of 495).
3. **The checks.** BR16 is extended to that tallest seat, reached by the reader's own gestures inside `br16To19`, which already stands on the place-on-the-archipelago seat after BR19's pick, so no navigation is added. A new step BR21 reads the computed font size of the four dropdowns and the shape box. RH28's clause that held the house idiom's 0.95rem on `#type` moves to the Specimen Book's `#sb-report`, a plain primary that still wears it.
4. **The doctrine.** `handbook/specs/ui-design.md`: the 16 px paragraph says the floor binds on a slip as in the corner chrome (ruling 1), and the Broadside paragraph names the tallest seat, its check and the tightened foot as provisional (ruling 2).
