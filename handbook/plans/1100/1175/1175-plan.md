## The design

`runnerHooks` in `e2e/support/runner.ts` moves its one viewport reset, `await bounded(ctx.clearMobile(), boundMs);`, out of `onSuiteError` and into `onSuiteNotWhole`, as that hook's first statement, before the media clear PR #1176 put there. `runSelected` in `e2e/support/suites.ts` already awaits `onSuiteNotWhole` after a suite that stopped early and after one whose step never reached its assertion, and never after a whole one, so:

- a lane in which every suite runs whole sends the browser nothing new;
- a suite that stopped early is followed by the same calls as today, in the same order (the reset, then the media clear), since today `onSuiteError`'s reset runs before `onSuiteNotWhole`'s clear; one thing does move on that path: `runSelected` reads a suite's `ms` after `onSuiteError` and before `onSuiteNotWhole`, so the reset's time, which a stopped suite's reported seconds carry today, now falls after them, as the media clear's already does (milliseconds on a live browser, at most the 5 s bound on one that stopped answering);
- a suite whose step caught a throw is now followed by the reset too, which is the change.

`onSuiteError` keeps its stderr line and its red check and loses `async`, since it no longer awaits anything (Calls made C4). Nothing is printed by the rescue.

### Alternatives rejected, with the reason

- **The media clear first, then the reset** (the body's "beside"): it would reorder what a stopped suite's rescue sends today for no gain. C3.
- **The two resets run at once (`Promise.all`)**: halves the worst-case wait when the browser stops answering, but interleaves the CDP calls (`clearMobile` is two sends) and changes the stopped path's order; that wait is already 10 s on the stopped path today. C3.
- **One bounded race around both resets**: a reset that hangs would then also keep the media clear from being sent.
- **Fix the CD48 path in the suite instead** (a `finally` in `cd48TouchHandle`): the path does not leak (above), and the class needs the runner.
- **Make region-detail size its own window**: measured unnecessary (Part 2), and a file the issue never names.
- **A browser check of the rescue**: C8.
