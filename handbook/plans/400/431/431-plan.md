## The design

**Two new event kinds, computed from the road chain, in a new module.** `src/itinerary/realm-wood.ts` holds the two rules as pure functions over a per-cell array, so the rule is tested on synthetic arrays and the world reading on real roads:

- **A border** (`kind: "border"`, the discriminant Alex left to the builder in comment 5533954720, since `"crossing"` is a river): `borderSteps(labels)` reads the realm label of each chain cell (`world.realms.labels`, flat index). The road sets out in the first named realm it stands in (a leading stretch of label -1 is skipped). A border fires at chain step `k`, the first cell of a run of a named realm other than the current one, when that run holds `BORDER_HOLD = 3` cells or more, or runs to the road's end; the run's realm becomes the current one. A run of -1 is never a realm and never a border, and a shorter run of another realm is a graze and fires nothing. `borderEvents` names each step from `world.names.realms`: `name` the realm entered, `fromName` the realm left. A world of one realm has one label, so it fires nothing; no guard on `names.realms.length` is written.
- **The named wood** (`kind: "forest"`): `woodEntries(inside)` reads whether each chain cell is in the named wood. The road enters the wood at the first cell `k` of a run of `WOOD_HOLD = 6` cells or more inside it (or a run reaching the road's end), when none of the `WOOD_REARM = 24` cells before `k` is inside the wood; a road that sets out inside the wood has not entered it. `forestEvents` takes the wood from `namedForestBlob` (below) and names each entry with `world.names.forest`.

Both carry `k`, the integer chain step (the road's sample index: `input.samples[k]` is chain cell `k`), and `dist`, the distance along the road, as every kind does (the union's own comment: fork keys use `k`). The events draw nothing from the rng; their captions do (below).

**One gate for the named wood, shared with the chart.** A new `src/render/named-forest.ts` takes `FOREST_BIOMES` out of `src/render/layers/feature-labels.ts` (the chart's five: temperate forest, rainforest, tropical forest, jungle, taiga, per re-baseline point 4 and the ruling) and holds `namedForestBlob(world)`: `null` when the world names no forest, otherwise `largestBlob` (4-connected, `src/render/blobs.ts`) over the five biomes, `null` again when it holds fewer than 25 cells. `forestLabelNodes` calls it in place of its own three lines, computing the same blob in the same order, so every chart is byte-identical; the itinerary calls the same function, so the plate and the map read one wood. Under Q3's ruling that is the whole gate: the Ribbon captions the wood by its size, whether or not the chart has found room for its name, and the chart's own room is the separate issue below.

**The events join the road's list after the branches.** `findEvents` in `src/itinerary/events.ts` spreads `...borderEvents(...)` and `...forestEvents(...)` after `...branchEvents(...)`, before the summit is pushed and the list is stably sorted by `dist`. A border fires at `k >= 1` and the wood at `k >= 1` (a road that sets out inside has not entered), so the departure stays the first event; a border or wood event at the road's end sorts after the arrival waypoint and, past the last strip, is dropped as every such event is.

**The captions (Q2, ruled: a few phrasings each, picked road by road).** `src/itinerary/prose.ts` gains two lists beside `NAMED_CROSSINGS`, three phrasings each as every list there has three, in the house's road-book register (the summits' "the height of the way", the crossings' "here the ... is crossed by a bridge"):

- `ENTERED_REALMS`: `here you enter ${n}`, `here begins ${n}`, `the road passes into ${n}`.
- `ENTERED_WOODS`: `the road enters ${n}`, `the way runs into ${n}`, `into the shade of ${n}`.

`eventCaption` gains `case "border": return rng.fork(\`border-${e.k}\`).pick(ENTERED_REALMS)(e.name);` and `case "forest": return rng.fork(\`forest-${e.k}\`).pick(ENTERED_WOODS)(e.name);`, the bridges' form: a named fork per event keyed by its step, so the pick is the same for the plate and the slip (both fork `ribbon-${fromIdx}-${toIdx}` from the seed) and moves no other draw (`handbook/specs/engine-invariants.md`, Fork derivation). The name is the world's own, its leading "The" kept, as the plate's title line already writes "in The Hauwaiwa Atolls" and as Alex ruled for the bridge captions on Issue #1215: no phrasing puts "the" before the name, so "The Hauwaiwa Atolls", "Greater Tolo" and "Deep Railo" each read whole, and none can double the article. No phrasing prints a term of the genre: "the way" is already the summits' word. In `src/itinerary/dress/captions.ts`, both kinds take the description's first seat (`captionSeat`: `{ sx: p.sx, sy: p.sy - 8, side: freeSide, lines: [{ text: caption }] }`, the crossing's), both rewrap narrower before anything shrinks (`rewrap` lists them beside crossing and summit), neither keeps any art clear (`eventKeep` pushes the crossing's mark box for `"crossing"` only, by name), and **a border's caption wants its block's foot 3 units above its line**: in `layCaption`, the wanted top is `p.sy - 3 - need` for a border and `first.sy + 2.6` for every other kind, inside the same `Math.min(..., track - need)` and `Math.max(..., strip.y0 + 22)` clamps, so "here you enter" stands in the realm it names. Everything else of the seating is `seatCaption` as Issue #511 left it.

**The border line (Q1, ruled D).** Each drawn border is ruled straight across its strip at the border's height `sy` (`stripPos(strip, e.dist).sy`), between the band's inner hairlines on antique (`stripBody(strip).left(sy) + BAND` to `.right(sy) - BAND`) and the inner rules on ink (`strip.x0 + 3` to `strip.x0 + strip.w - 3`), in the dress's own border tokens on `MapStyle` in `src/render/style.ts` (`borderStroke`, `borderDash` joined by spaces, `borderOpacity`; on antique and ink these are the dress's ink, `6 3 0.6 3` and 0.85, the chart's realm border) at a layer width of 1.1, round caps, `class="border"`. **It gives way to marks, not to lettering**: it is broken wherever it would cross, within 2 units, a box the strip keeps clear (a town's houses, a summit's glyph, a crossing's mark, the ink plate's fifth-league numerals, the continuation line) or the square around a disc it keeps (the rose, its ornament, a league number), and a piece shorter than 3 units is not drawn. It is drawn after the strip's event art, under the captions layer. **Lettering over it takes its backing**: every caption with a line box whose height, widened by 2 units each way, holds a border line's `sy` on its strip is drawn as a backed caption (`captionNode` with `backed: true`: the 2.4 stroke of the dress's ground under the letters, the across-the-road seat's backing), so the line runs under the words and is blanked only under the letters. The seat itself is not changed: the backing is drawn, not seated. A new `src/itinerary/dress/border-line.ts` holds the heights, the line and the test for lettering on it; `eventNodes` in `src/itinerary/dress/strip.ts` draws the lines once after its loop from the strip's keep, and `stripNodes` backs the captions.

**The realm names on the band (painted dress only; "names at the line", ruled with Q1's D).** `realmEdges` leaves `src/itinerary/dress/strip.ts`. A new `src/itinerary/dress/bands.ts` draws the band names for the whole plate, called from `renderRibbon` in `src/itinerary/dress/plate.ts` after the strips' art and before the captions group, on the painted dress alone (ink has no band):

- **A road with no drawn border** keeps today's two ends exactly: `realmFrom` centred on the first strip's left band (`x0 + 4.6`, `rotate(-90)`), `realmName` centred on the last strip's right band (`x0 + w - 4.6`, `rotate(90)`), each only when not null.
- **A road with drawn borders** draws no end names (they moved to the line). For each drawn border in road order, on the left band of its strip (`x0 + 4.6`), reading up the scroll (`rotate(-90)`): the **new** realm's name starts `GAP = 5` above the line (`text-anchor: start` at `sy - 5`), and the **old** realm's name ends 5 below it (`text-anchor: end` at `sy + 5`). A name needs its house-estimated length (`textBox(0, 0, name, 7.6, "start", WIDTH_FACTOR.mixed, 1.2).w`) between the line's gap and `PAD = 6` inside the strip's end. Where the new name lacks that room above, it starts at the foot of the next strip's left band (`start` at `next.y0 + next.h - 6`), the place the road comes to after the line; where the old name lacks it below, it ends at the top of the previous strip's left band (`end` at `prev.y0 + 6`); where there is no such strip, that name is not drawn. A name whose span would meet a name already placed on the same strip is not drawn (the same realm named twice between two close borders).
- Every name keeps today's lettering: the style's `fontFamily`, 7.6, italic, letter-spacing 1.2, `fill` the dress's ink, `dominant-baseline: middle`, in today's attribute order.

On the eight seeds this draws 7,098 of 8,158 names beside their own line, moves 937 to the neighbouring strip and does not draw 123 (1.5 percent; `431-bands.log`); on the 75 journeys, 154 names, 11 moved, none dropped, every one inside its strip's ends (`431-sweep75.log`).

**The slip.** `RibbonRow.kind` is `RibbonEvent["kind"]`, so the rows carry the new kinds with no change to `src/site/explorer/ribbon-job.ts`; `rowText` in `src/site/ribbon/row-text.ts` sets a non-waypoint row's caption in the italic as a crossing's; `rowNode` in `src/site/ribbon/seats.ts` writes `li.border` and `li.forest`; `rowProspectTarget` in `src/site/ribbon/address.ts` gives them no prospect. ### Alternatives rejected

- **One wood caption per raw entry:** up to dozens a road on a ragged edge (`431-forest-wide.log`). **One per road, the first entry only:** a road that leaves the wood for 100 cells and enters it again would not say so; the 24-cell clearance keeps that case (1,374 roads enter twice, 79 three times, over 24,778).
- **Wood entries counted from the end of the last counted stay** (a nick of the edge ignored) instead of from the last cell inside: it changes seed 42 Paukilua to Loatunui's plate from the one Alex is shown and no other still road (`431-stays-diff.log`); the stills' rule is kept.
- **The ribbon's own three tree biomes** (`FOREST` in `src/itinerary/dress/strip.ts`): re-baseline point 4 and the ruling both send the plate to the chart's five.
- **A copy of `FOREST_BIOMES` and the 25 in the itinerary:** two copies of one gate drift; one function serves both.
