## The design

**One press, the chart's touch press, at the top of the Surveyor's Glass, in every chart room and on home.** It is hidden in the markup and shown by script only where touch is the main pointer, the house's own gate, `(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)` (Alex, item 4), read once as the page boots. So every phone and, most likely, every iPad get the press (what an iPad reports with a trackpad is UNVERIFIABLE here); touchscreen laptops and desktop touchscreens, whose main pointer is a mouse or trackpad, get none, and there the chart takes a finger exactly as today. It starts OFF on every visit: off, a finger belongs to the page everywhere, on the chart and off it (one finger scrolls the page, two fingers zoom it), and the Glass's presses, the wheel and the keys still drive the chart. Pressed, the chart takes the finger exactly as it does today. The choice is held for the visit (this tab's session storage), so it follows the reader from room to room.

**The camera's half (`src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`).** The controller keeps a touch state, true by default, which the press flips through a new `takeTouch(on)`. Off: d3-zoom's touch listeners are unbound from the gesture box (`sel().on("touchstart.zoom touchmove.zoom touchend.zoom touchcancel.zoom", null)`), the box loses its `zoomable` class (so the eight rooms' existing `#map-viewport.zoomable { touch-action: none }` rules stop applying and a new kit rule gives the box `touch-action: manipulation`), and the behaviour's filter refuses a `mousedown` or `dblclick` that followed a touch `pointerdown` on the box. On: the touch listeners are bound again by re-calling the behaviour on the box (idempotent; `__zoom` is kept) and the class returns. d3's `touchable` is replaced by one that reads the state, so the Explorer's per-draw `attach()` never rebinds touch while the press is off. The filter's touch-event arm is a backup: with the listeners unbound no touch event reaches it, and it holds only for a press flipped while a finger is down. Wheel, mouse, keys and the programmatic entry points (`glideBy`, `glideHome`, `zoomTo`, `refit`, `panBy`) never pass through either gate.

**`manipulation`, not `auto`, while off.** A tap on the chart (a town, a Hunt guess) does not wait for a possible double tap under `manipulation`, as it does not today under `none`; under `auto`, on a page laid out 1024 wide, a phone may wait about 300ms before every tap (UNVERIFIABLE in the harness, a phone pass item). The cost is the page's own double-tap zoom over the chart; a pinch still zooms the page.

**The press's half (`src/site/shared/chart-touch.ts`, new).** `bindChartTouch(press, take, env)` shows the press only where touch is the main pointer, reads the visit's choice, sets `aria-pressed` and calls `take`, and on a click flips, stores and calls `take` again. Three callers, one per way a Glass is bound today: `bindGlassKeys` in `src/site/shared/glass-keys.ts` (the seven rooms on a plain controller), `glassPresses` in `src/site/explorer/glass.ts` (the Explorer, wired by id as its doctrine line requires), and `src/site/home/app.ts` (home's own camera).

**Home's half (`src/site/home/input.ts`, `src/site/home/app.ts`, `public/index.css`).** Home's stage gets the same `zoomable` class with the same meaning (it takes a finger), added at boot and toggled by the press. `bindStageInput` takes a `takesTouch` getter: with it false a touch pointer never enters the pinch (no press, no pan, no pinch), and a `dblclick` that followed a touch pointer never dives. Home's `touch-action: pan-y` on the stage and on its controls applies only under `.zoomable`; without it the stage is `manipulation` and its controls fall back to the stage's.

**The look (ruling 1, candidate A):** a manicule (☞, U+261E, the plain serif stack as `handbook/specs/ui-design.md` requires of an ornament) as a fourth Glass press, first in the column, at the Glass's own gap, dressed at rest exactly as the other presses; pressed, it is lit: `--parchment-bright` ground, `--ink-dark` glyph, a `--parchment` rule, which no hover or focus wears, so a press just tapped off (still hovered and focused) never reads as on. Accessible name "The chart in hand", with `aria-pressed`. It is not called "the Hand": the Broadside already has a panel of that name.

### Rejected, with the reason (do not rediscover these mid-build)
