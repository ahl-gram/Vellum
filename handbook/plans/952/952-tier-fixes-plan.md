## The design

### F2: the larger house and the cross wheel

`src/render/layers/settlements.ts` draws the farmstead's house at scale `HOUSE_SCALE = 1.8` (the ruled 1.15 before) and the mill's wheel at radius `WHEEL_RADIUS = 3.9` (3.2 before) with two spokes, a horizontal and a vertical diameter, at stroke `SPOKE_WIDTH = 1.2` (four spokes at 0.7 before). These are the arm M5's sitting showed as "the same drawn larger" and the plate read measured as `large-cross` (`out/952-tier-fixes/plate/png/marks-crops-*.png`). The fills, strokes, outline widths and the house's outline in `labelHalo` stay as they are. `finerMarkBox`, the box each mark reserves before any name is set (M11), is computed from the same constants, so the reservation grows with the mark. The key's farmstead icon, drawn by the same `settlementGlyph`, is lowered `4 * k` instead of `3 * k`, which brings its centre to its row's text ink centre (measured 1.00 unit above it at `3 * k`); the capital's and the seat's icons keep `3 * k` and the mill's icon stays centred.

### F3: a farm stands where its whole mark's ground is land

`placeFarmsteads` snaps each candidate with a new `snapToStandingLand` instead of `snapToLand`: the same scan, from the candidate's own cell outward in `snapToLand`'s ring order within the same radius, but a cell is taken only when every in-grid cell of a block around it is land: two cells either side, three above and one below (`MARK_FOOTPRINT` `{ up: 3, down: 1, side: 2 }`), the block the larger marks need. The block is whole cells because the drawn coast is marching squares over the cell centres: inside a block whose every cell centre is land the unsmoothed contour cannot pass. At 4.276 units a region cell, the larger house with half its outline reaches 1.09 cells either side and 2.10 up, and the cross wheel with half its rim 1.03 cells round, so the wheel's rim reaches 0.03 cells past the block's bottom row and its sides keep 0.97 cells. The coast's Chaikin smoothing can pull the drawn coastline a fraction of a cell into the land at an outward corner and no bound for it is derived, so the fit is a measurement, not a guarantee: no mark off the drawn land with the larger marks and the move over the 34 sheets (Mara aside). A candidate with no such cell in reach is not placed, as one with no land in reach is not today. `snapToLand` is untouched.

- Measured with the larger marks (`census-summary-control+final+final-cover.txt`, arm `final`): 6 of 315 marks move, by 1 to 4 cells (Raihaikoa 304,98 to 305,98 on chart 42; Pan 201,142 to 203,141; Gorgrad 310,55 to 311,59; Krysty 278,220 to 280,220; Bru 94,136 to 96,138; Nuapaco 230,199 to 232,199), none is lost, none changes between farmstead and mill, and every mark's 25 samples then lie on the drawn land on all 34 sheets. On the test witnesses the data of the capital's, Paukilua's, Laulume's and the sixty-fourth's windows is unchanged (`probe-final.ts`). Radin's sheet is a ruled still (M11's near-towns page), so Gorgrad's move joins the deviations in the PR body.
- The data moves with it, so the drawing, the place list and the Chart Table's redraw agree with no render-side rule.

The marks under the furniture stay as drawn, faded under the panel, as hamlets and towns are today. Only the errata row changes (Rosters and doctrine).
