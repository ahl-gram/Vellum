## What this pull request delivers

When a reader arrives in the Explorer from a Try it on the Primer (`?step=<id>` before the hash), the room rings the control the step names, lays the house's manicule beside the ring, and seats a slim note under the head cluster's trail with the step in hand: "From The Primer", the lesson and the step's place ("The Land, step 2 of 6"), a dot per step of the lesson, the step's sentence with its controls as chips, "Back to The Primer" and "Next step". When the room sees the step done, the step is sealed in the Primer's store (`vellum.primer.v1`), the note's row reads the wax seal with the step's number, "done" and "Next, <word> ›", and the key leaves the address with the hash kept. A step with nothing to see is sealed on arrival; a tablet or motion step opened where it cannot apply is never sealed and its note carries its mark. A small × sets the note aside, which drops the key too. The other eight rooms take the hand in later pull requests (M1).

It covers the 41 steps whose Try it opens `/explorer/`: the Explorer's own 37 and four from other ladders (`prospect-enter`, `ribbon-enter`, `explorer-portfolio-turn-down`, `explorer-portfolio-take`). A step id addressed to another room is ignored here, so the Next press of those four, which leads into rooms this pull request does not reach, lands in a room whose page loads no hand: the key rides there harmlessly and nothing reads it.

## The design

**The steps a room reads are a data file the build writes** (M2). `scripts/primer-steps.ts` writes `generated/primer/steps.json` from `handSteps(LADDERS)`, one record per step (40,464 bytes, 6,519 gzipped: `out/1253-dry-run/1253-json-weigh.log`). The hand fetches `/primer/steps.json` only when the address carries a valid `step` key, so a room visited without one downloads nothing more, and no room's bundle imports a ladder (guarded, PK16). Measured against importing the ladders as code in the real press (`out/1253-dry-run/1253-weigh.log`, `node out/1253-dry-run/1253-weigh.ts`, minify false as the press ships): every ladder on demand adds 76,584 bytes (24,482 gzipped) to a key-bearing Explorer visit and 98,377 to 120,990 elsewhere, and only the ladders addressed to the room 33,321 (12,221 gzipped) in the Explorer; and either way the press re-cuts every room's boot into more files (the Explorer from 21 to 31 or 37), an extra cost on every visit, key or not. The hand's own code, imported statically, costs the Explorer 2,520 bytes (927 gzipped) on every visit and no new file (arm e of `out/1253-dry-run/1253-weigh-de.log`, `node out/1253-dry-run/1253-weigh.ts cde`, the hand stood in by `out/1253-dry-run/weigh-entries/hand-standin.ts`); once every room imports it, it is one shared file the press names in place of one it names today (arm d), about 2.6 KB a room.

**The hand is a shared kit piece, pure where it can be.** Pure modules under `src/site/shared/primer/` hold what a unit test can drive (the key, the step's record, the address, when it seals, the outcome's judgement, the ring's box, the note's words); `src/site/shared/primer-hand.ts` (the file the issue names) binds them to the page; the note's markup is built by its own module, testable under linkedom as `bindPrimer` is.

**When a step is sealed** (`sealWhen`):
- `never`: the step is marked `only` and does not apply on this device (`applies`, touch through `TOUCH_PRIMARY`, motion through `prefersReduce()`), ruling M4 of Issue #1155. The note carries its mark ("on a tablet", "with motion on") in the Primer page's mark dress and no done row; the key stays until set aside.
- `arrival`: no `then`, no `do`, not play, not a commission: a step with nothing to see, sealed on arrival by its Try it (N2).
- `use` (M3): a play step, a commission, and a step with a `do` and no `then`: sealed when the reader uses a control the step names or acts on. Each `do` act maps to the event a reader's use sends on its `css`: `click` and `repeat` a `click`; `set`, `choose` and `pick` a `change`; `key` a `keydown` of that key; `wait` none. A step whose `do` is empty (the commission) watches a `change` on each control of its `words` and `relies`, in that order, each css once. Listeners are on the document in the capture phase; a target counts when `target.closest(css)` matches, or the target is a `label` whose `control` matches.
- `outcome`: every other step with a `then`: sealed when every `then` entry holds and its reads differ from the reads taken when the hand armed (call C1). The reads use the same judgement as the drift check (`wordsOf`, `wordsHold`, `stateOf`, `fits`, moved into `src/site/shared/primer/judge.ts`), with the facts gathered from the live element without scrolling it. A `MutationObserver` on `document.body` (subtree, child list, attributes, character data) schedules one judgement per animation frame.

**What the ring wraps** (M4 and call C2). Each frame the hand finds the step's named controls (`words`) that stand (shown, with a box at least 2 px each way); a control's box is taken together with its labels' boxes (a form control) or its control's box (a label). If none stands, it takes the first act of `first` that is not a `wait` and whose control stands. The ring is their union grown by 6 px; the manicule stands 44 px to its left, centred on it, never less than 4 px from the window's left edge. Where nothing stands, the ring and the manicule hide (a step with no named control, `explorer-look-about`, `explorer-dog-ear` and the commission, shows its note alone). The ring and manicule are `div`s appended to `document.body`, fixed, never inside `#map`. A style is written only when the box changed, so the hand adds no mutation to a room at rest.

**The note** is an `aside.primer-slip` appended as the last child of `header.chrome`, so the room's existing refit (`refitOnChrome` in `src/site/shared/room.ts` observes `header.chrome`) fits the chart under it and the head's slide-away carries it. Its width is `min(620, L - head.left - 24)` px, `L` the room's open slip's left edge (`.slip` not `.folded`) or the window's width, re-read each frame (the seats round's B2, `out/1248/mock/seats-hand.ts`). **Where it meets Sub 8** (Issue #1254): Sub 8's "learn this room" press, ruled on a line of its own under the trail (Issue #1155 comment 6754142680, item 3), is also a child of `header.chrome`; the note, appended last, stands under it, and the two together leave the Explorer's chart 444 by 342 at 1024 and 495 by 382 at 1280 (`out/1248/combined/notes.md`, ruled with the combination in comment 6754985610). Whichever pull request lands second merges the two `extraCss` lines and keeps the note last in the cluster. Its words: "From The Primer"; `<lesson title>, step <n> of <of>`; dots, `on` for each step of the lesson sealed in the store (and the step in hand once done), `now` for the step in hand while not done; the sentence with its chips (`segmentsOf`); "Back to The Primer" to `/primer/#<step id>`; "Next step" (a pressed-tan press) to the next step's address while not done; once done, the seal (`b.primer-seal`, the step's number in its lesson), "done" and "Next, <word> ›" (gold) to the same address, `<word>` the next step's first marked phrase, or "Next step ›" where it marks none; no next press after a ladder's last step. The × (`button.primer-slip-aside`, `aria-label` "Set the step aside", M5) removes the ring, the manicule and the note, stops every watcher and drops the key.

**The address.** Done or set aside, the hand calls `history.replaceState(history.state, "", withoutStep(pathname, search, hash))`: the `step` key goes, every other query key and the hash stay. The room's own hash writes keep whatever search stands (`replaceState` with a `#` address), measured in all nine rooms by the round (`design/the-primer/measure/query-key-results.json`) and again on this build (the key survives the Explorer's boot at all 41 steps, `out/1253-dry-run/1253-probe-A.log`).

**The Explorer boots the hand once its first draw has landed**: `bootHandOnce({ route: "/explorer/" })` after `landDraw(...)` inside `draw()`'s `.then`, which on the first draw places the chart in `#map` synchronously (`landInPlace`; `#map` is empty in the built markup, so the first draw never turns). Arming after the chart is in place keeps a chart's arrival from reading as the reader's change.

**The look** (M6): the ring, the manicule and the note are dressed as the two rounds drew them (`RING_CSS` in `design/the-primer/mock/inject.ts`; `SLIP_CSS` `.slim` in `out/1248/mock/seats-slip.ts`; the done row as Issue #1251's words sitting set it, `out/1251/w10-note-done-a-2x.png`; the seal at 1.5rem as the seats round's item 6 measured it), with one change the contrast floor needs: the manicule takes a dark outline painted under its pale glyph (`-webkit-text-stroke: 0.18em var(--chart-ink)` with `paint-order: stroke fill`), since as drawn its glyph reads 1.39:1 over a pale chart (`out/1253/measure/measurements.md`, the card arm). Measured on the spike (`out/1253-dry-run/1253-stroke-018.log`, `node out/1253-dry-run/1253-stroke.ts read` after shooting `out/1253/stroke/shots.json`): over the pale chart its outline reads 8.70:1 against the ground and its core 8.46:1 against the outline; over the seed box its pale core reads 10.51:1. A 0.06em stroke painted over the fill was tried first and lost the glyph over a dark ground (`out/1253-dry-run/1253-stroke.log`, 1.39:1). The ring's outer halo breathes (C13). Under reduced motion the blanket in `src/styles/shared/motion.css` cuts its one iteration to 0.01 ms, so nothing pulses. The ring, the manicule and the note print as nothing.

### Alternatives rejected

- **The ladders imported as code on demand** (M2's other arms): rejected for the weight and the re-cut boot above.
- **A hash key** for the step: ruled out by Issue #1155 ruling 12.
- **The hand evaluating `then` with its own copy of the judge**: `vellum/no-word-for-word-copy` and the step 14 copy hunt refuse a second copy; the pure judge moves to `src/site/shared/` and `e2e/` imports it.
- **The hand performing `first` acts for the reader** (M4 (b)): a synthetic click on the engine's town or the Drawer, and in a later room (`print-room-road`) half the step's own sentence done for the reader.
- **Sealing an outcome that already holds on arrival** (C1): `explorer-sea-level` (its readout says "34% land" on arrival), `explorer-drawer` (the Drawer's count holds its text while shut) and the commission would seal before the reader did anything (`out/1253-dry-run/1253-probe-A.log`).
- **One ring per named control**: the round drew one ring; the widest union among the 41 steps is 360.8 x 31.1 px (`explorer-theme`), one Broadside row.
- **The note's width as a CSS calc from tokens**: the ruled seat is measured off the Broadside's box (`out/1248/mock/seats-hand.ts`), and the Explorer's inset token is redeclared per page.
- **Splitting `src/site/explorer/app.ts`** (434 lines on main, 436 after): call C7.
