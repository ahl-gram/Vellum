## What this pull request delivers

When a reader arrives in the Explorer from a Try it on the Primer (`?step=<id>` before the hash), the room does five things:
- It rings the control the step names and lays the house's manicule beside the ring.
- It seats a slim note under the head cluster's trail with the step in hand: "From The Primer", the lesson and the step's place ("The Land, step 2 of 6"), a dot for each step of the lesson, the step's sentence with its controls as chips, "Back to The Primer" and "Next step".
- When the room sees the step done, it seals the step in the Primer's store (`vellum.primer.v1`). The note's row then reads the wax seal with the step's number in its lesson, "done" and "Next, <word> ›", and the key leaves the address with the hash kept.
- A step with nothing to see is sealed on arrival. A tablet or motion step opened where it cannot apply is never sealed, and its note carries its mark.
- A small × sets the note aside, which drops the key too.

The other eight rooms take the hand in later pull requests (M1).

It covers the 41 steps whose Try it opens `/explorer/`: the Explorer's own 37 and four from other ladders (`prospect-enter`, `ribbon-enter`, `explorer-portfolio-turn-down`, `explorer-portfolio-take`). A step id addressed to another room is ignored here. So the Next press of those four, which leads into rooms this pull request does not reach, lands in a room whose page loads no hand: the key rides there harmlessly and nothing reads it.

**The hand is a shared kit piece, pure where it can be.**
- Pure modules under `src/site/shared/primer/` hold what a unit test can drive: the key, the step's record, the address, when it seals, the outcome's judgement, the ring's box and the note's words.
- `src/site/shared/primer-hand.ts` (the file the issue names) binds them to the page through `HandEnv`. That makes its reads of the page (the location, the store, the device, the frame, the scroll, the page's width) arguments a unit test can state.
- The note's markup is built by its own module, testable under linkedom as `bindPrimer` is.

**When a step is sealed** (`sealWhen`):
- `never`: the step is marked `only` and does not apply on this device (`applies`; touch through `TOUCH_PRIMARY`, motion through `prefersReduce()`), ruling M4 of Issue #1155. The note carries its mark ("on a tablet", "with motion on") in the Primer page's mark dress, and no done row. The key stays until the note is set aside.
- `arrival`: no `then`, no `do`, not play and not a commission. This is a step with nothing to see, sealed on arrival by its Try it (N2).
- `use` (M3): a play step, a commission, and a step with a `do` and no `then`. It is sealed when the reader uses a control the step names or acts on.
  - Each `do` act maps to the event a reader's use sends on its `css`: `click` and `repeat` to a `click`; `set`, `choose` and `pick` to a `change`; `key` to a `keydown` of that key; `wait` to none.
  - **(revision 1, C25)** M3's words narrow two cases. A `set` act whose next act is a `key` act on the same control gives no watch of its own, since the key is the use ("any number at Enter"): `explorer-enter` seals at Enter, not on the seed box's `change` when it loses focus. A `key` act whose key is an arrow key watches a `keydown` of each of the four arrow keys ("an arrow key"), in the order `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `ArrowUp`, `ArrowDown`. M3's "+" is the Glass's + press, `#zoom-in`, which `explorer-look-about`'s `click` act already watches.
  - A step whose `do` is empty (the commission) watches a `change` on each control of its `words` and `relies`, in that order, each css once.
  - Listeners are on the document in the capture phase. A target counts when `target.closest(css)` matches, or when the target is a `label` whose `control` matches.
- `outcome`: every other step with a `then`. It is sealed when every `then` entry holds and its reads differ from the reads taken when the hand armed (call C1).
  - The reads use the same judgement as the drift check (`wordsOf`, `wordsHold`, `stateOf`, `fits`, moved into `src/site/shared/primer/judge.ts`), with the facts gathered from the live element without scrolling it.
  - A `MutationObserver` on `document.body` (subtree, child list, attributes, character data) schedules one judgement per animation frame.
  - Read again on the built hand at all 41 steps, no `outcome` step seals on arrival, and the ten `arrival` steps all do. Three `then`s already hold on arrival and stay unsealed: `explorer-sea-level`, `explorer-drawer` and the commission (`use`). See parts A and T of `out/1253-dry-run/1253-r1-probe-89bfacd6.log`, from `node out/1253-dry-run/1253-r1-probe.ts ABCT`.

**What the ring wraps** (M4 and call C2).
