## What this pull request delivers

When a reader arrives in the Print Room from a Try it on the Primer (`/print-room/?step=<id>`, `#seed=42` on seven of the eight steps), the room does what the Explorer, the Writing Room and the Reading Room do: once its first proof is on the desk it rings the controls the step names, lays the manicule beside the ring, seats the slim note under the head cluster's trail, seals the step when the room sees it done or used, and drops the key with the hash kept. The eight steps of the Print Room's ladder are the only ones whose Try it opens this room, and no step's press onward leads into or out of it (`out/1253-dry-run/1253-pr4-steps.log`, `node out/1253-dry-run/1253-pr4-steps.ts`).

It teaches four of the room's steps what using them is, as ruling E1 has it (ruling P1 A, "Wait for the press"): the thumbnail step at a press of a thumbnail, the keep step at Print or Download, the impression at Desk, and the road at Ribbon.

It teaches the ring every room shares that a greyed press is not yet there to press (ruling P2 a, "Bind first"): the ring holds the first thing the step leans on until the press it names can be pressed, as ruling M4 rules for a press that appears later. In this room that is the keep step's Bind, until the atlas is bound and Print and Download can be pressed.

It lists the room in `HAND_ROOMS`. The other five rooms follow in later pull requests (M1); this one leaves Issue #1253 open (call C60).
