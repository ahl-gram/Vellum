## The design

**1. The soft edge's floor stays, derived, and the window retires.** RB21 keeps asserting each strip's soft edge at no less than `BAND_FLOOR = 10` plate units part inked, unchanged in code. The bound is derived from the two things it separates: a hard edge, which anti-aliases over at most a row or two of the screen and reads 1.3 plate units (one row at the harness's 0.7995 px a plate unit) on both machines (finding 5), and the narrowest soft edge offered, a feather of 75, which reads 56.6 on the Mac and 63 on the runner at its narrowest (finding 3). 10 is 7.7 times the hard edge and under a fifth of the narrowest soft edge on either machine. The width itself is a dressing-level number, provisional until Alex has used it live, so it is not pinned. The plan's stop window of 0.7 to 0.85 times the feather was fitted to Mac readings and read as if the 0.8 of a linear ramp were the runner's too; finding 3 shows the runner's band reads 0.84 to 0.90, and finding 4 puts the cause in paint. It retires, and no ceiling replaces it: the runner's masked paint follows no model the plan can state (finding 4), so the revision's stop conditions (below) keep only a red and the floor's headroom below the narrowest feather.

**2. The dated line at `BAND_FLOOR` is rewritten** to name both machines' readings and the cause measured, and the "scales out" clause goes (finding 1). One long line, the comment rule's carve-out for a hand-measured browser quirk.

**3. The difference joins `handbook/specs/settle-doctrine.md`'s list** as one bullet, "Masks", directly under "Paint", its home `BAND_FLOOR` and this plan's readings, as that section's own rule requires ("A difference found later joins this list, in the change that records it, as one more line").

**4. RB21 holds call 2 at every boundary between two turns.** In `edgeFaults`, which already shoots each strip's middle-columns block at `i / n` (the moment its turn begins and the previous strip's ends) and at `(i + 1) / n` (`LAST_SEEK` for the last strip), each strip's block is also shot at 0 (`first`, every strip bare) and at `LAST_SEEK` (`last`, every strip but the last whole). Then, with `rowsPastHalfWay(block, from, to, noise)` counting the rows whose blend from `from` toward `to` passes half way (the same per-row blend `partInkedRows` uses, `inkedShare`):
- for every strip but the first, no row of its block at `i / n` stands past half way from `first` toward `last`: fault `no turn overlapping the next: strip i bare until strip i - 1 is whole`;
- for every strip but the last, no row of its block at `(i + 1) / n` stands past half way from `last` back toward `first`: fault `no turn overlapping the next: strip i whole as strip i + 1 begins`.
So at each boundary `k / n`, `k` from 1 to `n - 1`, the strip whose turn ends is whole and the strip whose turn begins is bare, on every row of the block, which is call 2 for any overlap of more than about a fifth of a turn (finding 9 measures where the read stops). The references are the paused animation's own frames (finding 8). The half line is the threshold (finding 9). The fault list's soft-edge faults keep their wording and come after the turn faults.

**5. The head read covers every strip.** `orderFaults`' read of every strip whole to its head at `LAST_SEEK` drops its `.slice(0, -1)`: the last strip at `LAST_SEEK` (its own progress 0.994) reads 0 columns on paper at its head row on both machines (finding 7's probe, `headShareAtEnd` of strip 5 on the fixture), and its band's lower stop stands above that row for any feather under 2000 plate units. The read stays beside the turn read (Gate 1 item 21): the turn read's whole reference is the last frame, so a band that never leaves a strip's head (row (h)) reds only the head read.

**6. The label** gains the claim: "its strips revealed one after another from the bottom-left, no strip's turn overlapping the next's, each from its foot to its head along a soft edge ...".

**7. The ui-design paragraph** marks Issue #1226's confirmed calls as the house marks a review's confirmed calls ("**Ratified at the post-use review (Issue #1226):**"), keeps the pace and the soft edge's height "**Provisional until Alex has used them live (Issue #1229):**", and is rewrapped from its fourth line on, every line at 101 characters or fewer.

**8. No merge commit.** The branch's files are untouched by main since its merge base (Planned at), PR CI checks out GitHub's merge of the head with main (`HEAD is now at 40f52e6 Merge 3e52c632... into ff67b785...` in lane B's log), and step 10's combined-state run is made locally by merging without committing and aborting.

### Alternatives rejected

- **A refitted two-sided window** (any band read from the runner's samples): a bound fitted to the samples taken (`CLAUDE.md`, "A bound is derived"); the runner's figure has no principle behind it (finding 4's path is unknown), and finding 10's row noise moves a strip by 7.8 plate units shot to shot.
- **A window on the Mac and another on the runner:** two fitted bounds instead of one.
- **Reading each strip's head at `(i + 1) / n` through the paper comparator** (the skeptic's first suggestion): measured blind (finding 7).
- **Sending the `/ 1.3` mutation to the prover** (the skeptic's other suggestion) without a new read: the run shows today's RB21 passes it (finding 6); a read had to be added first, and the prover's one round is spent (step 15).
- **The landed plate as the whole reference** (it does not depend on the timing under test): it lays out at another top (finding 8).
- **The plate with its mask taken off as the whole reference:** paints up to 60 levels apart from the masked plate on the runner (finding 4), and writes to the element under test mid-check.
- **The edge read's 0.1 and 0.9 lines for the turn read:** 0.03 of headroom on the fixture side (finding 9).
- **One summed blend over the whole block** in place of every row: the late-end overlap moves a block's sum by about a ninth, which the half line reads on a few dozen rows and a summed read barely sees.
- **Replacing the head read with the turn read:** loses row (h) (design 5).
- **Leaving the `.slice(0, -1)`:** it protects nothing (design 5), and the skeptic named it.
- **Deleting "Provisional" and naming nothing ratified:** the house form names what a review ratified (the Issue #736 line).
