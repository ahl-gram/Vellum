## The design (replaces revision 1's "Zoomed" paragraph and its `closedAt`)

**At every zoomed rest the bill stands at its content's height, exactly as at 1024 by 768.** "Zoomed" is the camera's depth past 1 (C48). Only a press glides it there in step.

**The press is the Glass's own glide between the whole sheet and the first press's depth** (C44): Draw nearer from the whole sheet, its `+` and `=` keys; The whole sheet and its `0` key; Draw back to the whole sheet and its `-` key. Through such a glide the bill closes, or opens, in step with the camera's depth: closing, it finishes as the camera reaches the first press's depth or the press's own landing if shallower; opening, it starts from where the press set out or the first press's depth if deeper, and finishes as the camera reaches the whole sheet. A press that sets out from a zoomed rest and lands on one moves nothing, since the bill is closed at both (C45). Every other move of the camera has the bill at its rest at once (https://github.com/ahl-gram/Vellum/issues/1260#issuecomment-6686257343): closed in the frame the chart first zooms, open in the frame the chart reaches the whole sheet. A double click and a touch double tap are among them: d3's own glide, not a press of the Glass, and the ruling's question named the double click.

**`closingOf`** (pure, in `bill-spread.ts`) takes `{ was, from, k, to }`: the closing at the last apply, the depth there, the depth now, and the depth the press's glide is bound for, or `null`.

- `to === null` (no press is gliding): `k > 1 ? 1 : 0`.
- `to > 1` (the press is bound for a zoomed rest): with `reach = min(to, ZOOM_STEP)`, it is 1 once `k >= reach`, else `1 - (1 - was) * (reach - k) / (reach - from)`. From the whole sheet (`was` 0, `from` 1) that telescopes to `(k - 1) / (reach - 1)`, revision 1's in-step closing. From a closed bill (`was` 1) it is 1 throughout.
- `to <= 1` (the press is bound for the whole sheet): with `reach = min(from, ZOOM_STEP)`, it is 0 once `k <= 1` or `reach <= 1`, else `min(was, was * (k - 1) / (reach - 1))`. From the first press's depth or deeper that is `min(1, (k - 1) / 0.4)`, revision 1's in-step opening; from a shallow rest (C46) it opens from closed over the whole glide, with no jump.
- The closing lands on exactly 1 and exactly 0. The float noise between is floored to the 64th grid by `spreadOf` (revision 3's C34), so the snap revision 1 gave `closedAt` has nothing left to do (C54).

**`zoomBill(k, heading)`** (in `bill-grow.ts`) keeps the depth of the last apply beside the closing and passes both to `closingOf`; as before, it writes only when the closing changes, and reads no layout.

**The heading** (in `glass.ts`) is `zoomController.glideTarget()` while Draw nearer or Draw back glides, and 1 while The whole sheet glides. The controller leaves the home glide unnamed, so `glassCard` keeps a flag the voiced home sets (`goHomeVoiced`), cleared when the camera reaches the whole sheet (an apply at k 1), when a press takes over (an apply with a target), and at the camera's settle. `setCardZoom` hands `heading(k)` to `onView`; `followView` hands it to `zoomBill`. A reduced-motion press applies at once with no target, and the bill with it, as revision 1 has it.

**The settle backstop** (C50): something that interrupts The whole sheet's glide (a wheel, a pinch, a drag, an arrow key) is not seen by the Glass, so its applies still carry the home heading and can leave the bill part open. At the camera's settle, 250 ms after its last move, a home flag still standing gives way and `setCardZoom` runs once more, which lays the bill at its rest. BR25i holds it.

**Rejected, so the builder does not rediscover them:**

- **A `heading()` read in the shared zoom controller.** It is the right home in principle, but `zoom-controller.ts` counts 396 of 400 lines and the read needs a home flag, a sequence guard on its `end interrupt cancel`, the interface line and the method: past the cap, and a split of a shared file every chart room uses.
- **Setting `glideTargetK` to 1 inside `glideHome`.** It changes what four other callers read (`glideBy` compounding, `refit`'s restart, `setScaleMax`'s interrupt, the Specimen Book's `glideTo`).
- **d3-transition's `active()` read in `glass.ts`** to tell a glide's frame from a gesture's. It makes the controller's head comment ("the ONLY file that imports d3-zoom (plus d3-selection to attach it)") false in spirit, cannot tell The whole sheet from d3's own double-click glide, and still needs the target.
- **Capture listeners for `wheel`, `pointerdown` and `touchstart` on the viewport** to end the home flag at a gesture. Exact for gestures, but an arrow key still needs the settle, so it is two mechanisms where the settle alone holds the class.
- **A slide on the side panel's curve (320 ms) after a wheel, a pinch, a double click or a link.** Ruled against (6686257343). Its first frame, staged on the candidate by writing the grown values back over the zoomed rest, hides 3,515 px² of chart after one notch and 13,623 px² under the link at 1512 by 1200 (`armB-first-frame-staged` rows in `1260-r4-cand-wheel.log` and `1260-r4-cand-rest.log`), and it eases while the camera has moved, which the "Motion and ceremony" bullet does not allow.
- **In step with the depth through the gesture, closing the rest of the way at the settle.** Ruled against (6686257343). At each pause the bill stands part grown for the settle's 250 ms: on the head, which shows exactly that until the settle, 327 px and 2,986 px² after one notch at 1512 by 1200, 9,545 px² after two, 8,774 px² under the link (`1260-r4-head-wheel.log`, `1260-r4-head-rest.log`).
- **The depth map kept and the bill closed at the settle, as the mechanism.** A press of Draw back landing short of 1.4 would open the bill part way through its glide and then close it again at the settle: a bounce, measured on the head as 65 frames part way.
- **A depth-only rule with memory** (the bill stays closed while it is closed, and opens in step as the depth falls). A wheel that falls from two notches to one leaves it part open at rest (`(1.1487 - 1) / (1.3195 - 1)`).
- **Pinning only the end states**, as revision 1's C16 had it. With the press told apart from the wheel, a wiring slip (the heading never passed, or the home flag never set) turns the press into a jump and passes every end-state check: measured, `no-heading` and `no-home-watch` red BR25h and nothing else.
