## The design

**What grows.** On a page taller than 768 the Broadside opens its gaps, groups first.

- A gap between the groups opens two shares: the gap above the Land, the gaps between the groups and the gap above the printer's line.
- A gap between rows opens one share: the ledger's row gap, the gap between a held box's rows, and the gap under each group's gloss.
- The masthead opens one share above and one below.
- A held box's note keeps today's gap under its row.

This is the round's stills as drawn (ruled 1 and 6; the fidelity rule).

**How much room it takes.** All of these come from what the page lays out:

- **natural**: the bill's own height with no gap opened.
- **room**: `placeSlip`'s inline `max-height`.
- **page**: `pageBox().h`, never the window's height. The desk frame's page is 768 tall, so it never grows.
- **width**: the bill's own `offsetWidth`.

From these:

- **allow** = 2 × (page − 768), and 0 at or under 768 (ruling 2).
- **opened** = the least of: room − natural, allow, and 2 × width − natural (ruling 3). Never less than 0.
- The opened height is shared out at one share per unit. The units are:
  - (rows + groups) × 1,
  - (groups + 1) × 2,
  - 2 × 1 for the masthead.

  So the default seat has 19 units and the held seat 21. `rows` is each ledger's grid rows less one, plus each shown held box's rows less one.

**The border to the floor** (ruling 4 with P1). Once the gaps stop at twice the width, the bill still takes the room the start leaves it: min(room, natural + allow). The height past the gaps is blank paper, laid above the printer's line. The paper holds a printer's flower, the ivy leaf with a curled stem at 1.35rem (P2, P3), between two double rules. The flower shows only when the paper is at least twice the flower's own height (P4).

**Zoomed** (ruling 5, with P5 for its motion). The shares and the paper are multiplied by (1 − closed). `closed` is the camera's depth, `closedAt(k)`, and runs from 0 at the whole sheet to 1 at the first press's depth (1.4, `ZOOM_STEP`). It snaps to exactly 1 at that depth. So the bill closes with the chart as the first press glides, finishes as the press lands, and stands at its content's height from then on. It opens the same way on the way home. Under reduced motion the camera jumps, and the bill with it.

**On a resize, and on a choice that adds or removes rows** (P6), the bill follows at once. No spacing it writes ever transitions.

**Beside the chart's slide with the fold** (Issue #1261, PR #1265). These were measured at ef0944ff, with the growth staged.

- **The two write to different elements.**
  - The slide animates only `#sheet`'s own `translate` and `scale`, composited (`createSheetSlide` in `src/site/shared/sheet-slide.ts`). It reads `#sheet`'s rect and computed width.
  - The bill writes only `#broadside`'s four custom properties and one class. It reads only the slip's sizes and inline styles.
  - Neither reads what the other writes, so the two can share a frame without either moving the other.
- **A fold while the first zoom press glides.** The fold was pressed 80 ms into the press's glide, with P5's closing staged on every frame. The sheet's slide ran as many frames as on main: 20 at 1512 by 882 and 17 at 1280 by 1024. It landed on the same box and at the same depth, 1.4, as on main, and the bill ended closed (`1260-r1-foldpace.log`, the `zoomfold-*` rows).
- **A fold with the bill grown.** The fold was pressed with the growth run in the press's own task, as `onLayout` runs it.
  - The sheet and the slip landed on main's boxes, and nothing was left running at 1280 by 800 and 1920 by 1080.
  - Their first moves, three presses each window: the sheet 32 to 49 ms after the press at five presses, against 38 to 56 ms on main, and the slip 47 to 65 ms, against 54 to 81 ms (`1260-r1-foldpace.log`).
  - The sixth press, at 1920 by 1080, moved at 79 ms (sheet) and 101 ms (slip). That is 23 ms (sheet) and 20 ms (slip) past main's latest in this probe, and inside the 58 to 128 ms the slip took on main in the stall probe below. One press is too few to call a cost, and no check asserts the first move's time.
- **The fold's first frame on a tall window** (`handbook/specs/settle-doctrine.md`, the bullet on a fold's slide; the stall belongs to the window's raster). The slip's first move after the fold press, three presses each, today against the grown bill (`1260-r1-foldstall.log`):
  - the harness's 1280 by 2263, the bill 2094 px tall: 81 to 109 ms against 61 to 128 ms today;
  - 1920 by 1080: 80 to 118 ms against 84 to 122 ms;
  - 1280 by 1200: 76 to 111 ms against 58 to 88 ms.
  - Across the three windows, the grown bill's first moves (76 to 118 ms) sit inside main's (58 to 128 ms). At 1280 by 1200 alone, one grown press (111 ms) ran later than main's latest there (88 ms). A bill four times taller does not lengthen the wait beyond what main's own presses already spread across.
- **The soft lettering Alex took at 1024 by 768 zoomed** (Issue #1261 comment 6670826059) is out of the bill's reach.
  - At 1024 by 768 the bill never grows.
  - Zoomed, it stands at its content's height.
  - Its per-frame writes during the first press land on `#broadside`, which is no ancestor of the chart's SVG. So they cannot re-raster the chart's lettering, the cost `setCardZoom` in `src/site/explorer/glass.ts` names for a write on an SVG ancestor.
- **P5's worst frame, retaken.** The ruling quoted 162 px² at 1512 by 882 and 4,089 at 1280 by 1024, read at 089881ad. At ef0944ff it reads 161 and 3,971 (`1260-r1-frames-sum.log`). The first press glides for 269 ms and 284 ms. The figures are within run-to-run spread, so the ruling reads the same.

**When it runs:**

- **At the end of every room layout**, through a new optional `onLayout` on `bindRoom`'s parts. That covers a resize, the fonts arriving, the folio or cluster changing size, a draw landing, and each fold twice: at its press and when its slide ends.
- **After every change of seat**: `refreshLand`, after `seatLand`.
- **On every camera apply**, through the Glass's `onView(k)`. This one only rescales the last measure and reads no layout.

**Reads.** The growth reads only the slip's own sizes (`offsetHeight`, `offsetWidth`, its body's `scrollHeight` and `clientHeight`, its rect's height) and its inline styles. It never reads a position. So a fold, which moves the slip by `translate` and the sheet by `translate` and `scale`, does not change what it reads, at the fold's press or at its slide's end.

**Natural height** is the slip's fractional rect height when its body does not scroll. When the body scrolls, the bill is taller than its room and opens nothing. Measured: the whole-pixel read (`offsetHeight - clientHeight + scrollHeight`) leaves the default bill scrolling by a subpixel at 1512 by 882 (`scrollHeight` 596 against 595, `1260-planned-int.log`). The fractional read leaves 595 against 595.

**Rejected, so the builder does not rediscover them:**

- **The Explorer watching the slip's inline style** (the round's other way to hear a layout). The bill writes its own custom properties on that element, so the watcher would hear its own writes. It also needs a guard the callback does not.
- **Keying the gaps to the window's height in a stylesheet alone.** The round measured that it either scrolls the held seats or half fills the default one ("Why no arm keys the gaps", `out/1260/notes.md`).
- **The mock's whole-pixel natural height**: it scrolls the default bill by a subpixel (above).
- **The mock's window height** (`documentElement.clientHeight`): it would grow the bill in the desk frame, whose page is 768.
- **A new stylesheet for the growth.** It would override declarations `broadside.css` and `broadside-bill.css` still carry, leaving them present and losing (`handbook/specs/cascade-traps.md`). The bill sheet's own head line says its values are changed in place. So every spacing is edited where it is declared.
- **Inline `height` on the slip with a flex column** (the mock's floor arm). An explicit height on the paper box needs no flex mode, no `margin-top: auto` and no second layout mode.
- **`transition-property: none` on every box in the bill** (the mock's `#broadside .broadside *`). It would also stop transitions the bill's controls own. Only the boxes whose spacing the script writes take it.
- **Closing at once on zoom, or on a timed glide on the fold's curve.** Ruled against at P5. The motion follows the camera's depth.
- **Closing in step with the head and the chart folio as they slide away.** That slide starts only at the second press, so through the first press the bill would hide 4,756 px² (1512 by 882) and 19,665 px² (1280 by 1024) more chart than today (`1260-zoom.log`, read the same at ef0944ff in `1260-r1-zoom.log`).
- **The border ending with the rows** (the playbill kept whole). Ruled against at P1.
- **A flower that shrinks to fit short paper.** Ruled against at P4.
- **A glide on a resize or a change of seat.** Ruled against at P6.

### Created

**`src/site/explorer/bill-spread.ts`** (pure, about 50 lines). One head comment line: `// How the Broadside shares a tall window's free height among its gaps (Issue #1260 comment 6666875967): pure arithmetic over what bill-grow.ts measures.`

- `export const SPREAD_START = 768;`, `export const SPREAD_PACE = 2;`, `export const SPREAD_STOP = 2;`, `export const ROW_SHARE = 1;`, `export const GROUP_SHARE = 2;`, `export const HEAD_SHARE = 1;`
- `export interface Ledger { readonly tracks: number; readonly held: readonly number[] }`. `tracks` is the ledger's grid rows; `held` is the rows of each shown held box.
- `export interface BillRoom { readonly natural: number; readonly room: number; readonly page: number; readonly width: number; readonly rows: number; readonly groups: number; readonly closed: number }`
- `export interface BillSpread { readonly row: number; readonly group: number; readonly head: number; readonly paper: number }`
- `export const NO_SPREAD: BillSpread = { row: 0, group: 0, head: 0, paper: 0 };`
- `export function rowGapsOf(ledgers: readonly Ledger[]): number` returns, summed over the ledgers, `max(0, tracks - 1)` plus each held box's `max(0, h - 1)`.
- `export function closedAt(k: number): number` (revision 1) gives how far the bill has closed at the camera depth `k`. It computes `raw = (k - 1) / (ZOOM_STEP - 1)` and returns 1 when `raw >= 1 - 1e-9`, 0 when `raw <= 1e-9`, and else `raw`. The snap is measured, not a guess: at the first press's landed depth, (1.4 − 1) / 0.4 is 0.9999999999999998 in floating point. Without the snap the bill writes 2.37e-15 px at 1512 by 882 where 0 is meant (`1260-r1-m-zoom-p5-nosnap.log`). It imports `ZOOM_STEP` from `../shared/zoom-keys.ts`, which reads no DOM at module scope.
- `export function spreadOf(b: BillRoom): BillSpread`:
  - If `!(b.room > 0)`, return `NO_SPREAD`. This is a slip not yet seated: its inline `max-height` reads `NaN`.
  - Else:
    - `allow = max(0, SPREAD_PACE * (b.page - SPREAD_START))`
    - `opened = max(0, min(b.room - b.natural, allow, SPREAD_STOP * b.width - b.natural))`
    - `units = (b.rows + b.groups) * ROW_SHARE + (b.groups + 1) * GROUP_SHARE + 2 * HEAD_SHARE`
    - `d = units > 0 ? opened / units : 0`
    - `frame = max(b.natural + opened, min(b.room, b.natural + allow))`
    - `open = 1 - min(1, max(0, b.closed))`
    - return `{ row: d * ROW_SHARE * open, group: d * GROUP_SHARE * open, head: d * HEAD_SHARE * open, paper: (frame - b.natural - opened) * open }`

**`src/site/explorer/bill-grow.ts`** (DOM-bound, about 55 lines). One head comment line: `// The Broadside measured and spread on every room layout, every change of seat and every camera depth (Issue #1260): DOM-bound at module scope by design, never imported from a unit test.`

- Module scope:
  - `const bill = $("broadside");`
  - `const body` = `bill.querySelector<HTMLElement>(".slip-body")`
  - `const flower` = `bill.querySelector<SVGSVGElement>(".bill-flower")`
  - Each lookup throws `new Error("no Broadside <part> on this page")` when null, as `panelOf` in `src/site/explorer/land-seat.ts` does.
- `const PROPS = { row: "--bill-row", group: "--bill-group", head: "--bill-head", paper: "--bill-paper" } as const;`
- `type Measured = Omit<BillRoom, "closed"> & { readonly need: number };`
- `let measured: Measured | null = null;` and `let closed = 0;`
- `function write(s: BillSpread, need: number): void` sets each of the four properties on `bill.style` to `${value}px`. Then `bill.classList.toggle("flowered", s.paper >= need)`.
- `function measure(): Measured | null`:
  - `write(NO_SPREAD, Infinity)`;
  - `room = parseFloat(bill.style.maxHeight)`; if not finite, return `null`;
  - `natural` = `body.scrollHeight > body.clientHeight ? bill.offsetHeight - body.clientHeight + body.scrollHeight : bill.getBoundingClientRect().height`;
  - the ledgers: for each `.broadside .ledger` in `bill`:
    - `tracks` = `getComputedStyle(l).gridTemplateRows.split(" ").filter(Boolean).length`;
    - `held` = one count per `:scope > .held` that is not `hidden`, counting its `:scope > .l-row` that are not `hidden`;
  - return `{ natural, room, page: pageBox().h, width: bill.offsetWidth, rows: rowGapsOf(ledgers), groups: bill.querySelectorAll(".broadside > .panel").length, need: 2 * flower.getBoundingClientRect().height }`.
- `export function growBill(): void`: `measured = measure();` then `write(measured === null ? NO_SPREAD : spreadOf({ ...measured, closed }), measured?.need ?? Infinity)`.
- `export function zoomBill(k: number): void`:
  - `const next = closedAt(k);`
  - if `next === closed`, return;
  - `closed = next;` then, if `measured !== null`, `write(spreadOf({ ...measured, closed }), measured.need)`.
  - It reads no layout, so a camera frame costs four property writes.
- Imports:
  - `$` from `../shared/by-id.ts`;
  - `pageBox` from `../shared/page-box.ts`;
  - `closedAt`, `NO_SPREAD`, `rowGapsOf`, `spreadOf` and the types from `./bill-spread.ts`.
