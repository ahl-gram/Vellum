## The fix (finding 1), and what moves

**`spreadOf` floors the one share to a whole number of 64ths, after the closing factor, and writes the row, the group and the masthead as whole multiples of it; the paper is floored the same way.** A length already on the grid lays out at exactly its value on top of any base, since adding whole units does not change the part of a unit the base loses. So the content lays out at natural + units × share ≤ room, and both sides lie on the grid, so it can never pass the room as laid. The remainder, under one 64th per share, is left at the foot (C33).

**The failing heights, after** (the same sweeps on the candidate build: `sweep cand default,held 1280,1512,1920 1 760 1110 1200,1400,1742,2000,2263`, `sweep canddpr default,held 1512 2,1.25,1.5,2.2 760 1110 1200,1742`, `sweep candw written 1280,1920 1 760 1110 1200,1742`; logs `1260-r3-sweep-cand.log`, `1260-r3-sweep-canddpr.log`, `1260-r3-sweep-candw.log`): **none.** No height scrolls and none passes the room, in any of the three seats, at any width or ratio; the written seat's foot stands at most 0.3125 px short. The foot stands short of the floor by at most 0.28125 px in the default seat (at 860) and 0.3125 px held (at 797), inside `ON` (0.5). Every value written is on the grid at every height (`sharesOffThe64thGrid=0`, against 228 and 347 of 356 before). The group stays exactly twice the row and the masthead exactly a row.

**Rejected, so the builder does not rediscover them:**

- **A grid derived from `devicePixelRatio`** (1/128 px at a ratio of 2). The harness lays out at 1/64 px at every ratio it emulates (measured above), so a 1/128 share there lies off the grid and brings the overrun back in the very run that checks it.
- **The remainder given to the paper** (the paper as the frame less natural less the floored shares). It keeps the foot exactly on the floor, but every held paper height moves by up to 0.33 px, the room-limited band gets a sliver of paper under the Hand, and SP3's `paper === 0` no longer holds. Leaving it at the foot moves fewer ruled numbers (C33).
- **Each share floored on its own** (`floor(2 × d)` for the group). It can make the group a 64th more than twice the row, which ruling 1's "twice" does not allow (`group-floored-alone` reds SP2).
- **The share floored before the closing factor** (`floor(d) × open`). Every frame of the first press's glide (P5) would write off the grid (`floor-before-open` reds SP10 at closed 0.37).
- **A margin of slack taken off the room** (for example one 64th per grown box). It moves the foot further from the floor than the floor does and still assumes the grid.
- **Re-measuring after the write and backing off a 64th while the body scrolls.** It forces a second layout on every room layout, it cannot run on a camera frame, which reads no layout, and in the harness, where the floor already holds, nothing could make it run, so no guard could prove it.
- **A unit check of the sum alone** (natural plus the shares at most the room). The unfloored sum is the room to within 1e-9 at all seven heights that scroll, so SP3's form of the check passes on the bug, and a strict "at most" passes at 870, 889, 908, 927 and 946 while it fails at 965 and 984 by a floating-point 1e-13, not by the layout's rounding (`node out/1260-r3-dry-run/1260-r3-sumcheck.ts` at 58787135, log `1260-r3-sumcheck-head.log`). The arithmetic cannot see the browser's rounding; what it can hold is the grid (SP10), and the browser holds the outcome (BR25b).
