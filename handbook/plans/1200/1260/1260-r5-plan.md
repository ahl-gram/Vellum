## The design (replaces nothing in revisions 1 to 4; adds the three waits)

**A wait for a room laid out at a new window departs on the slip's floor standing at that window's floor.** `placeSlip` in `src/site/shared/room-seats.ts` writes the slip's inline `top` and `max-height` so that their sum is the page's height less `SLIP_FLOOR`, and it runs early in the same synchronous `layout()` call whose last statement runs `onLayout`, which is `growBill`. So a floor at the new window's proves that whole call ran for this window: the bill's spacings, the sheet's size and the reserves included. The floor is read back from the inline strings, which carry six significant digits; a whole-pixel window and a whole-pixel `SLIP_FLOOR` leave a floor a whole pixel apart from the next window's, so half a pixel tells them apart (`FLOOR_NEAR` in `e2e/suites/broadside/kit.ts`, the dated line there). The departure is clause 5's: before the window changes the floor is the old window's, and only the room's layout moves it; it carries no check's claim (clause 6), since BR6 claims a tap and BR25 to BR25c claim the bill's foot, spacing and flower, none the floor.

- **BR6:** `NARROW` gains `floor` (null when the page has no slip); the wait adds `onSlipFloor(d.floor, d.ih)` to its departure. The label stays "BR6 the room laid out at 1024x700", which the unit test pins.
- **BR25's `reader`:** its departure becomes `laidOut(w, h, also)`, the same conjunction as before with `onSlipFloor(d.floor, h)` added. `BILL` already reads `floor`.

**A wait for a camera to land counts the camera's writes and asks for a frame between its reads.** `FRAMES_ON` already ticks once a frame; it now also counts every `style` record a `MutationObserver` sees on `#map`, the zoom controller's `targetEl` (`createGlass` in `src/site/explorer/glass.ts`), and `FRAMES_OFF` disconnects it. `moveAndRest` reads `LANDING` (the camera as `CAMERA` rounds it, the write count and the frame count) before the move and after it, departs on the camera having left its start, and takes two reads for rest only when `landed`: the camera equal, no write between, and at least one frame between. A runner that withholds frames gives two equal cameras with no frame between, which `landed` refuses; a last ease step below the camera's rounding is a write, which it refuses too (the environment bullet opening "A transform reads to six significant digits"). The departure is the one `moveAndRest` had.

**Rejected, so the builder does not rediscover them:**

- **A longer fixed pause, or a larger budget.** A sleep sized to one runner is the flake clause 3 forbids, and a budget does not change what two equal reads mean.
- **A departure on a frame having passed since the window changed**, for the resize reads. It leans on the browser dispatching `resize` before the next frame's callbacks, which no check here reads; the floor is the layout's own output.
- **`COUNT_RESIZES` (a resize counted after the room's listener, `e2e/suites/zoom/resets.ts`).** It needs a listener installed before each change, misses a layout the chrome's `ResizeObserver` runs, and proves a resize ran, not one for this window. The doctrine bullet does not name it.
- **A departure on the floor having left the read before the change**, for `reader`. Every sequence's first read follows a navigation at the same window, where the floor does not move; the target form holds there too.
- **The camera's raw values compared across a frame**, with no write count. Exact for a d3 glide, but it rests on every frame of every glide changing a float, which nothing pins; the write count is the doctrine's named shape.
- **Mirroring 22 in the e2e files**, instead of exporting `SLIP_FLOOR`. A copied number drifts from the one `placeSlip` reads.
- **Departing on the bill's `--bill-*` values** for BR6. They are BR25's claims, and the floor is the same for every room with a slip.
- **Pulling BR6 out of the run now, under an issue.** Alex's direction is the waits first; the waits are measured to clear the stale read, and the skip is the gated second branch.
