## The design

**A station moored at a town knows where the chart letters that town's name.** `Station` gains `readonly nameGap: number | null`: the distance, in chart px on the 1500 px sheet, from the station's anchor east to the left edge of its town's baked name in `src/static/charts/chart-42-antique.svg`; `null` for a station that stands on no town (the Gallery) and for How It Works. The four values are the renderer's own gaps for those tiers on a world sheet, read from the committed chart (`out/1254-r1-dry-run/1254-r1-gap-exact.log`, `node out/1254-r1-dry-run/1254-r1-gap-exact.ts`: every name is lettered once, `start` anchored, and its `x` less the anchor's `nx * 1500` is 11, 7, 8 and 7 within 6e-14): the Explorer 11 (the capital Laukuwelua), the Reading Room 7 (Lamahai), the Atlas 8 (the seat Weki), the Primer 7 (Poalo).

**The page carries it and the sheet steps the station.** Each pip with a gap carries `--name-gap:<gap>px` in its `style`, after its `left` and `top`. `.lf-station`'s transform gains one term after the counter-scale, so the step rides inside the division and is measured in screen pixels at every depth (`handbook/specs/explorer-doctrine.md`, the nudge bullet):

```css
transform: translate(-50%, -50%) scale(var(--inv, 1))
  translateX(min(0px, calc(var(--name-gap, 1000px) / var(--inv, 1) - 16px)));
```

`var(--name-gap) / var(--inv)` is the name's distance from the town's point on screen at the live scale; 16px is the diamond's reach (its half-diagonal: a 15px square with a 1.5px border and a 1px outline reaches 14.14px, drawn as 13.44px where the border rounds to 1px, as the harness draws it) and 2px of paper. Where the name is nearer than 16px the station (diamond, pulse and label slip together) steps west by the difference; where it is farther, `min(0px, ...)` leaves it on the point; a pip with no gap takes the 1000px fallback and never steps. No script changes: the camera already publishes `--inv` on the sheet every frame, and the step adds no per-frame write (`handbook/specs/explorer-doctrine.md`, "Never write an inherited custom property ... per frame"). `.lf-station` (0,1,0) is the only rule that sets the button's `transform` (`git grep -n "lf-station" -- src/styles`; `src/styles/shared/house.css` and `src/styles/shared/motion.css` exclude it from the house lift), and no transition runs on it, so L20's "no transition and no shift under a held press" holds.

### Alternatives rejected

- **A fixed step at every zoom** (Q1's answer B, not taken): clear at rest, but it touches the "P" again after the whole-sheet press and further out, and once zoomed in it shows the town's mark beside the diamond as a ring (the spike's `fixed-*` arms).

- **The Primer alone stepped, the three other town stations left as they are** (Q2's answers B and C, not taken): the three hide a letter of their town's name the same way on main.
- **The step computed in script** (a pure function that `src/site/home/app.ts`'s `apply` would call to write a per-pip step every frame): it would make the arithmetic unit-testable, but the per-frame wiring would stay as untested as the sheet's formula, and it adds a per-frame write where the sheet already has `--inv`; the sheet's formula is the boring form.
- **A smaller diamond, or the round at-sea glyph at Poalo**: the round still reaches 9.5 px against a name 7.35 px away at rest, the at-sea dress is earned from the terrain and Poalo is land (`test/site/landfall-prose.test.ts`, "every pip's at-sea dress is earned from the terrain"), and a smaller diamond changes the ruled station dress of every station.
- **Moving the Primer's anchor (`nx`, `ny`) off Poalo**: it would move the card's flight anchor and unclaim Poalo's dot (`stationSpots` keys on the anchor), and a step in chart units grows with the zoom, the opposite of what the name needs.
- **The station moored elsewhere**: not needed, since the fault is fixed at Poalo (M2 stands).
