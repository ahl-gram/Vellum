## The design

**P24 keeps its claim, waits for the pinned card to finish unrolling, and aims its hover and wheel at the centre of the card's name instead of the card's middle, asserting in the same check that the aim is clear of every card action.**

- **Aim**: the centre of `.pc-name`'s rect, read once the roll has finished. The name is the card's first line by construction (`fillCardInner` in `src/site/shared/living-chart/place-overlay-card.ts` puts the head, name first, before the actions row), and on this fixture it sits 62.7 px above the nearest action at rest (name 317.6 to 339.2, aim y 328, `.pc-prospect` top 390.7; `out/1211-dry-run/p24-scrub-parts.log.json`). Under the pinned card, which stays `pointer-events: none` there, the aim falls through to the chart at every moment of the roll (scrubbed 0 to 650 ms, step 25: `rect` at every one, same log's `scrubAtNameAim`). This is Gate 2 item 4: sample the thing's own rect, never a proxy coordinate; the card's middle was a proxy for "the card's body" and decayed the day PR #1193 put a press there.
- **The wait**: `makeStable(settle)` with `animated: CARD_CARRIER` (`"#place-card .pc-inner"`, `e2e/support/witness.ts`), so the read is taken only once `.pc-inner`'s animation list is non-empty and every entry is finished, and two consecutive reads are equal (settle-doctrine clause 3 and its unfurl bullet). The departure is `d.name === "Kralgov" && d.pinned`: the press that pins restarts the roll in the same `showPlaceCard` call that sets `.pinned` (`src/site/shared/living-chart/place-overlay.ts`, the `animation = "none"` reflow), so once `.pinned` is read the list holds the pinned roll and "finished" can only be its end. The wait makes the aim and the wheel independent of how fast the runner is, which is what hid this defect: the centre-aimed check passed on the Mac only because it wheeled mid-roll.
- **The witness**: `clear`, true when the aim lies outside the rect of every child of `.pc-acts`, is a conjunct of P24's own assertion (Gate 1 item 2, the precondition in the same check), so a later change that lays an action over the name fails P24 by name, with `aimClearOfActions: false` in its payload, instead of flaking.
- **No faces wait.** The issue proposed one. Every read at the wheel, 24 on CI and 2 on the Mac, found the faces loaded (6 of 6), and a face swap moves the name line a few pixels against a 62.7 px clearance (Calls made, 3).

### Alternatives rejected

- **The issue's fix (wait for the animations and the faces, aim at the middle)**: measured red on every run (above). The menu's option B; Alex ruled A (6605023971).
- **The middle, no wait, any sleep**: the outcome is the roll's phase at the wheel, which is the runner's speed: flaky by construction.
- **A product change, the press letting a wheel through**: the menu's option C, not ruled (Alex ruled A, "No site change"); a behaviour change to the card for a test, against the pattern every control over the chart keeps (the chart drawer's own stop list in `src/site/explorer/chart-drawer.ts`, the loupe's `STOPPED` in `src/site/explorer/loupe-bind.ts`).
- **Aiming at a link (`.pc-prospect`)**: a wheel there bubbles through `.pc-inner`'s scroll guard, so it would also hold the guard's no-tail branch, but the claim is about the card's body, and a link raises on hover (`transform` on `:hover`); rejected, and the gap is named under Calls made, 7.
- **Aiming at the prose below the actions (`.pc-tale`)**: it lies below the actions, farther from the roll's hinge (the card's top, `transform-origin: top center`), so the roll moves it most; the name sits at the hinge and moves least.
- **Searching the card for a clear point**: more code for the same claim; the name is clear by construction and the witness says when it stops being.
- **The witness as a throw before the wheel**: a throw names the step's timeout or message, not P24's payload; a conjunct keeps the failure on P24's own line with the aim and the camera beside it.
