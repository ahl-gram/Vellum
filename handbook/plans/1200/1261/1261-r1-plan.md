## The design of r1

**A wait for the sheet at rest after a fold or an unfold keeps its own departure. It also asks that `#sheet` list no animation, and it compares that count in its equal reads.**

- **Why the count.** A fold's slide is a Web Animations animation with no fill, so it leaves `getAnimations()` when it ends. A count of 0 therefore holds at rest and in the one frame before the slide exists. It holds neither in the stall nor mid-slide.
- **A wait that reads only the sheet afterwards needs nothing more** (Z13c and `toState`). If two reads fall in that one frame, they read the new fit, which is the sheet's true rest box, laid out and with the camera restored synchronously at the press.
- **A wait whose caller then counts out stillness on other pieces needs one more term** (SW7, SW2c, SB18, through `foldLanded`). It also waits for the slip's left to have moved more than half a pixel. The slip starts on the same frame as the slide, so this term cannot hold in the frame before the slide exists, nor in the stall.
- **The precedent is FS's own wait** (`pressRecorded` in `e2e/suites/slide-away/fold-slide.ts`, C21).

**Rejected:**

- **Departing on the sheet's width in Z13c.** That is Z13c's own claim (`z13d.sheet > before.sheet`), and settle-doctrine clause 6 forbids a predicate that carries it.
- **`animated: "#sheet"` in `makeStable`.** It asks for a non-empty list with every entry finished. The slide leaves the list when it ends, so the wait would time out (clause 3).
- **Putting the count into the shared `still` in `e2e/suites/slide-away/kit.ts`, or into `rest` in `e2e/suites/stage/stage.ts`.** Both serve every room and both arms of motion, and the stage suite's EA checks walk every page. A call-site helper touches only the waits that read the sheet after a fold.
- **Avoiding the stall in the product** (C24).
