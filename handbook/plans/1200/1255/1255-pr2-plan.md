## The design

**Data.** Each of the 24 steps carries `picture`: the address, in the shared grammar the rooms read (`readWorldKeys`, `readDressKeys` in `src/site/shared/address-grammar.ts`, `readWritten` in `src/site/shared/written-address.ts`), of the chart once the step is done, its path the room the step opens, naming its style, legend, arms and beasts explicitly so no default is guessed. The 24 values are the rooms' own addresses once each step was done (the after probe), the Writing Room's sentence written as the room writes it (`writtenHash`) and its plate as `theme=vegetation`.

**The build.** A new last step of `astro:generate`, after the Primer's steps file, `node scripts/primer-pictures/main.ts`, runs in every `npm run build` and every `npm run astro:dev`: the deploy, the four e2e lanes, the proof runner and local builds. It finds a browser (`findBrowser`); with none, it fails naming the missing browser where `VELLUM_REQUIRE_BROWSER` is set and otherwise skips with a warning. For each pictured step it parses `picture` into a job, founds a written sentence with the committed name index, builds the world the one way (`defaultRecipe`, `generateWorld`), draws it with `renderMap` at the Explorer's width (1500) and the address's dress (a job drawn once however many steps share it), checks the drawn root size is the frame's, and presses every chart through one headless browser over the DevTools protocol into `generated/primer/pictures/<step-id>.webp`, 432 px wide at WebP quality 70. It writes `generated/primer/pictures.json`, each pictured step's file and its chart's signature (stamp, label, layer ids), and logs `pictures: <n> pressed with <browser path> (<product>), <bytes> bytes in all`, which names the publishing machine's browser and the pictures' weight in every deploy log.

**The page.** Each pictured step's `.pic` holds `<img src="pictures/<id>.webp" width="1500" height="1158" alt="" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">`; every other step keeps `<span class="pic none"></span>`. Eager, low priority, decorative, its frame reserved by the chart's own root size. It wears the Gallery's plate dress, a tan hairline and the sheet shadow (Q3, ruled).

**The guards.** The unit suite holds the address half (an address gives the world and dress the grammar reads, a sentence founds its world, a dress left to a default is refused, every picture on the ladders makes a job, the frame is the drawn root size), the signature reader, the generator's browser policy and its manifest and log line, the workflows' variables, the page's markup, and that a pictured step is never skipped as held by another. The browser holds the room half: the existing drift-check walk, once a pictured step's `do` is done, reads the room's own chart's signature and holds it against the manifest's, so a step whose address, acts or picture move apart reds by name. A press that writes no file reds IX7 (`e2e/suites/document-rooms.ts`, no 4xx on the Primer), since the page loads every picture eagerly (measured: 24 of 24 `complete` with one room open).

**Rejected, with the reason:**
- **Deriving the picture from the step's acts** (`set("#land", "300")` read as `land=300`, `choose("#theme", "rainfall")` as `theme=moisture`): a second copy of every room's controls (option words to values, the touched gates, `gateLand`, held rows under an outline, the founding), which drifts silently where only the room could say. The written address plus the browser check holds the same claim with no copy.
- **A unit test "picture recipe equals the address with `do` applied"** (the body's): no unit test can know what a room's control does to its chart; the claim is the room's, so it is read in the room (`handbook/specs/check-placement.md`, a claim goes to a unit test wherever one can hold it alone).
- **`rasterizeSvg` in `src/cli/raster.ts`**: its `--screenshot` path ignored a scale below one half (750x579 at both 0.144 and 0.288) and has no quality, so it cannot make a 432 px picture of about 13 KB.
- **`start` in `e2e/harness.ts` for the press**: it carries module state, its own site server and the fixed day; a production build step takes a small press of its own.
- **Pictures as committed files**: they would be the only raster beside the committed list and go stale at every regen; ruling 8 rules them made at each deploy.
- **Lazy loading**: the page's print sheet unfolds every room, and a lazy picture in a room never opened prints blank (`handbook/specs/ui-design.md`, Print). The 24 weigh 303,746 bytes in all; `fetchpriority="low"` keeps a clicked navigation ahead of them (rulebook, Heavy lazy plates).
- **Drawing each picture small (`widthPx` 432)**: a different label layout from the room's own chart, so the picture would not be the chart once done; the room's 1500 drawing is pressed down.
- **A step after the build** in the deploy and the lanes: two more workflow edits and a second home for the same work; inside `astro:generate` it runs wherever the site is built, and the unit suite's builds skip it anyway.
