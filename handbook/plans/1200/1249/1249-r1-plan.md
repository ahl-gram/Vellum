## The design

### Ruling 10 reaches these words

- **Its words** (Issue #1155, https://github.com/ahl-gram/Vellum/issues/1155#issuecomment-6663627921): "A browser check, plus the words each room already exports (Recommended)".
- **The option as the round put it to Alex** (`design/the-primer/notes.md`, line 144): "plus importing the few words a room already keeps in one place (the held notes, the journal's road press, the pace) instead of retyping them".
- **Issue #1249's body**: "Exported words, never retyped (ruling 10)", whose list already counts a number array as exported words (`PACES`, from which "twice or four times" is derived), and already reaches past the folder of the room a word belongs to (`ROAD_TAKE` lives in `src/site/shared/living-chart/`, `PLATE_STEPS` in the Portfolio's `how-plate.ts`).

So a function a room exports, which makes a word from a value, is how that room keeps that word in one place, and deriving through it is the opposite of retyping; and "each room" does not mean "the step's own room". The list in the body and the plan's call 12 are instances, not the bound: call 12 itself took `LAY_ON_CARD`, which the body does not list. This needs no decision the ruling left open, so it is call 17 and not a menu.

- **A one-item table written out as an object literal, for `tabLine` and `countLine`.** Rejected: a second spelling of a table item, where the house builds items through `src/site/shared/table-address.ts`, and one that would not follow the `LAID` address; it would save about 3.8 KB minified.

- **A unit test that each derived word equals its export.** Rejected: it would assert the data against itself (Tests).
- **Export more words from the rooms.** Rejected, as the plan's call 13.
