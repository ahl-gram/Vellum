## The design

As ruled (Q1, Q2 and Q3 each ruled A, comment 7055649407): every one of the seven copies is shared, by Issue #1218's pattern, and no test file changes. The drawing module exports the seat or the extent; the keep and the drawing both use it. Every plate stays byte-identical.

| copy | its home after the share | how |
|---|---|---|
| continuation line | `continuationLabel(strip, isLast): Lettering \| null` in `src/itinerary/dress/road.ts` | `continuation()` in `strip.ts` draws it, `captionKeep` boxes it |
| crossing box | `crossingMarkBox(p): Box` in `src/itinerary/dress/glyphs.ts`, beside `bridgeMark` and `fordMark` | the literal moves beside the marks it measures |
| houses | `clusterSeat(p, kind): ClusterSeat` in `glyphs.ts`, holding the seat `p.sy - 1`, the farmstead and mill to hamlet mapping, and `CLUSTER_BOX` (moved, private) | `eventArt` draws at `s.x, s.y, s.tier`; `eventKeep` keeps `s.box` |
| compass | `compassDiscs(painted, x, y, needleDeg): SeatDisc[]` in `src/itinerary/dress/compass.ts`; `FLEUR_REACH = 20.5` shared with `fleur` | the fleur's reach is shared; the rose and mark radii (15.5, 14, 17, 5, 4.5) move beside the drawing |
| league radii | `leagueMarkReach(l, painted): number` in `road.ts`, from `LEAGUE_DOT_R`, `LEAGUE_DISC_R`, `WIDE_LEAGUE_RX` and `WIDE_FROM`, which `leagueDots` and `leagueNumerals` draw with | the keep adds its own 0.5 |
| side-road stub | `sideRoadStub(p, side): SideRoadStub` in `road.ts` | `eventArt` draws `M p Q bend end`, `eventKeep` keeps `[p, bend, end]`, `captionSeat` seats at `end` |
| `layCaption`'s clamp | `room.top` (the `SeatRoom` `layCaption` is already handed) | no export |

`FifthNumeral` is renamed `Lettering` and shared by `fifthNumeral` and `continuationLabel` (the same shape; nothing outside `road.ts` names it).

Why each value is byte-identical: every shared expression is the same arithmetic in the same order as the copy it replaces (`(p.sy - 1) + k.y` for `p.sy - 1 + k.y`, `p.sx + side * 12` and the rest unchanged); `leagueMarkReach(l, painted) + 0.5` gives exactly 5.6, 4.1 and 1.9 (`5.1 + 0.5 === 5.6`, `3.6 + 0.5 === 4.1`, `1.4 + 0.5 === 1.9`, each `true` in Node); `room.top` is `strip.y0 + 22` on both dresses. Measured, not argued: the Probe line's byte comparisons.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- A share homed in `strip.ts` (where the continuation line and the stub are drawn): `strip.ts` imports `captions.ts`, so `captions.ts` importing `strip.ts` is a cycle. `road.ts` imports neither and already holds the league lettering's seat (`fifthNumeral`), so the continuation label and the stub, a piece of road, go there.
- The house, crossing and compass extents derived from the drawing (path extents, stroke halves): the painted capital draws one more house than the plain one, the crossing mark is drawn turned along the road while its box is level, and the rose's margins are 0.5 and 0.6 over the drawn rims. Each derivation changes the keep and moves plates. The literal moves beside the drawing instead, as Issue #1218 did with the summit box.
- The rose radius derived from the rim (`15 + 0.5`, `13.4 + 0.6`, both exact): it reads as an arbitrary pair of margins, and a rim drawn wider already reds the caption test's plate read ("premise: every strip draws one rose") before and after this change.
- The ink "N"'s reach (17) shared with its letter (`y: -14.6`): no byte-identical derivation exists, so 17 stays a literal in `compassDiscs`, beside the letter.
- Dropping the continuation box from the keep, since it holds nothing today: `handbook/specs/chart-dress.md` names the continuation line among what the border line gives way to, and a line drawn lower would then take captions silently (12 lines in each dress in the trial).
- The test importing `CLUSTER_BOX`, `ROSE_R` or the ornament's reach: see Calls made.
