## The design

### One step of the check per ladder step

`walkLadder` (in `e2e/support/primer-walk.ts`) walks the ladder's steps in order, one `makeStep` group per step, named by the step's own id (`explorer-sea-level`). Each step:

1. **Clears the device** on the page before it: `localStorage.clear()` and `sessionStorage.clear()`, and throws unless that page is on the site's origin (ruling 6, "Every step stands alone", and Sub 3 call 16).
2. **Opens the step's address** through `bounce` (settle-doctrine clause 9), and waits for the room's boot token. A room that throws while it boots (an `EXCEPTION` in the console accumulator since the bounce, read through `dropExpectedCancellations`) fails the step at once, by assertion: measured, deleting the loupe press makes the Explorer throw at boot (`no element #zoom-loupe on this page`), and without this the suite spent 37 x 15.5 s, 9.5 minutes, in stops (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-m-loupe.log`, the prototype's first form); with it, 3.6 s (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-d1-m-loupe.log`).
3. **Waits for the room at rest** (below), then does the step's `first` acts, each followed by its own rest.
4. **Reads every control the step names**, `words` and then `relies`, the same way (ruling A1): present, in the state the step expects, and worded as quoted. One check: `<id> names every control as it stands, ...`.
5. **Reads the address**: every key the step's address carried is still in the page's hash (keys only; a value the room rewrites, `k=1.9600` to `k=1.96`, is kept). One check: `<id> keeps every key of its address`.
6. **Where the step writes a `then`**, and only when steps 4 and 5 passed and the step is on this arm, does the `do` acts and reads the `then` entries. One check: `<id> shows what it says once the step is done`. A step whose controls or address failed is not done, and the log says so; the step is red already, and doing it would turn a clean red into a stop, which the proof runner reads as NEEDS READ (`mutationCheck` in `scripts/proof-runner/ledger.ts`). Measured: with the acts done regardless, the archipelago mutation turned four steps into stops (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-m-arch.log`, the prototype's first form); gated, every red is an assertion (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-d1-m-arch.log`).

The suite ends with one health check, `PE0`, over the console and the network (`scopedHealth` in `e2e/support/room.ts`), as every room suite does.

Every check name begins with the step id, because the proof runner takes the first word of a `FAIL` line as the red's id (`e2eOutcome` in `scripts/proof-runner/outcome.ts`) and `E2E_SKIPS` names a step by its `makeStep` name.

### What each read expects

`expectFor` in the judge: a control in `words` or `relies` must be **reachable**, unless the data gives a `state` (`held`, `hidden`), which it must then be; a step marked `only: "touch"` is held **hidden** on the desk; a step marked `only: "motion"` is held **hidden** under reduced motion, which is the only motion this sub runs; an outcome in `then` is read for its words and, where the data gives a state, that state, and for its reach not at all. Measured, at 1280 by 800, outcomes the step's sentence names that a reader cannot press, rightly: the folio title under the stage, a card's name under its own frame, the chart under its place marks, and, at the zoom the step leaves, the caption off screen (`explorer-hold`) and the Drawer's tab hidden (`explorer-dog-ear`) (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-real-1280.json`); the round read outcomes for their words alone too (`design/the-primer/mock/landings.ts`).

**Reachable** is: present; not under `[hidden]`, `display: none` or `visibility: hidden`; a box of some size; not in a held box (`.held`); not disabled; and, once scrolled to the centre, the element at its press point is the control, inside it, or its label (Gate 2 item 2). **The press point** is the box's centre, or the mean of the corners of a polygon `clip-path`: the turned-up corner is clipped to a triangle (`polygon(100% 0px, 0px 0px, 100% 100%)`), its box's centre sits on the cut edge, and at 1024 by 768 it fell outside, onto the chart (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-cover.log`: `"top":"rect.","self":false` at the centre, `"self":true` at (0.72, 0.28)). The same point is where a `click` act presses, unless the act names its own `at`.

### The rest after an act

`rest` (in `primerKit`) is a `makeStable` wait on the room's own rest read: the step departs when the page's DOM has changed since the act (a `MutationObserver` count, `window.__primer.marks`, installed at arrival) and the room is quiet, and rests at two equal reads with a frame between them. For the Explorer, quiet is: `#verso-turn` not disabled (the draw lifecycle's own control), `#map > svg` present, no camera settle pending, no region being drafted (`#map .survey-pencil`), and no animation running that is not endless. Each is measured:

- **Not the status line.** Laying a survey on the table writes `#status` ("The Environs of Nanawotani lies on the table · one sheet laid · room for five more") and clears it 8.4 s later (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-dogear.json`); a rest keyed on an empty status line spent those 8.4 s on every lay. `waitSettled` keys on it and would too.
- **The camera's settle.** The Explorer writes the camera into its address 250 ms after a glide's last frame (`onCameraSettle` in `src/site/explorer/glass.ts`, `settleMs` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`), and nothing on the page shows that gap: without a read of it, four steps read their address in the gap and lost `cx`, `cy` and `k` (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-nohook.log`), and the repeat act overshot to the deepest band, 11 presses where 2 do. So the Explorer publishes `window.__vellumCameraSettling` (two lines, call 5), which the rest reads.
- **Animations.** A rest read off DOM changes alone handed over the Drawer mid-slide once in four runs (`#table-road` "off screen", `out/1250-dry-run/1250-m-held.log`, the prototype's first form); asking that no animation be running closed it (every run since). With motion on, the endless fog (`fog a`, `fog b`) never finishes, so endless animations are left out (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-motion-1280.log`, 37 of 37).

A `set` on a box (not a slider) changes nothing on the page until the next act, so it takes no rest; a `wait` and a `repeat` wait on their own element (15 s, the budget `REDRAFT_TRIES` in `e2e/suites/zoom/kit.ts` gives a redraft) and then rest without a departure.

### The acts (ruling D: a slider's or a list's value set in the page)

- `click`: `sendClick` at the press point (above), or at the act's `at`.
- `set` on a slider, and `choose` on a list: the value set in the page and the page told it changed, `input` then `change` (only `input` where the act says `release: false`), as 82 places in today's checks do (ruling D, "Set values from inside the page"). Before it sets the value, the driver reads the slider or list where it stands and refuses one no reader could reach (call 24): ruling D chose the page's own call for how the value is set, and this keeps the one thing a reader's own hand would have shown, that the control is there to be used. Every slider and list the Explorer's ladder sets stood reachable (the fixture runs above: no act refused).
- `set` on a box: focused in the page and its own words selected, then `Input.insertText` (the shape of `pickTexas` in `e2e/suites/broadside/grow.ts`): real input, outside ruling D's reach.
- `key`: the control focused in the page and the named key sent (`Enter` with `text: "\r"`, settle-doctrine environment bullet on Enter).
- `pick`: the box focused, the words typed with `Input.insertText`, the room's list waited on until its first entry is the name, `Enter`.
- `repeat`: press until its element comes, at most the presses that carry the whole sheet to the deepest band (`Math.ceil(Math.log(DEEPEST_K) / Math.log(ZOOM_STEP))`, 11, both imported).
- `wait`: until its element comes.

Every act that names a value checks it landed (a slider at the act's value, a list showing the act's option), and throws otherwise: a check whose act silently missed reads the room as it was and can pass, as the prototype's first drag of sea level did (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-d-drag.log`: no change made, outcome read "34% land", passed). Measured under D1 with the data's shipped value: `explorer-sea-level do set #land: #land landed at 100, not the 30 the act names` (`out/1250-dry-run/1250-d1-as-shipped.log`), which is what call 10 corrects.

// The Primer check's judgement, apart from the page (Issue #1250): what a step expects of each control it names, what the page's facts say it reads, and where a reader's press lands.
import type { Spot, Step, Words } from "../../src/site/shared/primer/shape.ts";

export type Arm = "desk" | "tablet";
export type Expect = "reachable" | "held" | "hidden" | "any";
export type Reach =
  "absent" | "hidden" | "no box" | "held" | "disabled" | "off screen" | "reachable" | `covered by ${string}`;
export type Box = { readonly x: number; readonly y: number; readonly w: number; readonly h: number };
export type Point = { readonly x: number; readonly y: number };

export type Facts = {
  readonly found: boolean;
  readonly shown: boolean;
  readonly box: Box | null;
  readonly clip: string;
  readonly held: boolean;
  readonly disabled: boolean;
  readonly text: string;
  readonly aria: string | null;
  readonly title: string | null;
  readonly placeholder: string | null;
  readonly attr: string | null;
  readonly label: string;
  readonly road: readonly [string, string];
  readonly options: readonly { readonly text: string; readonly disabled: boolean }[];
};

export const norm = (s: string | null): string => (s ?? "").replace(/\s+/g, " ").trim();

export function wordsOf(f: Facts, w: Words): string | null {
  if (!f.found) return null;
  switch (w.read) {
    case "text":
      return norm(f.text);
    case "aria":
      return norm(f.aria);
    case "title":
      return norm(f.title);
    case "placeholder":
      return f.placeholder;
    case "attr":
      return f.attr;
    case "label":
      return norm(f.label);
    case "road":
      return `${norm(f.road[0])} ${norm(f.road[1])}`;
    case "option": {
      const o = f.options.find((x) => norm(x.text) === w.says);
      return o === undefined ? "no such option" : o.disabled ? `${w.says} (disabled)` : w.says;
    }
  }
}

export function wordsHold(got: string | null, w: Words): boolean {
  if (got === null) return false;
  return w.match === "starts" ? got.startsWith(w.says) : w.match === "contains" ? got.includes(w.says) : got === w.says;
}

export function expectFor(w: Words, step: Step, arm: Arm, outcome: boolean): Expect {
  if (step.only === "touch" && arm !== "tablet") return "hidden";
  if (step.only === "motion") return "hidden";
  if (w.state !== undefined) return w.state;
  return outcome ? "any" : "reachable";
}

export function onItsArm(step: Step, arm: Arm): boolean {
  return step.only === undefined ? arm === "desk" : step.only === "touch" && arm === "tablet";
}

export function stateOf(f: Facts, hit: string): Reach {
  if (!f.found) return "absent";
  if (!f.shown) return "hidden";
  if (f.box === null || f.box.w === 0 || f.box.h === 0) return "no box";
  if (f.held) return "held";
  if (f.disabled) return "disabled";
  if (hit === "off screen") return "off screen";
  return hit === "self" || hit === "label" ? "reachable" : `covered by ${hit}`;
}

export function fits(state: Reach, expect: Expect): boolean {
  if (expect === "any") return state !== "absent";
  if (expect === "hidden") return state === "hidden" || state === "no box";
  return state === expect;
}

const corner = (token: string, length: number): number =>
  token.endsWith("%") ? (parseFloat(token) / 100) * length : parseFloat(token);

export function pressPoint(box: Box, clip: string, at?: Spot): Point {
  if (at !== undefined) return { x: box.x + box.w * at.x, y: box.y + box.h * at.y };
  const m = /^polygon\((.*)\)$/.exec(clip.trim());
  if (m === null) return { x: box.x + box.w / 2, y: box.y + box.h / 2 };
  const corners = (m[1] ?? "").split(",").map((p) => p.trim().split(/\s+/));
  const mean = (i: 0 | 1, length: number): number =>
    corners.reduce((sum, c) => sum + corner(c[i] ?? "0", length), 0) / corners.length;
  return { x: box.x + mean(0, box.w), y: box.y + mean(1, box.h) };
}

const keysOfHash = (address: string): readonly string[] => {
  const at = address.indexOf("#");
  return at === -1 ? [] : [...new URLSearchParams(address.slice(at + 1)).keys()];
};

export function keysLost(at: string, hash: string): readonly string[] {
  const kept = new Set(keysOfHash(hash.startsWith("#") ? hash : `#${hash}`));
  return keysOfHash(at).filter((k) => !kept.has(k));
}

export type NamedKey = { readonly key: string; readonly code: string; readonly vk: number; readonly text: string };

const KEYS: Readonly<Record<string, NamedKey>> = {

import type { Act } from "../../src/site/shared/primer/shape.ts";

const press = async (k: PrimerKit, act: Extract<Act, { kind: "click" }>): Promise<void> => {
  const aim = await k.evaluate(AIM(act.css, act.nth ?? 0));
  if (aim === null || aim.box.w === 0 || aim.box.h === 0) throw new Error(`${act.css} has no box to press`);

};

  const f = await k.evaluate(FIELD(act.css));
  if (f === null) throw new Error(`${label}: no ${act.css} to set`);
  if (f.type === "range") {

      await reachable(k, act.css, label);
      const before = await k.evaluate(k.room().rest);
      await k.evaluate(SET_VALUE(act.css, act.value, act.release !== false));
      await k.rest(before, label);

    if (!(await k.evaluate(FOCUS(act.css, true)))) throw new Error(`${label}: ${act.css} takes no focus`);
    await k.send("Input.insertText", { text: act.value });

    throw new Error(`${label}: ${act.css} landed at ${String(landed)}, not the ${act.value} the act names`);

  switch (act.kind) {
    case "click": {
      const before = await k.evaluate(k.room().rest);
      await press(k, act);
      await k.rest(before, at);
      return;
    }
    case "set":
      return set(k, act, at);
    case "choose":
      return choose(k, act, at);
    case "key": {

      if (!(await k.evaluate(FOCUS(act.css, false)))) throw new Error(`${at}: ${act.css} takes no focus`);
      const c = keyOf(act.key);
      await sendKey(k.send, c.key, c.code, c.vk, c.text === "" ? {} : { text: c.text });
      await k.rest(before, at);
      return;
    }
    case "pick": {
      const first = k.room().pickFirst;
      if (first === null) throw new Error(`${at}: this room names no search list to pick from`);

      if (!(await k.evaluate(FOCUS(act.css, true)))) throw new Error(`${at}: ${act.css} takes no focus`);
      await k.send("Input.insertText", { text: act.text });
      await k.settle(FIRST_ENTRY(first), (d) => d.name === act.name, `${at}: the list offers ${act.name}`);
      const enter = keyOf("Enter");
      await sendKey(k.send, enter.key, enter.code, enter.vk);
      await k.rest(before, at);
      return;
    }
    case "repeat": {
      for (let i = 0; i < k.room().repeatCap && !(await k.evaluate(PRESENT(act.until))).here; i++) {

        await press(k, { kind: "click", css: act.css });

      await until(k, act.until, at);
      await k.rest(null, at);
      return;
    }
    case "wait":
      await until(k, act.css, at);
      await k.rest(null, at);
      return;
  }
}

import type { Ladder, Step, Words } from "../../src/site/shared/primer/shape.ts";
import type { SuiteContext } from "../types.ts";

const TRIES = 300;
const DESK = { width: 1280, height: 800, deviceScaleFactor: 1, mobile: false } as const;
const TABLET = { width: 1024, height: 768 } as const;
const NAMES = "names every control as it stands, worded as quoted and reachable, held or hidden as the step says";
const KEPT = "keeps every key of its address";
const SHOWS = "shows what it says once the step is done";

export type Read = {
  readonly css: string;
  readonly says: string;
  readonly got: string | null;
  readonly state: Reach;
  readonly expect: Expect;
  readonly ok: boolean;
};

export function primerKit(ctx: SuiteContext, route: string) {
  const settle = makeSettle(ctx);
  const booting = makeSettle({ ...ctx, tolerant: true });
  const stable = makeStable(settle);
  const room = (): PrimerRoom => {
    const r = PRIMER_ROOMS[route];
    if (r === undefined) throw new Error(`no PRIMER_ROOMS entry opens ${route}`);
    return r;
  };
  const rest = (before: Rest | null, label: string): Promise<Rest> =>
    stable(room().rest, `${label}: the room comes to rest`, {
      departed: (d) => (before === null || d.marks > before.marks) && d.quiet,
      same: (d, last) =>
        d.marks === last.marks && d.view === last.view && d.quiet === last.quiet && d.frames > last.frames,
      tries: TRIES,
    });
  const bare = async (label: string): Promise<void> => {
    const origin = await ctx.evaluate(BARE);
    if (origin !== `http://127.0.0.1:${ctx.PORT}`)
      throw new Error(`${label}: the page before it is ${origin}, so the device could not be cleared`);
  };
  const { consoleErrors } = ctx;
  const arrive = async (address: string, label: string): Promise<string | null> => {
    const base = consoleErrors.length;
    let threw: string | undefined;
    await bounce(ctx.send, `http://127.0.0.1:${ctx.PORT}${address}`);
    await booting(
      room().booted,
      (d) => {
        threw = dropExpectedCancellations(consoleErrors.slice(base)).find((e) => e.startsWith("EXCEPTION"));
        return d.up || threw !== undefined;
      },
      `${label}: the room boots at ${address}`,
      TRIES,
    );
    if (threw !== undefined) return threw;
    await ctx.evaluate(ARM);
    await rest(null, `${label} on arrival`);
    return null;
  };
  const read = async (w: Words): Promise<{ facts: Facts; state: Reach }> => {
    const f = await ctx.evaluate(FACTS(w));
    const p = f.box === null ? null : pressPoint(f.box, f.clip);
    const hit = p === null ? "nothing" : await ctx.evaluate(HIT(w.css, p.x, p.y));
    return { facts: f, state: stateOf(f, hit) };
  };
  const reachOf = async (css: string): Promise<Reach> => (await read({ css, says: "", read: "text" })).state;
  const readAll = async (words: readonly Words[], expect: (w: Words) => Expect): Promise<readonly Read[]> => {
    const reads: Read[] = [];
    for (const w of words) {
      const { facts, state } = await read(w);
      const got = wordsOf(facts, w);
      const e = expect(w);
      reads.push({ css: w.css, says: w.says, got, state, expect: e, ok: wordsHold(got, w) && fits(state, e) });
    }
    return reads;
  };
  return { ...ctx, settle, room, rest, bare, arrive, reachOf, readAll, waitTries: TRIES };
}

export type PrimerKit = ReturnType<typeof primerKit>;

const told = (reads: readonly Read[]): string => {
  const bad = reads.filter((r) => !r.ok);
  if (bad.length === 0) return `all ${reads.length} as quoted`;
  return bad
    .map(
      (r) =>
        `${r.css} read ${JSON.stringify(r.got)}, ${r.state}; the step quotes ${JSON.stringify(r.says)}, ${r.expect}`,
    )
    .join("; ");
};

export async function walkStep(k: PrimerKit, step: Step, arm: Arm): Promise<void> {
  const name = arm === "tablet" ? `${step.id} on a tablet` : step.id;
  await k.bare(name);
  const threw = await k.arrive(step.at, name);
  if (threw !== null) {
    k.check(`${name} ${NAMES}`, false, `the room threw while it opened at ${step.at}: ${threw.slice(0, 300)}`);
    return;
  }
  for (const a of step.first ?? []) await perform(k, a, `${name} first`);
  const named = await k.readAll([...step.words, ...(step.relies ?? [])], (w) => expectFor(w, step, arm, false));
  k.check(
    `${name} ${NAMES}`,
    named.every((r) => r.ok),
    told(named),
  );
  const lost = keysLost(step.at, await k.evaluate(HASH));
  k.check(`${name} ${KEPT}`, lost.length === 0, lost.length === 0 ? step.at : `lost ${lost.join(", ")} of ${step.at}`);
  if (step.then === undefined) return;
  if (!named.every((r) => r.ok) || lost.length > 0 || !onItsArm(step, arm)) {
    console.log(
      `  ${name}: not done, ${onItsArm(step, arm) ? "since what it names is not as it says" : "since it is not this arm's step"}`,
    );
    return;
  }
  for (const a of step.do ?? []) await perform(k, a, `${name} do`);
  const shown = await k.readAll(step.then, (w) => expectFor(w, step, arm, true));
  k.check(
    `${name} ${SHOWS}`,
    shown.every((r) => r.ok),
    told(shown),
  );
}

export async function walkLadder(ctx: SuiteContext, ladder: Ladder, health: string): Promise<void> {
  const step = makeStep(ctx);
  const gate = scopedHealth(ctx);
  const k = primerKit(ctx, ladder.route);
  const steps = ladder.lessons.flatMap((l) => l.steps);
  await ctx.send("Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride", DESK);
  await withReducedMotion(ctx, async () => {
    for (const s of steps) await step(s.id, () => walkStep(k, s, "desk"));
    await ctx.setMobileViewport(TABLET.width, TABLET.height);
    for (const s of steps.filter((x) => x.only === "touch"))
      await step(`${s.id} on a tablet`, () => walkStep(k, s, "tablet"));
    await ctx.clearMobile();
  });
  gate.check(health);
}
