The ladder's form for "a step with an act and no outcome" is `do` with no `then`, which `sealWhen` in `src/site/shared/primer/hand-steps.ts` seals on use (`step.do.length > 0 && step.then === null`), watching what `useWatches` derives from the act: `[{ event: "click", css: "#map .region-inset .dog-ear" }]`, the watch `explorer-portfolio-turn-down` already has.
