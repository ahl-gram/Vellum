## The design

**One builder.** A new `e2e/support/fonts.ts` exports `withFonts(read)`, which wraps any measuring read in one evaluate that runs the read first and reads `document.fonts.status` after it: the read's own layout asks for every face its text needs, so the status it reports includes them. Each of the six waits polls `withFonts(<its measuring read>)` until that status says `loaded` (and, where the wait already asked more, that too), and uses the read that passed. No wait reads the status on its own any more.

**The six waits.**
1. **RH33, `pressRead` in `e2e/suites/runninghead/press.ts`** (Q1): a tolerant `makeSettle` on `withFonts(PRESS_READ)` until its `fonts` say `loaded`, 200 tries; PRESS_READ no longer reads the status itself. On a timeout it hands back a read marked `never loaded: settle timeout rh33-press <route>: <last read>`, which `pressSpacingFaults` already reports, since `pressRead` runs in the sweep's loop outside every step (`vellum/e2e-throw-inside-step` reads a `try` whose `catch` does not throw as holding it).
2. **P19, `sweepAt` in `e2e/suites/cards/kit.ts`**: its own loop of bare status reads goes; the kit's `settle` polls `withFonts(SWEEP)` until the sweep's status says `loaded` or the sweep found no chart box, 100 tries, and the sweep that passed is the one judged. Probe W2: today the sweep's first pass ends with a face loading on every run.
3. **The hold kit, `boot` in `e2e/suites/cards/hold.ts`** (P28 to P33, P30): the settle on the bare status goes; the kit's `settle` polls `withFonts(MARKS)` until its status says `loaded` and `boot` returns that read's marks.
4. **SB20, `slipInk` in `e2e/suites/specimen/slip-ink.ts`**: each element's lines are read through `slipInk`'s own `settle` on `withFonts(LINES_OF(...))` until its status says `loaded`; `FONTS_LOADED` and the settle on it in `sb20SlipInk` go.
5. **The corners, `restAt` and `load` in `e2e/suites/corners.ts`** (CO1, CO2): `restAt`'s frame read becomes `withFonts("read()")`, and a frame counts as ready only when its status also says `loaded`, so two equal ready frames are two whose own layout found the faces in; `load` drops its bare status conjunct.
6. **PR40, `STAGED` in `e2e/suites/print-room/runner-layout.ts`**: STAGED no longer reads the status (it read it before the select heights); `pr40RunnerLayout`'s settle polls `withFonts(STAGED)`, its predicate the same path and `readyState` plus that read's status `loaded`, and judges the read that passed.

**Why a readiness wait and not a poll to rest** (RH33, and the same for the others' changed predicate): nothing in the head cluster or the room folio moves after the visit; only the faces arrive, and the predicate waits for exactly that. Measured: the read at once and a read taken after the room's own drawing, more than a second later, never differed by a box in 216 runner reads (probe B). The shape is `pr40RunnerLayout`'s own: a `makeSettle` readiness whose passing read is judged. The corners keep their own two-equal-frames rest, with the faces added to it.

Rejected, with the reason, so none is rediscovered mid-build:
- **The read after each room's own drawing (`PRIMER_ROOMS[route].booted` beside PRESS_READ, through `makeStable`, two reads alike).** The doctrine's cleanest poll to rest, the boot an honest departure (false on the room before in all 216). It costs about 8 s a lane B run on the runner (8.5 s summed in probe B, 8.0 in probe V) for readings probe B found identical. Alex's Q1 ruled the read at once.
- **`makeStable` with the faces as the departure.** `fonts === "loaded"` already holds on the room before the visit, a departure in name only (clause 5); and a third `STILL_ONLY` reason is a ruling.
- **`await document.fonts.ready` in the page (`evaluate(..., true)`).** It leans on the browser's `ready` getter laying the page out first, which no reading here pins, and a promise that never settles would hold the evaluate past every budget the harness keeps.
- **Each wait writing its own status read into its payload, as PRESS_READ did.** Six copies of one order, held by nothing; `withFonts` keeps the order in one place, held by T0.
- **A layout-forcing status read before the measurement (`document.body.getBoundingClientRect(); document.fonts.status`) in place of `withFonts`.** It shuts the window only up to the measuring read; P19's sweep asks for its face inside the measuring read itself (probe W2), which only a status read after it sees.
- **The press reads moved into RH33's step.** It would visit the nine rooms twice; nothing the step needs is gained.
- **A deliberate late face written into a page inside a check (Gate 2 item 14).** T0 to T7 hold the waits' logic; probes T1 and W stage the race once, at planning.
- **Changing the rooms** (Q1's third option, not ruled): staged on the runner it cleared the misreads seen (probe P), but it covers the status lines and not the class.
