## The design

Two new shared sheets, each linked by the pages that wear what it dresses, **last of the page's shared sheets and immediately before the page's own `index.css`**. Every moved rule keeps its selector, its declarations and their order, so nothing it fights changes its specificity.

**1. `src/styles/shared/chart-stage.css`**: the stage a chart room's sheet stands in, on screen. `.stage` (the flex box that centres the sheet in the reserves), `#sheet` (the fitted mount), `#map-viewport` with its focus ring and `.zoomable`, `#map`'s seat (position, inset, transform origin), and `.stage .warning` with its `[hidden]`. Linked by the seven pages whose stage holds the sheet ahead of the gesture box: the five that seat `src/layouts/ChartStage.astro` (Ribbon, Prospect, Print Room, Specimen Book, Portfolio), the Reading Room and the Explorer. It carries **no print rule**: each room keeps its own stand-down for paper.

**2. `src/styles/shared/document-room.css`**: a document room's sheet, everything the Glossary and the FAQ share, their print block included: the body's reading line height, the heads' face, the parchment `.sheet` and its corners, `sheet-settle`, the slip's `--slip-w`, `main`'s margin, the columns and `h2`, links and `code`, and the stand-down for paper. Linked by the Glossary and the FAQ.

**Why the cascade cannot move, and what the probe confirms.** Each moved block sat at the head of its room's sheet, after only the header comment (and, on the Explorer, its `:root` block). The new sheet is linked immediately before that sheet, after every other sheet the page links (`shell.css` still follows both). So against every rule on the page but the page's own, the moved rules keep their order; against the page's own, they move ahead of the rules that sat between them and the warning rule, all of which dress other elements or other properties: the plates (`#rb-plate`, `#pp-plate`, `#pr-preview`, `#pr-turned`, `#sb-plate`), `.sheet-inner`, `#map-viewport .rf-chart`, the hold's glyph. The print blocks stay where they are, after the moved screen rules, as before. That argument covers the states the probe does not stage (`.zoomed`, `stage-under`, the desk frame, `sheet-drag`, `has-atlas`): each wins or loses against the moved rules by the same specificity and the same order as today. Two exceptions, both read: the Explorer's `#map` keeps `container-type: size` in its own sheet while its seat moves (staged as `explorer-keep`, 0 differences), and the Explorer's `body.chart-room .stage` (0,2,1) gives way to the shared `.stage` (0,1,0), which lets its own print rule win and fits its chart to the paper, as ruling D1 asks.
