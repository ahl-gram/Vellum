## The design

**What each read asks, in order** (`thinTopFaults(t, alias)`):
1. No trail: one fault, and nothing else is read (unchanged).
2. The trail's top on screen and less than its own height from the page's top (unchanged, the original plan's call 4).
3. The on-screen cluster lines whose rows lie inside the trail's span stand on one row: their rows spread by no more than `ROW_SPREAD` = 1. New.
4. The chart of the atelier press is among those lines on the trail's line, not merely on screen. Strengthened.
5. Where `alias`: no alias line is one fault. New (main's presence clause).
6. Where `alias` and an alias line stands, three clauses, each new:
   - it stands directly under the trail: its top at or past the trail's foot, and the gap less than its own line's height;
   - the on-screen lines inside its span stand on one row;
   - "also reached from" is among them.
7. The thin top line's foot, `thinTopFoot(t, alias)`, stands at or under `TOP_CEILING` = 48. The foot is the lower of the trail's foot and, where `alias` and an alias line stands, the alias line's. New, by Alex's ruling (comment 6825295330).
8. Every on-screen cluster line stands inside the trail's span, or inside the alias line's span where `alias`. The alias span no longer stretches a band from the trail's top. Changed.

`thinFootFaults(t)`:
1. No title: one fault (unchanged).
2. The title's foot within `FOOT_WINDOW` of the page's foot (unchanged).
3. The chart folio's on-screen lines stand on one row. New.

**Why one row.** `GLYPH_LINES` gives one entry per line box, its `row` the box's centre rounded to the pixel. Boxes of one line, set in one face and size, centre under a pixel apart, so they read at most 1 apart. Every box on the trail's, the alias line's and the title's line read one row on the Prospect at 1280x800 and the Explorer at 1280x800 and 1024x768, and under the staged runner layout too. A second line stands a whole line lower: 13 px at the least (the alias line, 38 then 51), 15 for the trail (14 then 29), 18 for the title (772 then 790). So a spread over 1 is a second line. That closes what the skeptic named: every line box of a wrapped text node carries the same `t`, so no clause that reads `t` can see a wrap, while rows do.

**Why the alias line's own height.** "Under it" says the alias line follows the trail, with no line between. A line between would need at least a line's height, and the alias line is a smallest line of the cluster (13.00, as the chart of the atelier press's, against the trail's 16.00). Its own line is the tighter of the two anchors, 4.20 px spare on today's layout (gap 8.80). The ceiling now holds the two slips main caught (below); the gap clause is still the layout's own reading of "directly under", and alone faults an alias line set a line or more under a trail that stands higher than today, where the foot stays under 48 (r1 call 7).

**Why a room flag.** Whether a room has an alias line is the page's markup, not its layout: `BaseLayout.astro` draws `p.also` only where the trail has an `also` list. A read of the page cannot tell an alias line that ought to be there and is gone from a room that never had one. SW1's name already claims "the Prospect's second line kept under the trail", and main held it with `room.id === "Prospect"`, the form `thinLines` passes again. ES1 and EZ3 pass `false`, so an alias line appearing on the Explorer faults as a line outside the trail's line.

**Why a fixed ceiling as well, and why 48.** Alex ruled it (comment 6825295330). The reads from the layout cannot fault a thin top line that keeps its shape but stands a few pixels lower, which main's 48 did: S1, the alias line alone 4 px lower, and S2, the whole line 6 px lower (both measured below). The ceiling is main's own number, on the form main held it on the Prospect (`also[1] <= 48`). 48 was checked against every room the three checks read, on both machines as far as a run shows today, and is wrong for none:
- On the Mac the foot stands at 44.20 on the Prospect at 1280x800 (3.80 under) and 22.41 on the Explorer at 1280x800 and 1024x768 (25.59 under), `1076-r1-probe-ceiling.log`.
- On the runner, main's SW1 passed `a.head.also[1] <= 48` on CI run 43181045607 at d2f4cf31 (lane C, `PASS  SW1`), and this branch lays the head cluster out exactly as d2f4cf31 does, since it touches no file under `src/`. So the runner's Prospect foot stands at or under 48. The Explorer's is bounded on CI only loosely until commit R2's run prints it: main's EZ3 held its cluster rows' centres under 48 at 1280x800 (lane B), main's ES1 held its trail's foot at or under 60 at 1280x800 and 1024x768, and the branch's step 2 holds the trail's top under its own height.

### Alternatives rejected

- **The ceiling alone, with no reads from the layout.** Rejected: it passes (A), the alias line gone (foot 22.41, the trail's), every wrapped title, and on the Explorer a wrapped trail (foot 35.41) and the chart of the atelier press on a row of its own (foot 22.41). Alex's ruling keeps both.
- **A ceiling derived from the layout in place of 48.** Rejected: the ruling names main's 48, and it holds on every reading there is today (Why a fixed ceiling, above). A reading past it on commit R2's CI run is a stop condition, not a reason to move it here.
- **The ceiling on every on-screen cluster line's row, EZ3's form.** Rejected: the foot of the thin top line is the form main held the Prospect's alias line to (`also[1] <= 48`), and a row is a centre, which lets a line's lower half stand past the ceiling.
- **The trail's line height (16.00) as the alias line's gap bound.** Rejected: the alias line's own 13.00 is the tighter anchor, with 4.20 px still spare today.
- **Telling a wrap by two entries with the same `t`.** Rejected: a wrap between two text nodes (the alias line's link moved whole to the next row, the trail's crumbs, which never break inside) gives each node one entry. Only rows see it.
- **A one-line test from heights (a span's height against its first line's, or its computed `line-height`).** Rejected: it needs a new in-page read, where the rows are already read and see the same wrap.
- **Exact row equality (`ROW_SPREAD` = 0).** Rejected: the boxes of one line centre a fraction of a pixel apart (the chart of the atelier press's span centres 0.02 px under the trail's in the row-above staging: `[60.921875, 73.921875]` against `[59.40625, 75.40625]`, `1076-r1-probe-all.log`). Their rounded rows could straddle a half pixel on another layout and split one line into two.
- **Inferring "this room has an alias line" from the page.** Rejected: see "Why a room flag". It is what let (A) pass.

// The slide-away's thin lines read off the page (Issue #1076): the trail's own line, with the Prospect's alias line directly under it, as the thin top line, and the chart folio's title, on one row 2 px above the foot, as the thin bottom line.
import type { Payload } from "../../types.ts";
import { GLYPH_LINES } from "./glyphs.ts";

export type Span = readonly [number, number];
export type ThinLine = { readonly piece: string; readonly t: string; readonly row: number };
export type ThinLines = {
  readonly innerH: number;
  readonly trail: Span | null;
  readonly also: Span | null;
  readonly title: Span | null;
  readonly lines: readonly ThinLine[];
};

// 2026-11-12: folioTravel in src/site/shared/slide-away.ts ceils the travel to the ruled 2 px FOOT_CLEAR, landing the title's foot 1 to 2 px above the page's foot (1.30 on the Mac, handbook/plans/1000/1076/1076-plan.md), so a window 3 px deep holds it with a pixel spare each side (Issue #996 comment 6365710190).
export const FOOT_WINDOW = 3;

// <date>: GLYPH_LINES rounds each line box's centre to the pixel, and the boxes of one line, set in one face and size, centre under a pixel apart (every box on the trail's, the alias line's and the title's line read one row on the Prospect at 1280x800 and the Explorer at 1280x800 and 1024x768 on the Mac, handbook/plans/1000/1076/1076-r1-plan.md), so they read at most 1 apart, where a second line stands a whole line lower, 13 px at the least (the alias line's).
const ROW_SPREAD = 1;

// <date>: ruled, Alex, 2026-11-12, Issue #1076 comment 6825295330, main's 48 px ceiling on the thin top line's foot kept beside the read from the layout, under Issue #1048's ruling on a number that stays: the foot stands at <mac> on the Prospect at 1280x800 and <mac> on the Explorer at 1280x800 and 1024x768 on the Mac harness browser, and at <ci>, <ci> and <ci> on the CI runner (run <run>, lanes C and B), the nearest <margin> under it (the Prospect on <machine>).
const TOP_CEILING = 48;

export const THIN_LINES: Payload<ThinLines> = `(() => {
  const span = (e) => { if (!e) return null; const r = new Range(); r.selectNodeContents(e); const b = r.getBoundingClientRect(); return [b.top, b.bottom]; };
  return {
    innerH: innerHeight,
    trail: span(document.querySelector("header.chrome .trail")),
    also: span(document.querySelector("header.chrome p.also")),
    title: span(document.getElementById("folio-title")),
    lines: (${GLYPH_LINES}).map((g) => ({ piece: g.piece, t: g.t, row: g.row })),
  };
})()`;

const within = (row: number, s: Span): boolean => row >= s[0] && row <= s[1];
const spread = (lines: readonly ThinLine[]): number =>
  lines.length === 0 ? 0 : Math.max(...lines.map((l) => l.row)) - Math.min(...lines.map((l) => l.row));
const rowsOf = (lines: readonly ThinLine[]): string => lines.map((l) => l.row).join(", ");

export function thinTopFoot(t: ThinLines, alias: boolean): number {
  if (t.trail === null) return NaN;
  return alias && t.also !== null ? Math.max(t.trail[1], t.also[1]) : t.trail[1];
}

export function thinTopFaults(t: ThinLines, alias: boolean): string[] {
  const { trail, also, innerH } = t;
  if (trail === null) return ["no trail stands in the head cluster"];
  const faults: string[] = [];
  if (!(trail[0] >= 0 && trail[0] < trail[1] - trail[0]))
    faults.push(
      `the trail stands off its thin top line: its top ${trail[0].toFixed(2)} is not on screen within its own height ${(trail[1] - trail[0]).toFixed(2)} of the page's top`,
    );
  const on = t.lines.filter((l) => l.piece === "cluster" && l.row >= 0 && l.row < innerH);
  const onTrail = on.filter((l) => within(l.row, trail));
  if (spread(onTrail) > ROW_SPREAD) faults.push(`the trail's line stands on more than one row: ${rowsOf(onTrail)}`);
  if (!onTrail.some((l) => l.t.startsWith("the chart of the atelier")))
    faults.push("the chart of the atelier press is not on the trail's line");
  if (alias && also === null) faults.push("no alias line stands under the trail");
  if (alias && also !== null) {
    const onAlias = on.filter((l) => within(l.row, also));
    if (!(also[0] >= trail[1] && also[0] - trail[1] < also[1] - also[0]))
      faults.push(
        `the alias line does not stand directly under the trail: its top ${also[0].toFixed(2)}, the trail's foot ${trail[1].toFixed(2)}, its own line ${(also[1] - also[0]).toFixed(2)}`,
      );
    if (spread(onAlias) > ROW_SPREAD) faults.push(`the alias line stands on more than one row: ${rowsOf(onAlias)}`);
    if (!onAlias.some((l) => l.t.startsWith("also reached from"))) faults.push("the alias line's text is not on screen");
  }
  const foot = thinTopFoot(t, alias);
  if (!(foot <= TOP_CEILING))
    faults.push(`the thin top line's foot ${foot.toFixed(2)} stands past the ${TOP_CEILING} px ceiling`);
  const under = alias ? also : null;
  for (const l of on)
    if (!within(l.row, trail) && !(under !== null && within(l.row, under)))
      faults.push(
        `the head cluster line "${l.t}" stands at ${l.row}, outside the trail's line ${trail[0].toFixed(2)} to ${trail[1].toFixed(2)}${under === null ? "" : ` and the alias line's ${under[0].toFixed(2)} to ${under[1].toFixed(2)}`}`,
      );
  return faults;
}

export function thinFootFaults(t: ThinLines): string[] {
  if (t.title === null) return ["no chart folio title stands on the page"];
  const faults: string[] = [];
  const foot = t.title[1];
  if (!(foot >= t.innerH - FOOT_WINDOW && foot <= t.innerH))
    faults.push(`the chart folio title's foot ${foot.toFixed(2)} is not within ${FOOT_WINDOW} px above the page's foot ${t.innerH}`);
  const folio = t.lines.filter((l) => l.piece === "chart folio" && l.row >= 0 && l.row < t.innerH);
  if (spread(folio) > ROW_SPREAD) faults.push(`the chart folio stands on more than one row on screen: ${rowsOf(folio)}`);
  return faults;
}
