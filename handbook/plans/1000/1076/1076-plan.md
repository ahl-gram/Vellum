## The design

**One rule, applied per check.** A number the room's layout decides is read in the run; a witness that a named instance reaches its case asserts the relation the claim names and, where that case is a reading held against a line, carries one dated line giving both machines' readings and the margin; a ruled number stays a named literal with a line naming its ruling; a bound around a ruled number names its principle. Every check keeps its id, its step and its presses on today's layouts, so no step reads a new page, window or press and none is made longer.

**The thin lines are read off the lines themselves**, in one new module, `e2e/suites/stage/thin-lines.ts`, because three checks (SW1, ES1, EZ3) hold the same two places and today each fits its own pixel band (48, 60, 32). The top thin line is the trail's own line box, with on the Prospect its alias line under it, exactly as `handbook/specs/ui-design.md` words it; the bottom thin line is the chart folio's title, 2 px above the foot. Two clauses hold the top, because either alone passes a defect the probe staged (the readings are under Tests):
- the trail's top stands on screen and less than one of its own line heights from the page's top (a row mounted above the trail moves it to 59.41, past its 16.00);
- every head cluster line left on screen stands within the band from the trail's top to the foot of the trail, or of the alias line where the room has one, and the chart of the atelier press is among them (a row mounted under the trail stands at 49 on the Explorer and 71 on the Prospect, outside the band, where ES1's `trail[1] <= 60` and SW1's two `<= 48` clauses pass it).
The foot is the title's foot within `FOOT_WINDOW` of the page's foot, the window SW1 and ES1 already hold with `innerH - 3`; on EZ3 it replaces `h - 32`, which passes a folio stopped 5 px short of its travel (title foot 6.30 above the foot, its row 785, over `h - 32`'s 768), as does EZ3's clause that every folio line on screen is the title (only the title is on screen there).

**ES9 reads the folded fill off the fold it already makes**: after the held reads, `foldLanded` (`e2e/suites/slide-away/kit.ts`) waits for the folded sheet's rest, 53 ms after the reads on the Mac, and its fit gives the folded fill. Two witnesses then ask that the fold was made past both fills (`folded <= pressDepths(n)`) and that a depth lies between them (`folded > open`), and the second fold poses at their geometric mean.

**ES8 wheels until it is past the fill**, from the press before the fill, at most `NOTCHES` times. d3-zoom 3.0.0's `defaultWheelDelta` (`node_modules/d3-zoom/src/zoom.js:34`) scales by 2^(100 × 0.002) = 1.1487 per 100 px notch, and the product sets no `wheelDelta` (`git grep -n wheelDelta -- src/site` prints nothing), so from the press before the fill, at most the Glass's 1.4 short of it, ⌈ln 1.4 / ln 1.1487⌉ = 3 notches cross any fill.

### Alternatives rejected

- **Bounding the thin lines on both layouts** (keep 48, 60 and 32 with a dated line). Rejected: `handbook/specs/check-placement.md` allows a two-layout bound only "where no read of the layout gives a measured number", and the trail's and the title's line boxes give these. The same bullet names this case as measured: "a bound that places a line a ruling names only in words, such as 'on the thin top line', since the ruling set the line and not its pixels". (As history only: Issue #1048's first plan, since superseded by `handbook/plans/1000/1048/1048-r1-plan.md`, sorted the three the same way.)
- **One clause for the top line** (rows within the trail's box, or the trail's top alone). Rejected: each passes one of the two staged defects above.
- **ES9 folding once at rest, before the presses, to learn the folded fill.** Rejected: it adds a fold and an unfold, 746 to 773 ms on the Mac (`ES9 at rest` in `out/1076-dry-run/1076-probe-es9.log`), making ES9 longer, which owes a ledger line and a timing; the witnesses carry the same fact at no cost.
- **ES9 reading the folded fill off the last held read.** Rejected: the held reads still catch the sheet mid-slide on the Mac (2.2221, 1.9044, 1.8261 before 1.8209, `ES9 folded at the fill press with the line mounted`), and `handbook/specs/settle-doctrine.md` names `foldLanded` as the shape.
- **ES8 keeping two notches and asserting `k >= fill`.** Rejected: whether two notches cross is a reading the layout decides, which would owe a dated line; the bounded loop reaches the case by construction.
- **A shared "press to the fill" helper.** Rejected: the conversion is one expression per check, `press(ctx, fillPressOf(fillOf(home)))`, and a helper in `e2e/suites/stage/slide-kit.ts` would import `fillPressOf` from `e2e/suites/slide-away/kit.ts`, which re-exports from `slide-kit.ts`, an import cycle.
- **Importing the product's `HEAD_CLEAR`, `FOOT_CLEAR` or `fillPress`.** Rejected: a check that takes its number from the code it checks cannot disagree with it (Issue #1048 plan, call 7); the product's two constants are not exported either.
- **Splitting `e2e/suites/stage/stage.ts`** (409 lines, 411 after). Rejected: the edit adds two comment lines, and the house's measure, `max-lines` in `eslint.config.ts:66` with `skipBlankLines` and `skipComments`, counts 377 (`grep -c -v -E '^\s*(//.*)?$' e2e/suites/stage/stage.ts`); call 13.
