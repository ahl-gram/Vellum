## The design

### What it does

The Explorer's slide-away (Issue #996, PR #1028) is lifted into the Atelier Kit and laid on the seven other zoom rooms: the Print Room, the Portfolio, the Prospect, the Reading Room, the Wayfarer's Ribbon, the Seed of the Day and the Specimen Book (ruling 1, "All seven"). On every one of them the head cluster (`header.chrome`) and the chart folio (`.corner.bl`) do exactly what they do on the Explorer, by the same code: they follow the camera's depth `k` up and down to the nearest edge, finishing as the chart, magnified as the Glass magnifies it, covers the screen beside the room's slip; they leave a thin top line (the trail with the chart of the atelier press) and a thin bottom line (the chart folio's title); a mouse or pen resting on a thin line brings that piece back (`REST_MS` 150, `LINGER_MS` 400); keyboard focus inside the head and the chart of the atelier opened bring the head back; zooming out brings both home; under reduced motion they stay home short of the fill and stand at their thin lines from it; nothing is hidden and nothing leaves the tab order; every seat reads the pieces' home boxes (`rectOf` in `src/site/shared/room-seats.ts`, already shared, so the 1024 button row stays where it stands); a fold or a refit re-measures, so folding a room's slip part way through a zoom moves the pieces with the chart. What differs is only what Alex ruled:

- **Where the follow starts (ruling 5, every room and the Explorer).** The follow starts at the depth where the Glass's press BEFORE the press that fills lands, so nothing moves until the press that fills, which carries the pieces the whole way. Press depths compound as the controller compounds them (`nextGlideTarget` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`: 1.4, then 1.4 * 1.4 = 1.9599999999999997, then 2.7439999999999993). Where the first press already fills (the Explorer at 1024x474, fill 1.3359) the start is the fill and the pieces leave on the frame the chart fills, as today. Where the fill lies past the first press and at or under the second (every room at 1280x800 with its slip open; the Explorer at 1280x800 and 1024x768) the start is the first press's 1.4, as today. Where it lies past the second press the start is the second press's depth, so the second press moves nothing and the third carries them: the Print Room (2.2069), the Specimen (2.0393) and the Seed of the Day at 1024x768; the Print Room (2.1115), the Reading Room (2.0956), the Seed of the Day (2.0145) and the Specimen (1.9776) at 1280x800 with the slip folded; and the Explorer at 1280x640 with the Broadside folded (2.5823) (`out/997/measure/fill.tsv`). On the Explorer the change reaches 49 of the 98 window shapes the step 6 spike measured (seven widths from 1024 to 1920 by seven heights from 600 to 1080, the Broadside open and folded; `out/1034/notes.md` Part B, `out/1034/measure/partB.tsv`), among them, with the Broadside open, every 1024-wide window measured but 1024x700 and 1024x768 (1024x800 fills at 2.01, 1024x1080 at 2.69), 1920x800 and every shorter 1920-wide window, 1440x640 and 1440x600, and with the Broadside folded or the Chart Table open 1280x700, 1366x768 and 1440x800; it does not reach 1280x800, 1440x900 or 1920x1080, open or folded. On those windows the shipped second press parks the head and folio part way (55% at 1280x640 folded, 72% at 1920x800 and 74% at 1440x640 open: `out/1034/measure/partA-rest.json`, `partB-spot.json`); under the rule it moves nothing and the third press carries them. The Explorer takes the rule on every window (Alex, 2026-10-23, the rulings comment's question 1, "Same rule everywhere").
- **The Reading Room (ruling 2, "Slide down like the Explorer").** Nothing room-specific in code. Its folio stands on the instrument strip (`.corner.bl.folio` in `src/styles/reading-room/index.css`), and the Explorer's `folioTravel` carries its title down to 2 px above the window's foot, which lies under the strip (`.strip` at `z-index: 12` over the corners' 10), about 150 px of travel; the fill is judged against the window's foot as on the Explorer. ### How it is built

- **The lift** (`handbook/specs/ui-design.md`: "The kit is lifted at second use, never at first ... it moves to the shared sheet and a component in the same change, and the first consumer is repointed"). `src/site/explorer/slide-away.ts` and `src/site/explorer/slide-away-bind.ts` move to `src/site/shared/`, their content unchanged in the move commit save the head comments. `src/site/explorer/slide-away-page.ts` is replaced by `src/site/shared/slide-away-page.ts`, which takes the room's elements and its two room-specific rules as parameters and imports nothing from `src/site/explorer/`. `src/styles/explorer/slide-away.css` moves to `src/styles/shared/slide-away.css`, served at `/shared/slide-away.css` and opted into through `extraCss` by all eight pages.
- **The seam.** No room but the Explorer hands its depth to a hook (`onApply` is passed only by `createGlass` in `src/site/explorer/glass.ts`). Six rooms' `createZoomController` calls gain `onApply: (s) => slide.follow(s.k)`. The Seed of the Day's controller may take no hook at all (`vellum/hunt-fixed-world` in `scripts/lint/import-bounds.ts`, Issue #161: "the Hunt's zoom controller is geometric only: it is built with an options literal written out, carrying no onSettle or onApply"), so its binder follows the camera by watching the camera's target: every camera apply writes `targetEl.style.transform` (`apply` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`), and a `MutationObserver` on that `style` hands the controller's own depth (`getState().k`) to the follow in a microtask, before that frame paints. The Explorer keeps `onView(k)` from `setCardZoom`.
- **The step.** `src/site/shared/glass-keys.ts` exports its `ZOOM_STEP` (1.4) and the rooms pass it; the Explorer passes `ZOOM_STEP` from `src/site/explorer/glass.ts`. The shared binder takes the step as a parameter and imports neither file, so the Explorer never bundles `glass-keys.ts` and no room bundles `glass.ts` (`glass-keys.ts`'s own comment: the Hunt must never bundle the LOD schedule).
- **What the page binder reads for itself**: reduced motion (`matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)")`, read live) and the ease (`--paper-settle` on the root through `parseFloat`, 340 when it does not parse), exactly as `prefersReduce` and `tokenMs` in `src/site/explorer/app.ts` read them for the Explorer today, so no room carries a copy.
- **The frame's reserves.** The binder watches the room's frame (`bindRoom`'s `frame`: `.stage` on six rooms and the Explorer, `#map` on the Seed of the Day) and its sheet for `style` mutations, as the Explorer watched `stageEl` and `sheetEl`, but it re-measures on a frame mutation only when one of the frame's three reserves (`--reserve-top`, `--reserve-right`, `--reserve-bottom`) differs from the last measure's: on the Seed of the Day the frame is also the camera's target, whose `style.transform` changes on every camera frame.
- **The slip's width** is read as the fit reads it, `slipWidth(rectOf(slip))` from `src/site/shared/room-seats.ts`, so a slip standing inside a hidden panel (the Reading Room's, between reads) still reserves its width; for a visible slip it is the number the Explorer reads today.

### Alternatives rejected

- **The rule left in `src/site/explorer/` and imported by the rooms**: the kit rule above, and the rooms would bundle Explorer code.
- **Today's fill read against all four edges** (the round's measured "first covers the room" column): on 1024x768 with the hunt open it never fills, and on 1280x800 it moves the press from 2 to 4, against ruling 4's "nothing changes on wider screens"; the fidelity rule holds the build to what Alex saw (`out/997/stills/special/today-1024x768-window-centre-press4.png`, `today-1280x800-window-centre-press2.png`).
- **Today's resting box read from `offsetLeft` and `offsetTop`**: they round to whole pixels; the camera's own state un-transforms the exact box.
- **A room's depth read off its transform** (the round's `depthNow`) on the six rooms whose controller may take a hook: `onApply` is the controller's own seam. On the Seed of the Day the transform's write is watched, but the depth is the controller's `getState().k`, never parsed from the style.
- **`onApply` on the Seed of the Day, with `vellum/hunt-fixed-world` narrowed to `onSettle`**: the rule is the guard of a ruled boundary (Issue #161), its test pins both hooks refused (`test/repo/import-bounds.test.ts`, "the Hunt stays a fixed world"), and watching the target needs no hook.
- **An arming rule for the Ribbon alone**: one rule for the seven rooms is simpler, and only the Ribbon and the Specimen make a zoom for the reader.
- **The Explorer's arming narrowed to keys on the chart as well, or any key arming in every room**: Alex ruled the split (the rulings comment's question 2).
- **The Explorer keeping its first-press start while the rooms take ruling 5**: Alex ruled the same rule everywhere (the rulings comment's question 1).
- **"slid away" at a fixed depth** (the old `STEP ** 3`): the ruled still shows the fill press (1.96 at 1280x800), where 2.744 shows a deeper chart than Alex saw.
- **A hook to pose a room's camera at a chosen depth for the browser checks**: the rooms' own presses land exactly on the depths the ruling is about (the press before the fill and the fill press), and the curve between them is the shared code ES1 reads on the Explorer.
- **Two pull requests (the lift, then the rooms)**: the kit rule lifts at second use in the same change, so a lift without its second consumer is a lift at first use.

### `src/site/shared/slide-away.ts`

Moved by `git mv`. Head line, its only comment: `// The arithmetic of a chart room's slide-away (Issue #996, lifted for every zoom room in Issue #1034): how deep the chart fills the screen beside the room's slip, where the follow starts, how far the head cluster and the chart folio have followed the zoom, and where a pointer holds them.`

```ts
export function fillPress(fill: number, step: number): number;
export function followStart(fill: number, step: number): number;
export function arms(e: ArmingEvent, keyAnywhere: boolean): boolean;
export function fillAboutCentre(sheet: Edges, room: Edges, gesture: Edges): number;
export function restingBox(
  onScreen: Edges,
  origin: { readonly x: number; readonly y: number },

```

- `pressesTo(fill: number, step: number): { readonly before: number; readonly at: number }` (not exported): `let before = 1; let at = step;` and `while (at < fill && step > 1) { before = at; at *= step; }`, returning both. The depths compound by `*=` exactly as `nextGlideTarget` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts` compounds a press's target, so `at` is the Glass's own landing depth (1.4 * 1.4 is 1.9599999999999997 in both).
- `fillPress`: `fill` itself when `!Number.isFinite(fill)`, else `pressesTo(fill, step).at`: the depth of the first press at or past the fill.
- `followStart`: `fill` itself when `!Number.isFinite(fill)`; else, with `before` from `pressesTo`, `fill` when `before === 1` (the first press fills, so the pieces leave on the frame the chart fills) and `before` otherwise (the press before the press that fills). For a fill at or under the second press this equals the shipped `Math.min(firstPress, fill)`.
- `arms`: `(e.type === "keydown" && keyAnywhere) || e.onChart`.
- `fillAboutCentre`: with `cx` and `cy` the centre of `gesture`, and `need = (gap, reach) => (reach > 0 ? gap / reach : Infinity)`, `Math.max(1, need(cx - room.left, cx - sheet.left), need(cy - room.top, cy - sheet.top), need(room.bottom - cy, sheet.bottom - cy))`. `room.right` is not read.
- `restingBox`: each edge `o + (v - o - t) / cam.k`, with `o` and `t` the origin's and the camera's `x` for `left` and `right` and their `y` for `top` and `bottom`: the box with the camera's `translate(x, y) scale(k)` about the origin taken off (`zoomTransformToCss` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`; the Seed of the Day's `#map` carries `transform-origin: 0 0`).
- The file stays near 115 lines.
