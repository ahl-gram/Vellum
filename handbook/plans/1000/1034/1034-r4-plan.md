### How it is built

- **The lift** (`handbook/specs/ui-design.md`: "The kit is lifted at second use, never at first ... it moves to the shared sheet and a component in the same change, and the first consumer is repointed"). `src/site/explorer/slide-away.ts` and `src/site/explorer/slide-away-bind.ts` move to `src/site/shared/`, their content unchanged in the move commit save the head comments. `src/site/explorer/slide-away-page.ts` is replaced by `src/site/shared/slide-away-page.ts`, which takes the room's elements and its two room-specific rules as parameters and imports nothing from `src/site/explorer/`. `src/styles/explorer/slide-away.css` moves to `src/styles/shared/slide-away.css`, served at `/shared/slide-away.css` and opted into through `extraCss` by all eight pages.
- **The seam.** No room but the Explorer hands its depth to a hook (`onApply` is passed only by `createGlass` in `src/site/explorer/glass.ts`). Six rooms' `createZoomController` calls gain `onApply: (s) => slide.follow(s.k)`. The Seed of the Day's controller may take no hook at all (`vellum/hunt-fixed-world` in `scripts/lint/import-bounds.ts`, Issue #161: "the Hunt's zoom controller is geometric only: it is built with an options literal written out, carrying no onSettle or onApply"), so its binder follows the camera by watching the camera's target: every camera apply writes `targetEl.style.transform` (`apply` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`), and a `MutationObserver` on that `style` hands the controller's own depth (`getState().k`) to the follow in a microtask, before that frame paints. The Explorer keeps `onView(k)` from `setCardZoom`.
- **The step.** `src/site/shared/glass-keys.ts` exports its `ZOOM_STEP` (1.4) and the rooms pass it; the Explorer passes `ZOOM_STEP` from `src/site/explorer/glass.ts`. The shared binder takes the step as a parameter and imports neither file, so the Explorer never bundles `glass-keys.ts` and no room bundles `glass.ts` (`glass-keys.ts`'s own comment: the Hunt must never bundle the LOD schedule).
- **What the page binder reads for itself**: reduced motion (`matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)")`, read live) and the ease (`--paper-settle` on the root through `parseFloat`, 340 when it does not parse), exactly as `prefersReduce` and `tokenMs` in `src/site/explorer/app.ts` read them for the Explorer today, so no room carries a copy.
- **The measure policy** (Revision 1). The binder measures with both pieces home: on every follow whose depth stands at or under the last measure's start (`k <= from`), and on every layout change `watchLayout` sees (below). At or under the start `slideProgress` is 0, so the pieces stand home and the measure reads their home boxes; `slide.measured` with an unchanged measure writes nothing (`place` in `src/site/shared/slide-away-bind.ts` returns when the translate it would write is the one written). - **The kept start** (Revision 1, Alex's decisions 1 and 2). A measure taken while the camera's depth stands past the start and short of the fill keeps that start: `keptStart(fresh, kept, k, fill)` in `src/site/shared/slide-away.ts` returns `fresh` when `k <= kept || k >= fill` and `kept` otherwise, and `pageSlideAway` passes it the depth of its last follow and the fill the measure reads. So a fold, the Chart Table opened or a resize made while the camera stands there moves the fill and the travels and leaves the start where the zoom began: the pieces come part way back by the follow's own log-depth progress, as today's Explorer brings them (measured in "Decision 1, ruled"), and leave again as the zoom goes on. The kept start ends at the start or at the fill. At or under the start, the first follow there measures and takes the room's own start, where both pieces stand home, so the next zoom from home starts where ruling 5 puts it in the room as it then lies. At or past the fill, `pageSlideAway` measures on the first follow there while its start differs from the room's own (`from < own`), and takes the room's own, where both pieces stand away whatever the start, so a reader who zooms past the creep and comes back down meets the folded room's own rule (decision 2). `from <= fill` holds throughout, so "at or under the start, both pieces stand home" stays true and the measure policy above stays sound: the kept start is returned only while `kept < k < fill`. A fold that narrows the room needs no lowering of the start, since its own start lies under the kept one only when its fill does too, at or under the depth (`followStart` returns the press before the fill press, or the fill itself), and there `k >= fill` already takes the room's own. The first measure, and every measure at or under the start or at or past the fill, take the room's own start, unchanged from the frozen plan. The keep is keyed on the depth, not on the pieces standing away: unarmed (a link that opens zoomed, a zoom a room makes) or held, a fold past the start keeps the start the same way, which shows nothing while they stay home; the edge it leaves is a first measure taken on a room still laid out narrower than it settles, at a depth between the start and the fill, whose too-low start then holds until the depth reaches the start or the fill, so the pieces can leave early there, never late (call 35; unmeasured). Under reduced motion nothing changes: the pieces stand home short of the fill and away from it whatever the start.
- **The frame's reserves.** The binder watches the room's frame (`bindRoom`'s `frame`: `.stage` on six rooms and the Explorer, `#map` on the Seed of the Day) and its sheet for `style` mutations, as the Explorer watched `stageEl` and `sheetEl`, but it re-measures on a frame mutation only when one of the frame's three reserves (`--reserve-top`, `--reserve-right`, `--reserve-bottom`) differs from the last measure's: on the Seed of the Day the frame is also the camera's target, whose `style.transform` changes on every camera frame.
- **The slip's width** is read as the fit reads it, `slipWidth(rectOf(slip))` from `src/site/shared/room-seats.ts`, so a slip standing inside a hidden panel (the Reading Room's, between reads) still reserves its width; for a visible slip it is the number the Explorer reads today.
