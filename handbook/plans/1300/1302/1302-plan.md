**2. The guard: an outcome stands in the window.** The walk asks of every outcome read with no state of its own the new expectation `"seen"`, in every room (one shared `expectFor`):

- `fits(state, "seen")` passes any reach but `absent`, `hidden`, `no box` and `off screen`. Being covered is allowed, since an outcome is read, not pressed: the chart folio's lines read `covered by stage` while plainly visible (part Q).
- The walk's own `off screen` is judged at the read's press point, the box's centre, which misses a line half past the edge: at 1280 with motion on, Try it, k 1.7, the caption's box starts at y 775.3 with its centre inside the window and its `held` line ends at 811.3 (part K). So for a `"seen"` read the walk also reads the quoted words' own line boxes (`QUOTED`), and where they are not in the window the read's state becomes `off screen`:
  - a `text` read: the rendered occurrences of the quoted words inside the element, for a read that matches part of its text (`match` set), else all the element's text; in view when every line box of some rendered occurrence lies wholly inside the window (`inView`);
  - any other read: in view when the element's box meets the window with some area.

**3. What the guard reads for `explorer-hold` under the ruling, plainly.** The walk opens the step's bare address at 1280x800 with motion reduced, presses the anchor, rests and reads. At 1.6 that depth stands under the window's fill (1.6327), so the folio stays home and the caption's `held` line stands 22.39 px inside: the check passes. The walk never runs this step with motion on (only a step marked `only: "motion"` walks with motion on) and never at a window other than 1280x800, so it does not read the cases the ruling accepted as still broken: with motion on, a reader at 1280 has `held` 0.77 px below the edge (Try it) or 42.73 px (no note), and at 1440x900, 1536x864 and 1920x1080 a reader with motion on loses it, a Try it reader at 1366x768 keeping it 1.45 px inside. The issue's acceptance at 1280 by 800 therefore holds for this step with motion reduced and not with motion on, as Alex was told when he ruled; the PR body says so beside the readings. Against main's ladder (`SURVEYED`, 1.96) the same check reds: `#caption` off screen, 52.61 px past.

**4. The margin the ruled depth leaves.** Band 1 is drafted from 1.584 (computed), 1% under 1.6; the walk's fill at 1280x800 is 1.6327 on this Mac, 2% over it, and the same with the runner's layout staged. The fill is a layout reading: a runner laying the Explorer's sheet out about 2% larger than this Mac would put 1.6 at or past the fill, the folio would leave under reduced motion, and the check would red on CI. That is a design stop, not a rewrite (Stop conditions).

**Blind spots, each with its direction:**

- A word painted over by another piece reads in view (cover is not judged): errs toward a pass.
- The walk reads desk steps at 1280x800 with motion reduced only, so a piece that slides part way with motion on, or leaves at another window, is not read; for the hold step this is the accepted case above: errs toward a pass.
- The walk's bare arrival fits the chart larger than a Try it arrival does, so a piece can read away where a Try it reader with motion reduced keeps it: errs toward a red.
- An outcome held as `held` or `disabled` that stands off screen reads `held` or `disabled` first (`stateOf`'s order) and passes: errs toward a pass; no outcome carries either without `state` set today.
- Words the page writes with other spacing than the step quotes are not found as a part, so the read falls back to all the element's text: errs toward a red.

      if (q === null) return false;
      const within = (b: Box): boolean => b.x >= 0 && b.y >= 0 && b.x + b.w <= q.width && b.y + b.h <= q.height;

        b.w > 0 && b.h > 0 && b.x < q.width && b.y < q.height && b.x + b.w > 0 && b.y + b.h > 0;
