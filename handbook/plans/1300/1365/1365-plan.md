## The answer, in one paragraph

**They are not bugs in the page; they are one bug in the tests, four times over.** On GitHub's runner the browser draws a camera's glide in a handful of frames, 100 to 250 ms apart, where the Mac draws one every 16 to 34 ms. Each of the six checks waits for the glide with "two reads in a row that agree", and two reads taken inside one of those long gaps agree on a camera still in mid-flight. In 648 glides on the runner, every one landed on its exact target with the right `zoomed` flag, a median 395 to 462 ms after the press (300 ms is the glide as set); none stalled and none landed wrong. The waits handed over before the landing at the rates their poll spacing predicts; the checks as they stand, with nothing of the investigation in the page, red on the proof runner today (SB6 and SB13 4 of 4, SB15, ZG5b and ZG8b 1 of 4); and a wait that also asks for a drawn frame with no camera write between its two reads (the house's `landed` shape) handed over only after the landing in every glide, on the Mac and on the runner, staged stalls of 600 and 1500 ms included. Built as this plan writes it (below), the six passed in 9 of 9 jobs on the proof runner where they red at main, and every other suite the change reaches passed there and on the Mac. Issue #871's stale `zoomed` flag is real (SB15's out press leaves it on at scale 1 on the Mac every time) but it is not what any of the six reads, and it does not happen on the runner's layout at all.

## The measurement, as taken

### What was read, and where

A throwaway patch (never committed) puts a recorder in the page before each press and reads the camera itself, not a picture of it: `#map-viewport.__zoom` (d3's stored transform, k, x and y at full precision), the `.zoomed` class, whether d3 still has a glide scheduled (`#map-viewport.__transition`), the target's inline `style.transform`, and, on the Explorer, `window.__vellumCameraSettling()`. On every frame the page draws it logs the time and any change; it counts the camera's writes to its target with a `MutationObserver`; and timers read the camera at once, at 500 ms and at 2000 ms after the press's first input event. Each glide is driven by the check's own gesture and waited for by the check's own wait, unchanged, and the record keeps what that wait handed over and when. After 2600 ms, a rest poll waits until no glide is scheduled, the Explorer's settle is done, and a frame has drawn between two equal reads (cap 8 s); a glide that never gets there is recorded as not rested.

- **Natural**: each check's own body runs once as it does in the run, the recorder armed around it (SB6's from the start of the step "SB6 setup", where its wrong state is made).
- **Unstaged cycles**: eight more glides per check through its own gesture and wait (SB13, SB15 and RR40 record two glides a cycle: in and home or out).
- **Staged cycles**: two more with the runner's withheld frames staged: a script added before the bundle loads (`Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument`, so d3's timer binds the wrapped `requestAnimationFrame`) holds every frame callback for 600 ms from the first frame the camera moves. On the Mac this reproduces each red's payload shape on demand.
- **Candidate cycles** (run 2 and the Mac): the same gesture with the candidate wait (below), at stalls of 0, 600 and 1500 ms. 1500 is past d3-timer's 1 s `poke`, where d3 stops its own clock while no frame comes.

Each check's record rides one always-red check, `DIAG1365-<id>`, whose FAIL line points at a `D1365 <id> [...]` line on stdout; a row expects exactly those reds, so a row that reads IMPRECISE is one where the check itself also went red, naturally.

### What each outcome was taken to mean (written into `analyze.ts` before the runner's results were read)

- **Late (a test defect)**: the wait handed over while the glide was still scheduled, or at a camera other than the one it landed on, and the camera then landed on its exact target (k within 1e-9) with the flag right.
- **Stalled or wrong (a page defect)**: no rest within 8 s after the window, or a rest at a camera other than the target.
- **Issue #871 (a page defect)**: a rest at k 1 exactly with a float translation and `.zoomed` still on.
- **ok**: the wait handed over the camera it landed on.

The only measured runner rate for a glide read mid-flight was Issue #1260's (`handbook/plans/1200/1260/1260-r5-plan.md`): the head's camera wait reddened 2 of 13 samples, one to three frames over the glide. ## The design (Alex's Q1 and Q4: fix the waits, the shared one for every caller)

### The shared helper

`e2e/support/camera-landed.ts` (new, about 30 lines), the spike's `camera-rest.ts` as measured:

```ts
export type Framed<T> = { read: T; frames: number; writes: number };
export const framed = <T>(read: Payload<T>, on: string): Payload<Framed<NonNullable<T>>>;
export const STOP_CAMERA_WATCH: Payload<boolean>;
export const landedWith: <T>(same: (d: T, last: T) => boolean) => (d: Framed<T>, last: Framed<T>) => boolean;
```

- `framed(read, on)` evaluates `read` and hands it back with `window.__cameraWatch`'s counts. It installs the watch the first time, or when the element `on` names is not the one watched, or after a stop: a `requestAnimationFrame` loop counting frames and a `MutationObserver` on that element's `style` attribute counting records. `on` is the camera's target, the element the room's zoom controller writes (`targetEl`): `"#map"` in every room but the Reading Room, `"#map-viewport > .rf-chart"` there.
- `STOP_CAMERA_WATCH` stops the loop and disconnects the observer; every wait below evaluates it in a `finally` around its poll, so the found read and the timeout throw both stop it and no frame loop outlives its wait.
- `landedWith(same)` is true only when both reads are non-null, `same(d.read, last.read)`, `d.writes === last.writes` and `d.frames > last.frames`.

### The four waits (each keeps its name, its signature, its label and its departure)

1. **`toState`** (`e2e/suites/specimen/kit.ts`): reads `framed(COUNTED, "#map")` through the same `stable`, `departed` reading `d.read`, `same: landedWith(sameJson)` with `sameJson` a local `(a, b) => JSON.stringify(a) === JSON.stringify(b)` (settle.ts's own is not exported); stops the watch; returns `d.read`. Its return type is unchanged.
2. **`glided`** (`e2e/suites/specimen/gestures.ts`): exported, so its unit test can drive it; the same loop (60 reads 100 ms apart, the `i >= 12` fallback that takes a press that goes nowhere as a fault, the same throw), reading `framed(LENS, "#map")` and resting on `landedWith((a, b) => a.t === b.t)` in place of `d.t === last.t`; the departure stays `d.read.t !== from || i >= 12`. Its comment becomes the one line: `// The matrix's scale reads home, not the camera's zoomed flag: a glide that rests at 1 can leave a translation of -2.8e-14, which the controller's strict isHome refuses (measured 2026-10-13 and 2026-11-24 on the Specimen, Issue #871).`
3. **`lensRest`** (`e2e/suites/print-room/print.ts`): a fifth parameter `on = "#map"`; the same loop and modes, reading `framed(lens, on)`, `left ||= d.read.zoomed`, resting on `landedWith((a, b) => a.t === b.t)(d, last) && (want === "still" || left || i >= 6)`, returning `d.read`. **`homeCamera`** takes a third parameter `on = "#map"` and passes it to both of its `lensRest` calls. Because the write count sees the first frame of a glide that has not yet moved the matrix (d3's first tween tick writes), `"still"` no longer hands over before the home press's glide starts.
4. **`kLeft`** (`e2e/suites/zoom-gestures/touch.ts`): exported; `makeSettle(k)(framed(ZOOM_K, "#map"), (d, last) => d.read !== 1 && !!last && landedWith((a: number, b: number) => a === b)(d, last), label)`, stops the watch, returns `d.read`.

The Reading Room's target becomes one constant: `export const RR_ON = "#map-viewport > .rf-chart";` in `e2e/suites/reading-room/kit.ts`, which `RR_LENS` reads through (`document.querySelector(${JSON.stringify(RR_ON)})` in place of its literal). The calls that read `RR_LENS` pass it: `rr40Glass` (its `lensRest` and both `homeCamera` calls, `e2e/suites/reading-room/gestures.ts`), `pressGlass` (`e2e/suites/slide-away/kit.ts`: `lensRest(ctx, "leaned", label, lens, lens === RR_LENS ? RR_ON : "#map")`), and the `EZ` presses in `e2e/suites/stage/zoomed.ts` (`page === "/reading-room/" ? RR_ON : "#map"` beside its `lens`).

### Rejected

- **Six equal reads in a row** (`kStill`'s shape, Issue #1366's Restore step 2): it sizes a window against the runner's gaps, which this run read at up to 247 ms; six reads 50 ms apart span 250 ms and still fit inside one, and the doctrine names the frame witness as the shape for a camera.
- **Waiting on d3's own `__transition`**: exact, and it is what the measurement read, but it is d3's private record, and a check written on it breaks silently on a d3 upgrade; the frame witness reads only what the page does.
- **Waiting on `window.__vellumCameraSettling()`**: the Explorer alone exposes it, and it goes false mid-glide on a double tap once 250 ms pass with no frame (the controller arms its settle on each zoom event, and a double tap's glide sets no glide target), so it would hand ZG8b over exactly when the runner starves (read from `settlePending` and `listenZoom` in `src/site/shared/zoom-controller.ts`, not measured).
- **Fixing the page instead, or with it** (not taken, Q1), and **fixing `isHome` (Issue #871) as the fix for these six**: none of them reads the flag at a rest the runner reaches with a float translation; SB6's camera homes through `glideHome`, whose last frame is d3's exact target (`if (t === 1) t = b` in `node_modules/d3-zoom/src/zoom.js`), and the runner's SB15 out glide lands exact.
- **Giving RR40 its own wait and leaving `lensRest`** (not taken, Q4): `lensRest` is RR40's wait; the runner read the same hand-over in `homeCamera` (1 of 48), and every other caller has it.
