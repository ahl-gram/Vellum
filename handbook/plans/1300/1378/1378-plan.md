## The design

1. `ENGLISH_BLOCKLIST` moves, verbatim, from `src/society/names.ts` to a new module `src/society/names-blocklist.ts` (ruling Q6), and `src/society/names.ts` imports it. `baseRejected` and `createBaseMaker` are not edited: a base that stands alone is still refused when it is `ARTICLE` or a member of the list, and the numbered fallback still skips a member of the list (ruling Q5).
2. The 18 ruled words join the list, one per line, after `"bra"`, in alphabetical order.

Every namer a world runs (`createNamer` in `src/world/generate.ts`, the title and surveyor namers in `makeMapTitle`, `conjureBestiary` in `src/society/bestiary.ts`, `assignFormerNames` in `src/society/renames.ts`, `hamletName` in `src/society/hamlets.ts`, which the farmsteads reuse) goes through that base maker and takes the screen with no edit of its own. A glued slot ("The Skyva", a suffixed town "Skygrad") keeps the word, as it keeps every listed word today.

### Alternatives rejected

- **The words added inside `src/society/names.ts`.** The lint's `max-lines` would count 409 there.
- **A separate list refused only where a base stands alone, as `ARTICLE` is.** Ruled out by Q5; it gave identical names on all 2062 worlds measured.
- **The test reading `ENGLISH_BLOCKLIST` as its oracle.** That is the existing "a standalone-slot base is never a blocklisted word" test, which cannot prove a new word: deleting the word from the list deletes it from the oracle too.
- **The test's sample kept at seeds 1 to 120.** Rape, homo and puke first stand alone at seeds 170, 224 and 186, so three of the 18 entries would be unguarded.
- **A culture built only to spell each word.** It would pin the list's membership on a culture no world speaks; the real cultures' own sample reaches every ruled word at seeds 1 to 240.
- **Bare, Kale, Lime, Mole, Non, Pan, Tame; Won and Pale; the broader groups.** Ruled out (Q2, Q4, Q1).
