## What the measurements found (they change the issue's premise)

The issue says "today's worlds very likely do have it ... nothing here was measured". Measured at `9683c29e` (`out/1315-dry-run/readings.log`, `mutations.log`, `planned.log`, `rivers-sweep.log`, each written by the probe of the same name beside it, run as `node out/1315-dry-run/<name>.ts` from this planner's tree):

1. **The rivers test has checked nothing at all.** Seed 42's world (100 by 75, land 0.38) grows 0 tributaries among its 5 rivers, so `if (tribs.length === 0) return;` fires on every run. Its assertion could not fail either: `allPoints` is built from every river, the tributary itself included, so a tributary's own last point is always in it. On seed 13, which grows two tributaries, the test as it stands still passes with every junction moved off the network (M2 below).
2. **The region sea-caption test passes by the caption's absence, by design.** Seed 42's capital plate holds no genuine deep sea: of the lattice spots the caption tries (`deepSeaSpots` in `src/render/layers/feature-labels.ts`), 0 are genuine sea and 2838 are a parent-world lake. ## The design

Each skip becomes a witness, written as an assertion that runs on every path, so the test reds when its world stops carrying the case instead of passing with nothing checked:

- **Assert the precondition the test already chose**, where the skip was an early `return` on a count: towns `>= 2` (roads), harbors `>= 3` (writer), tributaries `>= 1` (rivers). The threshold is the test's own; the witness only stops it being silent.
- **Count the cases that reached the assertion and assert the floor `>= 1`**, where the skip filters a loop: the "smell of" notes (lore, twice) and the side-placed shields (heraldry). The floor is the principle "the named case occurs at least once", not a sample.
- **Where the absent element is the correct outcome** (the region sea caption), the witness is a control arm in the same test: render the plate with the gate stripped and assert the caption then falls on a parent-world lake. That is exactly the case #234 fixed, so the witness reds when the plate stops holding it.
- **Where the assertion could not fail even with the case present**, correct it to the claim the test's name states: a tributary's end lies on ANOTHER river; adjacent notes open on different TEMPLATES.
- **The corridor test is deleted** (ruling 1): its one assertion `shared >= 0` cannot fail, and the behaviour its name claims is held by the #309 shadow guard, which reds under `REUSE_DISCOUNT = 1` (`src/society/roads.ts:22`) and under the ride discount dropped (`src/society/roads.ts:192`), worst runs 34, 30 and 16 cells against its `best < 15`.
- **The class is kept out by a lint rule, Issue #1329** (ruling 2), built after this pull request and Issue #1317's merge. This pull request records the five other tests of the class in one `handbook/errata/guards.md` row, which Issue #1329 cites and whose first day fixes or excuses each.
- The rivers test moves to seed 13, the first seed in a sweep of 1 to 200 whose world grows two tributaries (78 of the 200 grow one or more; in all 78 every tributary ends on another river, `rivers-sweep.log`).

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *Floors measured from today's counts* (lore `>= 4`, heraldry `>= 2`): `floor(60 / 14) = 4` is a principle for the lore only through the fresh pick's memory, and under M5 seed 11 carries 3 "smell of" notes, so that floor reds on a picker defect the test does not claim; heraldry's 2 is a count of one world. `>= 1` holds the one claim.
- *Heraldry asserting every realm reached the check* (`checked === w.arms.length`, 4 of 4 today): the test itself names a realm with no placed label as legitimate ("seat fallback"), so it would red on a placement change the test allows; the witness is the named case, a side-placed shield.
- *The sea caption asserted absent on seed 42* (`center === null`): over-specified; a caption on genuine sea is as correct, and the claim is only "never on a lake".
- *The sea-caption witness recomputed from the region's data* (deep cells the gate marks lake): it restates `deepSeaSpots`'s lattice in the test; the ungated render reads the renderer's own choice and costs one render (46 to 61 ms measured).
- *The writer test comparing opening sentences*: catches M5 on this fixture but misses a slotted template repeated with a different fill; the template lookup below is exact and builds no regular expression from template text.
- *Deleting the writer test as covered by "harbor notes do not repeat the same sentence excessively"* (`test/society/lore.test.ts`): that one runs on a stub world; this is the only one on a generated world, and once it compares templates it reds under M5.
- *Keeping the rivers test on seed 42 with a larger grid or a lower sea level*: changes two inputs where the seed changes one; the house form keeps the one seed that bites (`test/terrain/heightfield-detail.test.ts`).
- *Keeping the corridor test with a counted assertion* (at least 2 cells inside two roads on seed 7: 14 today, 1 with no reuse, 0 with no ride): ruled against (ruling 1). No principle gives the 2: over seeds 1 to 60 the mutants reach 23 such cells and the real builder falls to 0 on five seeds, and seed 42 reads 17 with reuse and without (`corridor-sweep.log`), so the floor would hold for seed 7 alone.
- *The lint rule in this pull request, no rule, or a periodic sweep in its place*: ruled (ruling 2); the rule is Issue #1329, built after this pull request and Issue #1317's, since its first day touches four files on Issue #1317's list (`renames`, `roads-realm`, `astro-scaffold`, `voyage`).
