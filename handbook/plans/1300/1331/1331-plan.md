## The design

Seven folds and two new tests, in one pull request. Every moved body keeps its arithmetic; only its home, its export and its callers' spelling change.

1. **One window frame and one segment clip for the close-up's roads and rivers** (ruling D1). `src/world/region-parent.ts`, which already maps the world's grid onto a window (`nearestParentCells`, `cellsPerWorldCell`), gains `type WindowBox`, `type WindowFrame`, `windowFrame(parentW, parentH, window, gridW, gridH)` (the box of the window in the parent's cells, and the map from a parent point to the region grid) and `clipSegment(a, b, box)` (the Liang-Barsky loop of `src/world/river-runs.ts`, byte for byte). `src/world/river-runs.ts` drops its `Box`, `Frame` and `clipSegment` and builds its frame with `windowFrame`. `src/world/region-roads.ts` drops its `toRegion` and `clipSegment`; its `Frame` becomes `WindowFrame` plus its own four fields, and its calls read `f.toRegion(p)` and `clipSegment(a, b, f.box)`.
2. **The label geometry spins about one origin.** `src/render/geometry.ts` gains a private `spinAbout(degrees, originX, originY)` returning the rotation `rotatedRect` writes today; `rotatedSpanBoxes` and `rotatedRect` each take their `spin` from it.
3. **A realm name's box is written once.** `placeRealmLabel` in `src/render/layers/realm-label-placement.ts` builds the one `boxAt(x, y)` and hands it to `placeOnWorldSheet` and `placeOnCloseUp`; the close-up's six calls read `boxAt(p.x, p.y)`.
4. **One rounder to the tenth** (ruling D2). `r1` moves to `src/core/math.ts` beside `clamp` and `lerp`. `src/prospect/dress/context.ts` re-exports it (its many importers do not change), `src/prospect/dress/burin.ts` takes it from `./context.ts` and re-exports it (its importers do not change), `src/prospect/letter/letter.ts` imports it and drops its copy, and `src/render/layers/glyph-clearance.ts` imports it in place of `tenth`.
5. **The ribbon keeps its world keys through `keptKeys`, now in the address grammar** (ruling D3). `keptKeys` moves from `src/site/shared/table-address.ts` to `src/site/shared/address-grammar.ts`, which the ribbon, the Prospect and the Chart Table already import; the three import it from there. `src/site/ribbon/address.ts` drops `worldKeys` and calls `keptKeys(hash, JOURNEY_KEYS)` at its three sites. The ribbon page loads the same 18 files as today (Readings, R6).
6. **The Explorer arms a landed sheet in one place.** `src/site/explorer/app.ts` gains `armLanded(res, seed, quiet, deferArm)`, the `armOnLanding({...})` call both landings make; `landInPlace` and the turn's callback in `landDraw` call it.
7. **The partition census takes the chain's own grid lookup** (ruling D4). `gridForWindow` in `src/world/detail-chain.ts` is exported; `scripts/region-detail/partition.ts` imports it and drops its copy.
8. **Two new unit tests** of the new shared frame and clip (T1, T2), since no existing test sees the clip's edges (Tests).
9. **A row in the errata ledger** for two gaps the measurements found that this pull request does not cause (P15, P16): no unit test notices the world sheet's realm-name box taking another width, or the glyph clearance rounding a nudged mark another way.

### Alternatives rejected, so the build does not rediscover them

- **Folding `scripts/region-detail/feature-match.ts`'s `clip` and `frameOf` into the shared ones:** its head line keeps them apart on purpose, so the guards in `test/world/region-roads.test.ts`, `test/world/region-rivers.test.ts`, `test/world/river-route.test.ts`, `test/world/region-major-rivers.test.ts` and `test/world/seat-settlements.test.ts`, which measure with it, do not share the code they measure (Call 7).
- **`windowFrame` taking the `World`:** the file's two functions take the parent's sizes as numbers; taking the world would import its type for two numbers (Call 3).
- **Folding only the `rearm` arrow (a curried `rearmFor(res, seed, quiet)`):** it leaves the identical `armOnLanding` object around it written twice (Call 4).
- **Folding the whole landing sequence the two paths share:** the two differ (the in-place landing starts the arrival between the layout and the arm, and applies a pending camera after); a shared helper needs either ten parameters or a reordering no unit test reaches (Call 4).
- **Keeping `boxAt` over a point:** the world sheet's five calls, the path the committed charts draw through, would each change; `spacedTextBox` takes `(x, y)` (Call 2).
- **A shared regular expression literal written three times in the ribbon's address:** one constant, `JOURNEY_KEYS` (Call 6).
- **Splitting `src/site/explorer/app.ts`:** it reads 434 lines by `wc -l` after the fold and about 390 by the house's count, which skips blank lines and comments and is the 400-line bound Alex ratified for that file (Call 5).
- **The frame and clip in a new `src/world/region-frame.ts`, or in `src/world/river-runs.ts` for the roads to borrow:** options B and C of D1, not taken (ruling D1).
- **Leaving both copies of the frame and clip:** option D of D1, not taken (ruling D1); it would have left the clip-edge slip P3 unseen by every unit test.
- **Folding only the two rounders the issue names, or only the three in the Prospect into `src/prospect/dress/context.ts`:** options A and C of D2, not taken (ruling D2); the second would also have had the lettering import from the drawing code.
- **`keptKeys` left in `src/site/shared/table-address.ts`, the ribbon importing it from there:** option B of D3, not taken (ruling D3); measured, the ribbon page would load 9536 bytes more (R6).
- **Leaving the census script's own copy of the grid lookup:** option B of D4, not taken (ruling D4).
