## The design

### Part H: the head in its slid-away state (D3)

At rest on the Primer the wordmark, the motto and the nav stand above the window, leaving the trail and its "also reached from" line as a thin top line on a band shrunk to fit it. A mouse at rest on the thin line, keyboard focus inside the head, or the chart of the atelier opened brings the head back, by the slide-away's own holds, and the band's ground comes back with it while the page does not move. Under reduced motion the head stands slid at once. With scripts off nothing slides.

- The Primer binds its own slide over the shared `bindSlideAway` at full depth from the start (C11). `bindSlideAway` takes a null chart folio, a piece that places and holds nothing.
- The travel is `headTravel` of the nav's line foot read in the WINDOW, less the head's own `translate`, since the head is fixed on a scrolling page (adding `scrollY` as `measure()` in `src/site/shared/slide-away-page.ts` does for a chart room slid the spike's head 704 px off on a page landed by an address).
- `--band-h` is written on the root in rem as the band less the travel, so `--room-pad-top`, every `scroll-margin-top` and the index slip's line (`rootPx("--band-h")` in `src/layouts/IndexSlip.astro`, which parses the token as a number of rem) follow.
- The band's paint is parted from its height: `.band::before`'s clip and `.band::after`'s top read `var(--band-paint, var(--band-h))`; the binder writes `--band-paint`, the band's full height in rem, while the head's own `translate` is empty, and removes it while the head is slid, read by a `MutationObserver` on the head's `style`; on the Primer alone (`:root.head-away`) the band's clip and top take the slide's ease (C12).
- The pointer is read against the head alone through `spotOf`, in page coordinates, as the zoom rooms read it.

Measured (`out/1392-dry-run/1392-probe.log`, below): the travel is 105 px at 1280x800, the band falls from 169.6 px to 64.6 px, the nav's ink ends at -3.2 px and the thin line's ink spans 6.4 to 44.2 px; brought back, the nav's ink spans 87.8 to 101.8 px, the band's paint reaches 169.6 px past the thin line's 149.2 px, and the first step stays at 380.5 px.

### Part S: one step at a time in the open room (D1, D2, D4, D5, D6)

While the page script runs, the Primer walks: under the opening, the open room's head and its line, the lesson's head (its aside out of sight), the one step as it is dressed today, and under it a row: "‹ Back a step", the lesson and count in the pointing hand's words ("The Land, step 3 of 6") with a dot for each step of the lesson, and "Skip this step ›". Every other room, lesson and step is out of sight. The chart of lessons stands under the open room (D2). Back from a Try it, a "Done:" line with the step's red seal stands at the head of the room's body (D5). Skip moves on and remembers nothing (D4). A room's end shows its "Turn the page to" road. With scripts off, and on paper, the page is the whole guide as today (C8).

- **Landing (C6, C14)**: no address: the room `roomOnArrival` picks, on its first step still to seal on this device; once every such step is sealed, the end (C4 as corrected below). A room's address (the trail press, a room's row in the index, a press on the chart, a room's road): that room's first step still to seal on this device, or its first step where none is. A lesson's address: the lesson's first step. A step's address (the hand's "Back to The Primer"): that step while unsealed; sealed, the next step after it still to seal on this device, with the Done line for it, or the room's end where none is. Every landing by an address brings the page to its top (`window.scrollTo(0, 0)`); a landing with no address leaves the scroll as the browser left it.
- **In-page links**: a press on any link whose `href` starts `#` and names something inside a room is taken by the page: `preventDefault`, `window.history.pushState(null, "", href)`, the landing, the top. A back or forward in history (`popstate`, and `hashchange` as today) lands on the hash it reaches the same way.
- **The walk**: Skip shows the step after the one shown, Back the one before, in the room's order, sealed or not, touch and motion steps with their marks (C3); Skip at the room's last step shows the room's end; Back stands disabled at its first step. A walk takes the Done line away.
- **The chart's hand** stands at the step the page shows, and off the chart at a room's end and at the end (C7).
- **The end**: every step this device can take sealed, with no address: no room shows, and the closing stands where the step stood, above the chart (still `a6-*`; C4 as corrected below).
- **The opening's second sentence** is the walk's words while the page walks.
- **The chart node moves** in the DOM, not by CSS `order`, so the tab order follows the reading: before the closing while there is a step to show, after it at the end. The built markup keeps it at the head (`BP-P7` stands).
- **The fold press goes** (C15): with every room but the open one out of sight, the "open these steps" press on a folded room's head can never show, so its button, its word, its handler and its rules leave.
- **The dots are lifted** (the kit lifted at second use): their dress moves from `src/styles/shared/primer-hand.css` to `src/styles/shared/primer-marks.css` under `.dots`, which the hand's slip and the Primer's row both carry.
