# The ladders, every step

Every step the round drew, in the guide's own order, from the same data the mock pages render (`mock/ladder/`). Words in **bold** are a control's own words as the built page shows them at 089881ad; the step quotes them exactly, and the drift check holds the two together. Each step's Try it address is given on the live site, so any step can be tried today: the address carries everything the step needs, which is direction A's whole premise. "Waits on a review" marks a step whose control `handbook/specs/ui-design.md` lists as provisional until its post-use review; that mark is for the sitting and the builder, never for the reader.

## The Explorer

The Explorer draws a whole world from a number, and every control on it reshapes or redresses that world.

### i · A world from a number, *the seed box, the dice and Draw, at the top right*

1. Press **⚄**, the dice beside the seed box.
   *A whole new world is drawn, and its number fills the seed box.*
   [Try it](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42) · the check passes · done on the built site, no outcome written · play, no wrong answer

2. Type 42 in the seed box and press **Draw**.
   *The Isle of Rahai comes back, line for line. Every chart is reproducible from the number in its margin.*
   [Try it](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=7) · the check passes · its outcome holds

3. Type any number you like in the **seed** box and press Enter.
   *Every number is a world, and the same number is the same world for anyone who types it.*
   [Try it](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42) · the check passes · its outcome holds · play, no wrong answer

### ii · The Land, *these shape the world itself*

*Every list starts at seed's choice: choose it again to hand the row back to the seed.*

4. On the Broadside, open **type** and choose **archipelago**.
   *The isle breaks into a scatter of isles, and the chart is renamed The Rahai Archipelago.*
   [Try it](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42) · the check passes · its outcome holds · waits on a review: the Broadside's rows and every word on them (handbook/specs/ui-design.md, the Broadside's provisional list)

5. A new row has come: drag **islands** from **few** toward **many**, then let go.
   *The sea fills with isles, and the readout says about how many. A few may touch or sink, so the count is never exact.*
   [Try it](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&type=archipelago) · the check passes · its outcome holds · waits on a review: the Broadside's rows and every word on them (handbook/specs/ui-design.md, the Broadside's provisional list)

## When the steps are done

*Lean on these, for a quicker answer, any time.* You may take these steps again whenever you like, from the first: **Begin again** clears the marks of the steps you have done from this browser, and touches nothing else. For a quicker answer than a whole ladder, Questions & Answers says how the worlds are made, and the Glossary says what every word on a chart means.
