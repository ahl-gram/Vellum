How big a step is: the Land at three grains

The same lesson three ways. Fine is what every still draws: one action to a step, the control's words in the sentence. The middle grain folds a row's variations into one step. The coarse grain is the Portfolio plate's own grain (PLATE_STEPS in src/site/explorer/portfolio/how-plate.ts), a whole panel in one line. The middle and coarse wordings are this sheet's alone, written to show the grain, and were never checked against the page.

Fine, as drawn

6 steps, one action each

  1. 4 On the Broadside, open type and choose archipelago.
    The isle breaks into a scatter of isles, and the chart is renamed The Rahai Archipelago.
  2. 5 A new row has come: drag islands from few toward many, then let go.
    The sea fills with isles, and the readout says about how many. A few may touch or sink, so the count is never exact.
  3. 6 Now sizes has come too: choose of every size.
    Some isles swell and others stay small.
  4. 7 Open climate and choose polar.
    Pine woods and open tundra, and the snow line comes down the hills. The coast stays exactly where it was.
  5. 8 Type Iceland in the shape box, and choose it from the list.
    The land is drawn inside Iceland's outline, north up, nearly edge to edge.
  6. 9 With Iceland still in the box, choose archipelago from type.
    The islands row steps into the held box at the foot of the Land, which says why: the outline holds the count; clear the place to free the islands.

Middle

3 steps, one row each

  1. 4 Choose archipelago from type, drag islands toward many, then choose of every size.
    The isle breaks into a sea of isles, each a size of its own.
  2. 5 Choose polar from climate.
    Pine woods and tundra, and the snow line comes down; the coast stays put.
  3. 6 Type Iceland in the shape box and choose it; then choose archipelago again.
    The land takes Iceland's outline, and the islands wait in the held box, which says why.

Coarse

1 step, the panel whole

  1. ii Shape the land: the type, the climate and the shape each reshape the world, and a row the world cannot use waits in the held box.
    A whole lesson in a line, as the Portfolio's plate tells the Chart Table.
