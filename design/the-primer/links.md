# Every step to try, on the live site

Ruling 13 asked for a longer list than five to seven. These are all 81 steps' Try it links on the live site (it deployed `089881ad`, the round's own commit), grouped by ladder in the guide's order, each saying what to do and what to look for, with a sitting note where a ruling is at stake. The links after the ladders show what two rulings do together. A step's link carries everything the step needs, so any one can be tried alone.

## The Explorer

### i · A world from a number

1. [Press ⚄, the dice beside the seed box.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: A whole new world is drawn, and its number fills the seed box.  
   Sitting note: The first thing a newcomer does (ruling 16). Does a whole world in a second feel like the moment that makes a child want the next step?

2. [Type 42 in the seed box and press Draw.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=7)
   Look for: The Isle of Rahai comes back, line for line. Every chart is reproducible from the number in its margin.

3. [Type any number you like in the seed box and press Enter.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: Every number is a world, and the same number is the same world for anyone who types it.

### ii · The Land

4. [On the Broadside, open type and choose archipelago.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: The isle breaks into a scatter of isles, and the chart is renamed The Rahai Archipelago.  
   Sitting note: The first step of the Land. The chart renames itself: would a child notice?

5. [A new row has come: drag islands from few toward many, then let go.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&type=archipelago)
   Look for: The sea fills with isles, and the readout says about how many. A few may touch or sink, so the count is never exact.  
   Sitting note: This row is on screen only because the link made the world an archipelago: the step needs nothing done before it.

6. [Now sizes has come too: choose of every size.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&type=archipelago&islands=20)
   Look for: Some isles swell and others stay small.  
   Sitting note: A row that comes only after a count. Is "has come too" enough to say where to look?

7. [Open climate and choose polar.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: Pine woods and open tundra, and the snow line comes down the hills. The coast stays exactly where it was.

8. [Type Iceland in the shape box, and choose it from the list.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: The land is drawn inside Iceland's outline, north up, nearly edge to edge.

9. [With Iceland still in the box, choose archipelago from type.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&outline=ne-1159320917)
   Look for: The islands row steps into the held box at the foot of the Land, which says why: the outline holds the count; clear the place to free the islands.  
   Sitting note: The step that teaches why a row waits. Read the held box's note: is it plain enough for a child?

### iii · The Strand

10. [Drag sea level slowly toward more sea.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: The sea rises as you drag, and the readout says how much of the sheet is land.  
   Sitting note: The one slider that moves the chart while you drag.

11. [Drag the coast from calm toward lobed, then let go.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: Nothing moves until you let go; then a new coast is drawn. Each step of the coast is a different world.  
   Sitting note: Nothing moves until you let go. Do the words prepare you for the pause?

12. [On a counted archipelago the coast row runs round to ragged: drag it toward ragged, and let go.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&type=archipelago&islands=20)
   Look for: Every island's shore frays.

13. [With Iceland's shape on the chart, drag looseness from exact toward loose.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&outline=ne-1159320917)
   Look for: The land slips its outline toward the seed's own island, and the coast row comes free.  
   Sitting note: Its words are provisional (ruling 14 teaches it now); the drift check reds if the review renames it.

### v · The Surveyor's Glass

20. [Press Draw nearer, the + by the chart.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: The chart comes closer.

21. [Drag the chart to look about it. Or click it, and use the arrow keys.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: The sheet slides under your hand. On the keys, + and − come nearer and stand off, and 0 shows the whole sheet.

22. [Keep pressing Draw nearer until the chart is drafted anew.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: A finer survey of that ground is drawn over the chart, with one corner turned up at its top right.  
   Sitting note: "Keep pressing until": the step names no press count, since the count moves with the window.

23. [Press Hold the sheet, the anchor.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&cx=0.5000&cy=0.5000&k=1.9600)
   Look for: The Glass drafts no finer survey and takes nothing away while it is lit, so the sheet you have stays put; the caption ends held.  
   Sitting note: Provisional: the hold's glyph and name may change at its review.

24. [Press The loupe, or hold the L key with the chart in hand.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&cx=0.5000&cy=0.5000&k=1.9600)
   Look for: Move the pointer over the chart: a round lens magnifies whatever lies under it.  
   Sitting note: Provisional, like the hold. Move the pointer over the chart once it is lit.

25. [Press Stand off to step back, or The whole sheet to see it all again.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&cx=0.5000&cy=0.5000&k=1.9600)
   Look for: The chart draws back to the whole sheet.

26. [On a tablet, press The chart in hand, the pointing hand, before you pinch.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42)
   Look for: Until it is lit, a pinch zooms the page; once it is lit, your fingers move the chart.  
   Sitting note: A tablet step (ruling 15): on a desktop its press is hidden, and the Primer shows the step with its mark.

   Look for: The Portfolio opens with every sheet you laid, ready to take home.

37. [Your commission: a polar archipelago of about forty islands, of every size, in the nautical style with its arms. Lay one survey of it on the table.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/#seed=42&style=nautical&type=archipelago&band=polar&arms=1&islands=40&isleSize=varied)

   Sitting note: A commission. Do it, then copy the address: with the pointing hand's key after the question mark, the copied link would carry the hand too (see the rulings below).

## The Reading Room

### i · Watch a world live

1. [Press Play.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/reading-room/#seed=42)
   Look for: The survey ship sails first, day by day; then the years turn, and towns rise and fall. With motion turned off on your computer, Play goes straight to the last year.  
   Sitting note: With motion turned off on your computer, Play goes straight to the last year: the step says so.

2. [Press 2× or 4× to hurry it.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/reading-room/#seed=42)
   Look for: The voyage and the years run twice or four times as fast. With motion turned off there is no pace to choose, since Play is a still frame.  
   Sitting note: Shown only with motion on; under reduced motion there is no pace to choose.

3. [Drag The ages to any year.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/reading-room/#seed=42)
   Look for: The chart shows the world as it stood that year, and the line under it tells what happened then.

4. [Read The Journal at the right.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/reading-room/#seed=42)
   Look for: Each line is a day of the survey, or a year of the ages.

5. [On a road leg's row in the Journal, press Take this road once the voyage has reached it.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/reading-room/#seed=42)
   Look for: It asks Leave the voyage? first; then the Wayfarer's Ribbon unrolls that road.  
   Sitting note: Provisional: the journal's road press is awaiting its review.

6. [Type a seed and press Read, or press ⚄.](https://www.vellumworlds.com/reading-room/#seed=42)
   Look for: Another world sits down to be read.

## What two rulings do together

- [The Print Room at the 1024 floor](https://www.vellumworlds.com/print-room/#seed=42): Open it in a window exactly 1024 wide and look at the style list beside Pull a proof. With a ninth item on the nav the corner narrows from 358 px to 304 px and the list cuts "antique": the build owes its widening (ruling 2).
- [The Prospect's trail line](https://www.vellumworlds.com/prospect/#seed=42&i=0): Under the trail: "also reached from The Reading Room". The Primer's own trail will carry "also reached from Questions & Answers" once it is both a nav item and a child of Q & A, and the trail press must find a seat that clears this line.
- [The pointing hand's key, today](https://www.vellumworlds.com/explorer/?step=explorer-islands#seed=42&type=archipelago): Today the key does nothing. Move the islands slider: the key stays in the bar while the room rewrites its address. Then press Take to The Print Room: the key does not follow (measured in all nine rooms). Copy the bar: the key goes with the link.
- [Home's How It Works card](https://www.vellumworlds.com/): Press How It Works at the title cartouche. The road to the Primer would sit at the foot of this card, under "Draw your own: read the README".
- [The FAQ's nearest step, out of date](https://www.vellumworlds.com/faq/): "I found a map I love": its answer says type, climate and land are not saved, and the address saves them all (a sibling defect, filed separately).
