The Journal

the survey sails first, and the history follows

Being a true & faithful chart of these waters, as surveyed by Reknin the Wayfarer in the year 876 of the Cedar Age

  1. day 1Year 843. We set out from Gru, seat of this survey, its walls raised in the year 435. The glass stood fair, and the tide served at dawn.
  2. day 6Year 843. We made sail for Tridzva, a town standing since 469. A fair wind and a following sea carried us in by the forenoon watch.
  3. day 12Year 843. We rode on to Bud, a town standing since 510. A cold rain met us on the last mile, and we were glad of its hearths.
  4. day 19Year 843. We made sail for Svi, a town standing since 522. A stiff breeze served us, and we made the roads before the light failed.
  5. day 29Year 843. We rode on to Tridkran, a town standing since 581. The hedges were white with may, and the air sweet the whole way down.
  6. day 40Year 843. We made sail for Likzvyl, a town standing since 567. We stood in past the shoals on a slack tide, the pilot wary of the reef.
  7. day 44Year 843. We made sail for Gru again, whence we set out, and handed the last of our sail in water we knew by heart.
  8. Here follow the annals of these waters
  9. 435Gru grew from a lone anchorage to a seat of some note.
  10. 438The standard of The Mak Balka was carried to the borders it holds yet.
  11. 456Settlers raised the first walls of Vlukkror.
  12. 458Zodkre was founded in a year the chronicles set down with care.
  13. 480The Gygid Voivodeship rose to claim the country between the waters.
