Laukuwelua

chief port of the Chiefdom of Rekekoa · founded An. 451

Laukuwelua, the capital. Once called Haitani. Envoys wait weeks in its antechambers for a single audience. Petitioners throng its steps on court days. Its founding is set down in the year 451.
