# Question 2: what stands for a lesson on the chart, and how does it grow?

The Primer has 18 lessons across 9 rooms: the Explorer has 8, the Print Room 3, and the other seven rooms 1 each. Both answers are drawn at the head of the page, at the same seals. Start with `../sheets/sheet-q2-what-stands-for-a-lesson.png`, where the first round's stand-in comes first.

## A (Recommended): a numbered waypoint for each lesson

Each lesson is a small round seal with its numeral, i, ii, iii, as the index slip numbers them. A room with several lessons rings them in an arc under its place, read left to right; a room with one lesson wears it on its own place. A done lesson is a red seal with a cream shine (the seal you ruled in the seats round), and a begun one has a red rim.

- What it gives: one press for each lesson, going straight to it. The numerals match the slip and the page's lesson heads.
- How it grows: the arc widens to keep the waypoints apart, so nothing ever overlaps. Measured on lessons added by copying a real one (none of these rooms has them; `../measure/growth.md`): the Explorer can take 16 lessons and still stand 13 px clear of the Reading Room at 1024, and the Portfolio 6 lessons and still stand 9 px clear of the Writing Room. As a room grows, a neighbour's name steps aside to the right of its place. No name ever lost its place.
- What you give up: the eight Explorer waypoints are small, 8.5 px numerals at 1024. It also needs question 4 answered.
- What it costs Sub 11: its unit check, "every lesson a waypoint", as the issue already names it.

## B: a ring of steps round each room (my own idea, flagged)

Each room wears a ring with one short mark for each step, its lessons parted by a gap, starting at the top and running clockwise. A step done is red. The tablet step stays a dotted mark that never fills.

- What it gives: the calmest picture, exact to the step, and it needs no answer to question 4. Measured on added steps: the Explorer can take 100 steps and still stand 18 px clear, and the Portfolio 30 steps and 15 px clear.
- What you give up: no numerals and no press for each lesson. Pressing a ring opens its room. Which mark is which lesson cannot be told from the chart.
- What it costs Sub 11: its unit check becomes "every step a mark" instead of the issue's "every lesson a waypoint".

Stills: `stills/q2-b-a-ring-of-steps-round-each-room-1280.png`, `stills/q2-b-a-ring-of-steps-round-each-room-1280-2x.png`, `stills/q2-b-a-ring-of-steps-round-each-room-1024.png`, `stills/q2-b-a-ring-of-steps-round-each-room-1024-2x.png`.

## Why A

It is the issue's own picture, it is numbered as the slip and the page number lessons, and each waypoint is a road to its lesson. It grows well past anything the ladders hold today.

## A new room

A room the site adds must already have a place on the chart of the atelier, which `test/site/atelier-chart.test.ts` checks. Its place is chosen by hand when the room is built, and its lessons then stand round that place by the same rule. No new room was drawn here, since any place this round picked would be invented.
