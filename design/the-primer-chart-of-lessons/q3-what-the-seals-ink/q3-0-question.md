# Question 3: what do your seals ink on the chart?

The issue asked for "the road from home inked as far as the reader's seals reach". The site's own roads, the ones the chart of the atelier draws, run from home to the Explorer, the Atlas and the Primer, and from the Explorer out to its rooms. The Seed of the Day and the Gallery are isles that no road reaches. Start with `../sheets/sheet-q3-what-the-seals-ink.png`.

## A (Recommended): the roads you have taken

Besides the lessons, the road from home to every room where you have sealed a step is inked red, along the site's own roads. The Seed of the Day and the Gallery have no road, so their seals ink on their own.

- What it gives: the road lights up as you go, which is the delight the issue described, and nothing is invented. In the stills the road runs from home to the Explorer and on to the Prospect.
- What you give up: the two isles never get a road, and the key line under the chart has to say what red means twice.
- What it costs Sub 11: a short walk over the chart of the atelier's roads to find each room's road from home (`pathFromHome` in the round's `mock/chart.ts`), and one more kind of line in its sheet. The chart of the atelier itself does not change.

## B: the lessons alone

Only the waypoints ink. The roads stay pale, as on the chart of the atelier.

- What it gives: the simplest chart, with one meaning for red.
- What you give up: the road from home the issue asked for.
- What it costs Sub 11: nothing beyond the waypoints.

Stills: `stills/q3-b-the-lessons-alone-1280.png`, `stills/q3-b-the-lessons-alone-1280-2x.png`, `stills/q3-b-the-lessons-alone-1024.png`, `stills/q3-b-the-lessons-alone-1024-2x.png`.

## C: a route of the chart's own, through the rooms in the page's order

A dotted route runs from home through every room in the order the Primer lists them, out by sea to The Seed of the Day and the Gallery. Each stretch inks when the room it reaches has a seal.

- What you give up: it is a tangle. It runs two and a half times the chart's width, crosses itself once (`../measure/route.md`), its stretch from the Ribbon back to the Portfolio runs through the Explorer's lessons, and its dots cross the names of the Writing Room, the Print Room and the Reading Room (the 2x still shows both; neither is measured), and since steps can be taken in any order, its inked stretches come in pieces.
- What it gives: every room, the isles included, on one road.

Stills: `stills/q3-c-the-primers-own-route-1280.png`, `stills/q3-c-the-primers-own-route-1280-2x.png`, `stills/q3-c-the-primers-own-route-1024.png`, `stills/q3-c-the-primers-own-route-1024-2x.png`.

## Not drawn: real roads to the two isles

If you want The Seed of the Day and the Gallery to have roads, that means changing the chart of the atelier itself, which every room shows in its head. That is outside this round and against Sub 11's "untouched" line, so it would be an issue of its own. Say so and it is filed.

## Why A

It is the issue's road, drawn only where the site really has one, and it is the most delightful of the three.
