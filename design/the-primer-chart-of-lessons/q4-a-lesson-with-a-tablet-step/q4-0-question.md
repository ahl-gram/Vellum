# Question 4: is a lesson done if one of its steps cannot be done on this device?

Two lessons hold a step only some devices can take: the Explorer's Glass (lesson v, seven steps, one of them "on a tablet") and the Reading Room's Watch a world live (six steps, one of them "with motion on"). You ruled in M4 that such a step is never sealed where it cannot apply, and keeps its mark. So on a desktop the Glass can never have all seven steps sealed. This question only arises if question 2 is answered with A (a waypoint for each lesson), or if the chart has a card (question 1, C or D). With B's ring of steps, the tablet step is simply a dotted mark that never fills.

The stills show the whole page at 1280 and 1024 and the Explorer's waypoints close up at twice the size, with every Glass step sealed except the tablet one.

## A (Recommended): done when every step this device can take is sealed

On a desktop the Glass reads done, a full red seal. On a tablet it waits for its tablet step too.

- What it gives: the same rule the Primer already uses to choose the room a returning reader opens on (it skips a step that cannot apply there), so the chart and the page agree.
- What you give up: "done" means "done here". The card, where there is one, still says "one on a tablet" under the Glass.
- What it costs Sub 11: nothing new; the page already has the rule (`applies` in `src/site/shared/primer/hand-steps.ts`).

## B: done only when every step is sealed

On a desktop the Glass stays begun, a red rim, for good.

- What it gives: "done" means every step, everywhere.
- What you give up: a desktop reader can never complete the Glass, and the chart disagrees with where the page sends a returning reader.
- What it costs Sub 11: a second rule beside the page's, used by the chart alone.
