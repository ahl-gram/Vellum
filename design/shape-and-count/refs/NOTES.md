# What the references taught

Issue #849's references are four Natural Earth vector files, downloaded 2026-10-19 from the public mirror into `natural-earth/` here. `refs.json` gives, for each, its source page, its original URL, its bytes, its git blob and SHA-1 checksums, its feature count and the feature this round read. They are vector data, so the file gives bytes and vertex counts where a scan would give a pixel size.

Licence, counted from `refs.json`: all 4 files carry "public domain", which comes from the terms page (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/, read 2026-10-19): "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public domain." The mirror repository's own licence field, as GitHub reports it, is "Other" (NOASSERTION). It is recorded beside the terms page and does not override it. Sub 4 (Issue #853) builds its own committed extracts later, so nothing here carries over. The archive keeps these links and never the files.

- **Texas, 1:110m states and provinces:** one ring of 79 vertices, the closing vertex included. That file holds the United States alone (51 features), which confirms the plan skeptic's reading quoted in Sub 4. Projected and fitted, it covers 19% of the sheet in the round's stand-in inner box. Its panhandle, the Rio Grande bend and the Gulf coast all survive at 320 by 240.
- **Japan, 1:110m countries:** three rings, of 37, 16 and 12 vertices. They are Honshu and Kyushu as one ring (this scale closes the strait), Hokkaido, and Shikoku. Okinawa and the small islands are absent. Projected and fitted north up, it covers 6% of the sheet; turned to the sheet's long axis (49.4 degrees clockwise, so its principal axis lies east to west), 7%. The cut-out reads as Japan at once. No soft direction does (likeness 0.30 at best, `measurements/shape-extras-sweep.md`).
- **The 1:50m files** were read and not drawn. There Japan is 34 rings, the largest of 385 vertices, and Texas is 6 rings: 415 vertices plus five barrier islands. A finer Japan adds islands. How many of them would survive as islands of 20 cells or more, in a cut-out or a soft likeness, was not measured: UNVERIFIABLE from this round.
- **The projection** is Sub 4's: longitude times the cosine of the place's middle latitude (0.8556 for Texas at 31.17 degrees, 0.7849 for Japan at 38.29 degrees), then fitted north up and centred. The shapes therefore sit on the sheet as they sit on a globe, not stretched east to west.
